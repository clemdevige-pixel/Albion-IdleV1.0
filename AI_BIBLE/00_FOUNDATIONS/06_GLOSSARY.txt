================================================================================
PURPOSE
================================================================================

This glossary defines every important gameplay and technical term used inside
the AI Bible.

Every document must use the terminology defined here.

A concept should only have one official name.

Avoid synonyms whenever possible.

Consistent terminology improves communication between documentation,
development and AI-assisted coding.

================================================================================
GENERAL RULES
================================================================================

• One concept = One official term.
• Always use the official terminology.
• Avoid abbreviations unless explicitly defined.
• New terminology must be added here before being used elsewhere.
• Gameplay terms take precedence over implementation names.

================================================================================
CORE GAMEPLAY TERMS
================================================================================

Combat

The primary gameplay activity where the Hero fights enemies to obtain rewards.

Hero

The playable character controlled by the player.

Enemy

Any hostile entity that can participate in combat.

Encounter

A single combat instance against one or more enemies.

Loot

Any reward generated after defeating an enemy.

Progression

Permanent improvement of the player's overall power.

Gameplay Loop

A repeatable sequence of actions that continuously drives progression.

Core Gameplay Loop

The primary loop of:

Combat

↓

Loot

↓

Equipment

↓

Stronger Hero

↓

Harder Combat

================================================================================
CHARACTER TERMS
================================================================================

Character

The complete representation of the Hero including statistics, equipment,
masteries and progression.

Equipment

Items worn by the Hero.

Equipment Slot

A location where one equipment item can be equipped.

Equipment Tier

Overall power level of equipment.

Equipment Quality

Quality modifier applied to an item of a given tier.

Mastery

Permanent specialization increasing efficiency within a gameplay domain.

Experience

Progression resource used to increase mastery levels.

================================================================================
COMBAT TERMS
================================================================================

Attack

A combat action dealing damage.

Damage

Reduction applied to an enemy's Health.

Health

Amount of damage an entity can receive before being defeated.

Defense

Statistics reducing incoming damage.

Critical Hit

Attack dealing increased damage.

Attack Speed

Frequency at which attacks are performed.

Combat Efficiency

Overall effectiveness of the Hero during combat.

================================================================================
ITEM TERMS
================================================================================

Item

Any object stored inside the inventory.

Stack

Multiple identical items occupying a single inventory slot.

Consumable

Item consumed after use.

Material

Resource used during crafting or refining.

Monster Material

Material obtained exclusively from defeated enemies.

Resource

Generic crafting ingredient.

================================================================================
RESOURCE TERMS
================================================================================

Raw Resource

Unprocessed resource gathered by the Hero or Workers.

Refined Resource

Processed material created through refining.

Production Resource

Any resource used by production systems.

Rare Resource

Resource with limited acquisition methods.

================================================================================
PRODUCTION TERMS
================================================================================

Crafting

Creating equipment from refined materials.

Recipe

Definition describing required ingredients and resulting item.

Refining

Conversion of raw materials into refined materials.

Production

Generic term covering crafting and refining.

Production Chain

Complete sequence transforming raw resources into finished equipment.

================================================================================
GATHERING TERMS
================================================================================

Gathering

Active collection of raw resources by the Hero.

Gathering Node

World object containing gatherable resources.

Gathering Tool

Equipment improving gathering efficiency.

Gathering Efficiency

Measurement of gathering performance.

================================================================================
WORKER TERMS
================================================================================

Worker

NPC permanently assigned to one gathering profession.

Profession

Permanent specialization of a Worker.

Assignment

Current task performed by a Worker.

Productivity

Amount of resources generated over time.

Automation

Passive production generated by Workers.

================================================================================
PLAYER ISLAND TERMS
================================================================================

Player Island

Permanent production hub owned by the player.

Building

Constructible structure placed on the island.

Production Building

Building dedicated to crafting or refining.

Storage

Location used to store resources and items.

Island Progression

Permanent improvements made to the Player Island.

================================================================================
ECONOMY TERMS
================================================================================

Silver

Primary in-game currency.

Purchase

Buying items or resources.

Sale

Selling items or resources.

Market

Future system allowing item trading.

Resource Sink

Mechanic permanently removing resources from the economy.

================================================================================
PROGRESSION TERMS
================================================================================

Progression Axis

Major category of permanent player progression.

Character Power

Overall combat strength.

Production Power

Efficiency of crafting and refining.

Economic Power

Ability to generate and manage Silver.

Island Power

Overall production capability of the Player Island.

================================================================================
SYSTEM TERMS
================================================================================

Gameplay System

Independent module responsible for one gameplay domain.

Dependency

Relationship where one system requires another.

Authority Document

Highest-level document governing a gameplay subject.

Single Source of Truth

The unique document responsible for defining a specific gameplay rule.

================================================================================
DEVELOPMENT TERMS
================================================================================

Prototype

Minimal implementation validating gameplay.

Vertical Slice

Small but representative version of the final game.

Milestone

Major development objective.

Placeholder

Temporary implementation intended for replacement.

Production Ready

Feature considered complete and maintainable.
