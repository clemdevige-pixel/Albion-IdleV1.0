## ALBION IDLE — INVENTORY SYSTEM

**Document type:** System Specification
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** LOCKED
**Authority level:** Core Gameplay System
**Parent authority:** `000_PROJECT_BIBLE.txt`
**Dependencies:** `10_CHARACTER_SYSTEM.txt`, `11_STAT_SYSTEM.txt`, `06_GLOSSARY.txt`

---

# 1. PURPOSE

The Inventory System manages all items owned by the player during gameplay.

It is responsible for:

* storing items;
* validating inventory capacity;
* managing item stacks;
* managing the active bag;
* transferring overflow items to the Bank.

It does not define item properties, crafting recipes, loot generation or equipment bonuses.

---

# 2. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The Inventory System must be:

* deterministic;
* persistent;
* category-driven;
* independent from Combat;
* independent from Crafting.

The inventory is the player's active storage.

The Bank is the player's long-term storage.

---

# 3. INVENTORY STRUCTURE

The inventory contains:

* inventory slots;
* one dedicated Bag Slot;
* item stacks.

The Bag Slot is **not** an equipment slot.

It belongs exclusively to the Inventory System.

---

# 4. INVENTORY CAPACITY

Inventory capacity is limited by the number of available slots.

Capacity is extensible.

The initial capacity is defined by balancing data.

The current capacity equals:

```text
Base Inventory Capacity
+ Active Bag Bonus
```

No other source modifies inventory capacity during the Vertical Slice.

---

# 5. ACTIVE BAG

Only one bag may be active at a time.

The active bag occupies the dedicated Bag Slot.

A bag provides only one gameplay effect:

* increasing inventory capacity.

Bags may be:

* crafted;
* obtained as loot.

Bag statistics and progression are defined by the Item System.

---

# 6. ITEM STACKS

Identical stackable items are grouped into stacks.

Each stack has a maximum size.

Maximum stack size is determined by the item's category.

The Inventory System reads this limit from the Item System.

---

# 7. STACK RULES

When receiving stackable items:

1. fill existing compatible stacks;
2. create new stacks if slots are available;
3. transfer remaining items to the Bank if inventory is full.

Stacks must never exceed their category limit.

---

# 8. NON-STACKABLE ITEMS

Items marked as non-stackable occupy one inventory slot each.

Their stacking limit is effectively one.

---

# 9. ITEM MOVEMENT

The Inventory System supports:

* adding items;
* removing items;
* splitting stacks;
* merging stacks;
* moving items between slots;
* moving items between Inventory and Bank.

The Item System defines whether an item is stackable.

---

# 10. INVENTORY OVERFLOW

When no inventory slot is available:

* newly obtained items are **not** discarded;
* they are automatically transferred to the Bank.

This rule applies to every acquisition source, including:

* loot;
* gathering;
* crafting;
* offline rewards;
* quest rewards;
* boss rewards.

Gameplay systems do not need custom overflow logic.

---

# 11. BANK

The Bank is a persistent secondary storage.

Its responsibilities are:

* receiving overflow items;
* storing player items long-term.

The Bank has its own capacity.

Bank capacity is extensible.

The detailed Bank implementation belongs to its dedicated specification.

---

# 12. PERSISTENCE

The Inventory System stores:

* slot contents;
* stack quantities;
* active bag;
* inventory capacity reference.

Derived values must be rebuilt when loading a save if required.

---

# 13. VALIDATION RULES

The following conditions must always remain true:

* only one active bag exists;
* every item occupies a valid location;
* stack quantities never exceed their category limit;
* inventory capacity is never exceeded;
* overflow items are transferred to the Bank;
* no item is silently destroyed because the inventory is full.

---

# 14. EVENTS

Recommended events:

* inventory_item_added;
* inventory_item_removed;
* inventory_stack_updated;
* inventory_capacity_changed;
* active_bag_changed;
* inventory_overflow_to_bank.

---

# 15. MINIMUM TESTS

Required tests:

* add a stackable item;
* merge compatible stacks;
* split a stack;
* refuse stack overflow;
* equip a bag;
* increase inventory capacity;
* replace the active bag;
* fill the inventory completely;
* transfer overflow items to the Bank;
* verify no item is lost during overflow;
* save and reload the inventory correctly.

---

# 16. OUT OF SCOPE

This document intentionally excludes:

* item definitions;
* item categories;
* item quality;
* item rarity;
* equipment rules;
* crafting;
* loot generation;
* bank upgrade costs;
* bank interface.

These topics are defined in their respective specifications.
