## ALBION IDLE — CRAFTING SYSTEM

**Document type:** System Specification
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** LOCKED
**Authority level:** Core Gameplay System
**Parent authority:** `000_PROJECT_BIBLE.txt`
**Dependencies:** `10_CHARACTER_SYSTEM.txt`, `12_INVENTORY_SYSTEM.txt`, `13_ITEM_SYSTEM.txt`, `14_LOOT_SYSTEM.txt`

# 1. PURPOSE

The Crafting System manages the transformation of player-owned materials into equipment and other craftable items.

It is responsible for:

* validating crafting recipes;
* consuming required materials;
* creating crafted items;
* managing specialized crafting professions;
* awarding profession progression;
* integrating boss-exclusive materials into recipes.

Refining is mandatory before equipment crafting but is defined in a dedicated system specification.

---

# 2. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The Crafting System must be:

* deterministic;
* data-driven;
* transactional;
* compatible with offline progression;
* independent from the user interface;
* extensible without modifying existing recipes.

A valid crafting operation must either:

* complete fully;
* fail without consuming any material.

Partial crafting transactions are forbidden.

---

# 3. CRAFTING LOOP

The intended crafting loop is:

```text
Raw Resources
↓
Refining
↓
Refined Materials
↓
Crafting Recipe
↓
Crafted Item
```

Raw resources cannot be used directly to craft equipment unless a future recipe explicitly permits it.

For the Vertical Slice, equipment recipes require refined materials.

---

# 4. CRAFTABLE ITEMS

The Crafting System may create:

* weapons;
* armor;
* accessories;
* bags;
* boss-exclusive equipment.

Currencies cannot be crafted.

Raw resources cannot be created through crafting.

Refined materials are created by the Refining System rather than the Crafting System.

---

# 5. CRAFTING PROFESSIONS

Crafting uses specialized professions.

All crafting professions are accessible to the same player.

There are:

* no exclusive profession choices;
* no permanent profession locks;
* no limit on the number of professions the player may progress.

Each profession progresses independently.

---

# 6. PROFESSION STRUCTURE

Each profession must contain at least:

```text
CraftingProfession
- profession_id
- display_name
- profession_mastery_id
- supported_recipe_categories
```

Recommended profession examples include:

```text
Weapon Smithing
Armor Smithing
Leatherworking
Clothworking
Accessory Crafting
Bag Crafting
```

The definitive profession list belongs to balancing and content data.

Profession identifiers must remain stable across save versions.

---

# 7. PROFESSION ACCESS

All professions are available to the player.

Availability does not necessarily mean every recipe is immediately usable.

Recipe access may depend on:

* required profession mastery;
* required item tier;
* recipe unlock conditions;
* owned materials;
* boss material requirements.

No profession selection permanently prevents access to another profession.

---

# 8. PROFESSION MASTERY

Each crafting profession has its own mastery progression.

Crafting an item awards mastery progress to the profession associated with the recipe.

Profession mastery may be used to:

* unlock higher-tier recipes;
* unlock new item families;
* improve crafting efficiency in future versions;
* unlock advanced recipes.

For the Vertical Slice, mastery effects must remain deterministic and fixed.

Exact mastery thresholds belong to balancing data.

---

# 9. CRAFTING RECIPES

Every craftable item requires a recipe.

Each recipe must contain at least:

```text
CraftingRecipe
- recipe_id
- profession_id
- output_item_id
- output_quantity
- output_tier
- output_quality
- output_enchantment
- required_materials
- required_profession_mastery
- crafting_duration
```

Each required material entry contains:

```text
MaterialRequirement
- item_id
- tier
- quantity
```

Optional boss material requirements use the same structure.

---

# 10. RECIPE IDENTIFIERS

Each recipe requires a unique and stable identifier.

Recommended format:

```text
CRAFT_<OUTPUT_ITEM>_<TIER>_<ENCHANTMENT>
```

Example:

```text
CRAFT_SWORD_T4_0
CRAFT_SWORD_T5_1
CRAFT_BOSS_AXE_T6_0
```

Quality should not necessarily be embedded into the recipe identifier when quality is selected or calculated separately.

The exact identifier convention must remain consistent across all recipe data.

---

# 11. RECIPE OUTPUT

A recipe creates a valid Item System object.

Equipment output is defined by:

```text
Base Item ID
Tier
Quality
Enchantment
Quantity
```

The resulting equipment variant must pass Item System validation.

The Crafting System does not calculate final combat statistics.

Item Power and equipment stats are resolved by the Item System.

---

# 12. TIER RULES

Craftable equipment tiers are:

```text
T3
T4
T5
T6
T7
T8
```

A recipe must explicitly define its output tier.

Required material tiers are defined by recipe data.

The Crafting System must not infer material tiers from the output tier unless a future rule explicitly defines that behavior.

---

# 13. QUALITY RULES

Crafted equipment supports the following qualities:

```text
Normal
Good
Outstanding
Excellent
Masterpiece
```

Canonical identifiers:

```text
NORMAL
GOOD
OUTSTANDING
EXCELLENT
MASTERPIECE
```

The source of crafted quality must be explicitly defined by recipe or balancing data.

The system architecture must support either:

* a fixed recipe quality;
* a selected quality;
* a future quality roll.

For the Vertical Slice, crafting quality must remain deterministic unless another locked specification defines a random roll.

---

# 14. ENCHANTMENT RULES

Crafted equipment supports:

```text
0
1
2
3
4
```

Display forms:

```text
No enchantment
.1
.2
.3
.4
```

The recipe defines the output enchantment.

Enchanting an already-crafted item is outside this document unless added by a dedicated upgrade system.

---

# 15. BOSS-EXCLUSIVE CRAFTING

Boss-exclusive materials may be required by dedicated recipes.

Boss-exclusive equipment may be obtained through:

* direct boss drop;
* crafting with boss-exclusive materials.

Boss-exclusive recipes must reference materials produced by the Loot System.

Boss-exclusive materials cannot be silently replaced by generic materials.

Boss-exclusive equipment must remain unavailable through generic crafting recipes.

---

# 16. MATERIAL VALIDATION

Before crafting, the system must validate:

* the recipe exists;
* the profession exists;
* the recipe is unlocked;
* the required profession mastery is met;
* every required item exists;
* every required tier matches;
* every required quantity is available;
* the output item definition is valid;
* sufficient storage exists or Bank overflow is available.

Validation must complete before any material is consumed.

---

# 17. MATERIAL CONSUMPTION

For the Vertical Slice, every successful craft consumes 100% of the listed materials.

There is:

* no resource return;
* no city bonus;
* no station efficiency bonus;
* no crafting refund;
* no random material preservation.

Material consumption is exact and deterministic.

---

# 18. FUTURE RESOURCE RETURN SUPPORT

A future Resource Return Rate may refund a portion of consumed materials.

This feature is not active during the Vertical Slice.

The Crafting System must remain compatible with a future calculation such as:

```text
Consumed Materials
- Returned Materials
= Net Materials Consumed
```

Recipes must always define gross material requirements.

Future resource returns must not modify recipe definitions.

---

# 19. CRAFTING TRANSACTION

Recommended transaction order:

```text
Craft Request
↓
Validate Recipe
↓
Validate Profession
↓
Validate Mastery
↓
Validate Materials
↓
Reserve Materials
↓
Resolve Craft Duration
↓
Consume Materials
↓
Create Output Item
↓
Store Output
↓
Award Profession Progress
↓
Complete Transaction
```

If any required step fails before completion:

* no materials are consumed;
* no item is created;
* no mastery progress is awarded.

---

# 20. CRAFTING DURATION

Recipes may define a crafting duration.

Crafting duration is external balancing data.

The system must support:

* immediate crafting;
* timed crafting;
* queued crafting;
* offline completion.

The exact queue behavior belongs to a future production or station specification if not already defined elsewhere.

---

# 21. BATCH CRAFTING

The system must support crafting multiple identical items in one request.

For a batch quantity:

```text
Total Required Material =
Recipe Material Quantity
× Batch Quantity
```

The full batch must be validated before execution.

The system must not partially complete a batch unless a future specification explicitly allows automatic maximum crafting.

---

# 22. INVENTORY AND BANK INTEGRATION

Crafting materials may be consumed from storage locations permitted by the final implementation.

At minimum, the architecture must support access to:

* Inventory;
* Bank.

The exact consumption priority must be defined consistently.

Recommended default priority:

```text
Inventory first
↓
Bank second
```

Crafted items are added to Inventory.

If Inventory is full, crafted items are transferred automatically to the Bank according to the Inventory System.

Crafted items must never be destroyed because Inventory is full.

---

# 23. ITEM STACK INTEGRATION

Crafted equipment may stack when it shares:

```text
Same Base Item ID
Same Tier
Same Quality
Same Enchantment
```

Crafted resources or other stackable outputs follow their Item System stack rules.

The Crafting System does not define stack limits.

---

# 24. OFFLINE CRAFTING

The architecture must support crafting completion while the player is offline.

Offline crafting must use the same:

* recipe requirements;
* durations;
* outputs;
* material costs;
* mastery rewards;
* storage rules.

Offline crafting must not apply a special production multiplier unless defined by balancing data.

---

# 25. DATA-DRIVEN REQUIREMENTS

The following must be externalized:

* profession definitions;
* recipe definitions;
* material requirements;
* output quantities;
* recipe tiers;
* recipe qualities;
* recipe enchantments;
* mastery requirements;
* crafting durations;
* profession mastery rewards;
* boss-exclusive recipe requirements.

Gameplay code must not hard-code individual recipes.

---

# 26. PERSISTENCE

The system must persist:

```text
Profession Mastery Progress
Unlocked Recipes
Active Crafting Jobs
Queued Crafting Jobs
Craft Start Time
Craft Completion Time
Batch Quantity
Reserved Material References
```

Completed outputs do not need to remain in Crafting System save data once transferred to Inventory or Bank.

Recipe display information must be loaded from definitions rather than duplicated in save data.

---

# 27. VALIDATION RULES

The following conditions must always remain true:

* every recipe references a valid profession;
* every output references a valid item definition;
* every required material references a valid item definition;
* material quantities are positive integers;
* output quantities are positive integers;
* tiers are valid;
* qualities are valid;
* enchantments are valid;
* profession mastery requirements are non-negative;
* crafting never consumes partial materials on failure;
* crafting never creates output without consuming required materials;
* mastery is only awarded after successful completion;
* boss-exclusive recipes require their defined exclusive materials;
* resource return is disabled during the Vertical Slice.

---

# 28. RECOMMENDED EVENTS

```text
craft_requested
craft_validation_failed
craft_started
craft_completed
craft_cancelled
craft_materials_consumed
craft_output_created
craft_output_sent_to_bank
crafting_mastery_awarded
crafting_mastery_level_changed
recipe_unlocked
```

Events must use stable identifiers rather than localized display names.

---

# 29. MINIMUM TESTS

Required tests:

* craft a valid equipment item;
* reject crafting with insufficient materials;
* reject crafting with incorrect material tiers;
* reject crafting with insufficient profession mastery;
* verify no material is consumed after failed validation;
* consume exactly 100% of required materials;
* craft each supported equipment tier;
* craft each supported quality;
* craft each supported enchantment;
* craft multiple identical items as a batch;
* reject a partially affordable batch;
* award profession mastery after success;
* prevent mastery rewards after failure;
* craft boss-exclusive equipment with valid materials;
* reject boss-exclusive crafting without exclusive materials;
* transfer crafted output to Bank when Inventory is full;
* complete a timed craft;
* complete a craft after offline progression;
* save and reload active crafting jobs;
* verify Resource Return Rate is inactive.

---
