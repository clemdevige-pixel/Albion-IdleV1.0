PURPOSE
================================================================================

This document defines the complete Worker System.

Workers are permanent non-player production units assigned to the Player Island.

Their purpose is to generate passive resources, reduce repetitive gathering and
support long-term production without replacing active gameplay.

Workers are not combat units.

Workers do not accompany the Hero.

Workers exist exclusively to support the production economy of the Player
Island.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

Workers follow six fundamental principles.

1.
Workers provide passive support.

They reduce repetitive gathering but never replace active play.

2.
The Hero is always the best gatherer.

An actively gathering Hero must always outperform an equivalent Worker.

3.
Workers are permanent investments.

Recruiting, assigning and improving Workers should contribute to long-term
account progression.

4.
Worker professions are permanent.

A Worker cannot change profession after recruitment.

5.
Workers do not have levels.

Worker progression is represented exclusively through profession-specific
Mastery and external productivity bonuses.

6.
Workers must remain predictable.

Their production should be understandable, calculable and consistent.

================================================================================
WORKER DEFINITION
================================================================================

A Worker is a permanent production unit owned by the player.

Each Worker contains:

• Unique Worker Identifier
• Display Name
• Permanent Profession
• Mastery Value
• Current Assignment
• Assignment State
• Productivity Modifiers
• Production Progress
• Assigned Building
• Ownership State

A Worker does not contain:

• Character Level
• Combat Statistics
• Equipment
• Inventory
• Health
• Combat Abilities
• Temporary Profession

================================================================================
WORKER OWNERSHIP
================================================================================

Workers are owned permanently once recruited.

Owned Workers remain available unless a future system explicitly introduces
dismissal.

Workers cannot be lost through:

• Combat
• Failed production
• Offline progression
• Building upgrades
• Island reorganization

A Worker may become inactive when no valid assignment or capacity is available,
but remains owned by the player.

================================================================================
WORKER RECRUITMENT
================================================================================

Workers are obtained through recruitment.

Recruitment may require:

• Silver
• Island progression
• Available Worker Capacity
• Profession unlock
• Specific building requirements

The exact recruitment cost is defined by balancing data.

Recruitment creates a permanent Worker.

The player must choose the Worker profession before confirming recruitment.

The profession selection must clearly communicate that it is permanent.

================================================================================
WORKER PROFESSION
================================================================================

Every Worker belongs to exactly one profession.

A Worker profession determines:

• Which resource family the Worker can produce
• Which assignments are valid
• Which Mastery the Worker gains
• Which profession bonuses apply
• Which buildings can support the Worker

Initial Worker professions:

• Woodcutter
• Stonecutter
• Miner
• Fiber Harvester
• Skinner

Profession mapping:

Woodcutter
→ Wood

Stonecutter
→ Stone

Miner
→ Ore

Fiber Harvester
→ Fiber

Skinner
→ Hide

Future professions may include:

• Fisher
• Farmer
• Herbalist
• Alchemist
• Animal Breeder

Future professions must follow the same permanent-profession rule unless the
AI Bible is explicitly revised.

================================================================================
PERMANENT PROFESSION RULE
================================================================================

A Worker profession cannot be changed after recruitment.

This rule exists to:

• Preserve strategic recruitment decisions
• Create long-term specialization
• Prevent one Worker from replacing an entire roster
• Give Worker ownership meaningful identity
• Support profession-specific Mastery progression

The interface must display a confirmation before final recruitment.

Example warning:

"This Worker will permanently become a Miner. This profession cannot be
changed later."

Temporary reassignment within the same profession is allowed.

Changing profession is not allowed.

================================================================================
WORKER MASTERY
================================================================================

Workers do not gain levels.

Each Worker has one profession-specific Mastery value.

Worker Mastery represents experience and efficiency within the Worker's
permanent profession.

Examples:

A Miner gains Mining Mastery.

A Woodcutter gains Woodcutting Mastery.

A Skinner gains Skinning Mastery.

Worker Mastery increases only while the Worker successfully produces resources.

================================================================================
MASTERY EFFECTS
================================================================================

Worker Mastery may improve:

• Production speed
• Resource yield
• Bonus resource chance
• Higher-tier production efficiency
• Assignment efficiency

Mastery must not change the Worker's profession.

Mastery benefits must remain understandable and data-driven.

Exact Mastery formulas belong to the future balancing specification.

================================================================================
MASTERY GAIN
================================================================================

Mastery is gained when a production cycle successfully completes.

Mastery gain may depend on:

• Resource tier
• Assignment duration
• Base production value
• Profession difficulty
• Successful output

Workers do not gain Mastery while:

• Unassigned
• Inactive
• Blocked by invalid conditions
• Waiting for required capacity
• Producing no output

Offline production grants normal Mastery.

================================================================================
NO WORKER LEVELS
================================================================================

Workers never use a traditional character level.

The following concepts are forbidden unless this document is revised:

• Worker Level
• Worker Experience Level
• Worker Stat Points
• Worker Level-Up Choices
• Worker Class Evolution

All Worker progression must come from:

• Profession Mastery
• Island bonuses
• Building bonuses
• Global production modifiers
• Future profession-specific systems

================================================================================
WORKER ASSIGNMENTS
================================================================================

A Worker can have one active assignment at a time.

An assignment defines:

• Resource Family
• Resource Tier
• Production Duration
• Base Yield
• Required Profession
• Required Building
• Required Island Capacity
• Output Destination
• Current Progress

A Worker may only receive assignments compatible with its permanent profession.

Example:

A Miner may gather Ore.

A Miner may not gather Wood, Fiber, Stone or Hide.

================================================================================
ASSIGNMENT STATES
================================================================================

A Worker assignment may be in one of the following states:

Unassigned

The Worker has no active task.

Ready

The Worker has a valid assignment and can begin production.

Producing

The Worker is actively progressing through a production cycle.

Completed

The current production cycle has finished and output is ready for delivery.

Blocked

The assignment cannot continue because one or more requirements are missing.

Paused

The assignment is intentionally paused by the player or system.

Inactive

The Worker has no valid workplace or Worker Capacity.

================================================================================
VALID ASSIGNMENT REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

An assignment is valid only when all required conditions are satisfied.

Possible requirements include:

• Correct Worker profession
• Valid assigned building
• Available Worker slot
• Unlocked resource tier
• Valid output storage
• Active island building
• No conflicting assignment

The system must never silently delete production progress because an assignment
becomes temporarily invalid.

================================================================================
PRODUCTION CYCLE
================================================================================

A production cycle follows this sequence:

Validate Assignment

↓

Start Production Timer

↓

Apply Productivity Modifiers

↓

Complete Production

↓

Generate Resource Output

↓

Grant Worker Mastery

↓

Transfer Output to Storage

↓

Begin Next Cycle

Assignments are continuous by default.

A Worker repeats the same assignment until:

• The player changes it
• The Worker becomes blocked
• The assigned building becomes unavailable
• The assignment is manually paused
• A system rule prevents continuation

================================================================================
WORKER PRODUCTIVITY
================================================================================

Productivity represents the Worker's production performance.

Productivity may affect:

• Production duration
• Resource yield
• Bonus resource probability

Productivity is influenced by:

• Worker Mastery
• Assigned building
• Building tier
• Island upgrades
• Global modifiers
• Future temporary bonuses

Productivity must not be confused with Worker Mastery.

Worker Mastery is permanent Worker progression.

Productivity is the final result of all applicable production modifiers.

================================================================================
BASE PRODUCTION
================================================================================

Every assignment defines a base production output.

The final output is derived from:

• Assignment Base Yield
• Worker Mastery
• Building Productivity
• Island Modifiers
• Global Modifiers
• Bonus Yield Rules

Exact values must remain data-driven.

Worker production must never depend on frame rate.

Worker production must use elapsed time and deterministic production rules.

================================================================================
RESOURCE TIER ACCESS
================================================================================

A Worker may only produce resource tiers unlocked for that profession.

Tier access may depend on:

• Worker Mastery threshold
• Building tier
• Island progression
• Assignment unlock
• Global progression requirements

Unlocking a higher tier does not remove access to lower tiers.

Workers may continue producing lower-tier resources after unlocking higher
tiers.

================================================================================
WORKER CAPACITY
================================================================================

The total number of active Workers is limited by the Player Island.

Worker Capacity may be provided by:

• Worker Houses
• Island upgrades
• Specific buildings
• Future account progression

Owning a Worker does not guarantee that the Worker can be active.

If active Worker Capacity is exceeded:

• Existing Workers remain owned
• Excess Workers become inactive
• No Worker is deleted
• The player chooses which Workers remain active

Detailed capacity rules belong to:

• 19_PLAYER_ISLAND_SYSTEM

================================================================================
WORKER BUILDINGS
================================================================================

Workers require valid island infrastructure.

A Worker may require:

• Housing
• Profession-compatible workplace
• Storage access
• Active building slot

The Worker System validates these requirements.

The Player Island System defines:

• Building placement
• Building capacity
• Building upgrades
• Building availability
• Worker slot allocation

================================================================================
OUTPUT STORAGE
================================================================================

Worker-generated resources are transferred automatically to island storage.

Workers do not maintain personal inventories.

Output handling follows this priority:

1.
Assigned production storage

2.
Primary island storage

3.
Overflow storage, if supported

4.
Blocked delivery state

If no valid storage destination exists, production output must not disappear.

The system must preserve completed output until storage becomes available.

A Worker may stop starting new cycles while completed output is blocked.

================================================================================
STORAGE BLOCKING
================================================================================

When output cannot be delivered:

• The completed resources remain pending
• The Worker enters a Blocked state
• No resources are lost
• Production resumes after storage becomes available

The interface must clearly explain why the Worker is blocked.

Example:

"Production paused: Island Storage is full."

================================================================================
HERO VS WORKER BALANCE
================================================================================

The Hero and Workers fulfill different roles.

Hero gathering is:

• Active
• Faster
• More efficient
• More flexible
• Capable of better bonus rewards
• Controlled directly by the player

Worker gathering is:

• Passive
• Slower
• Predictable
• Specialized
• Limited by island capacity
• Suitable for long-term production

An actively gathering Hero must always generate more value per unit of time than
an equivalent Worker under normal conditions.

Workers may exceed the Hero in total long-term output only because multiple
Workers can operate passively over extended periods.

A single Worker must never outperform an actively gathering Hero of comparable
progression.

================================================================================
WORKER EFFICIENCY LIMIT
================================================================================

Worker efficiency must remain below active Hero efficiency.

This comparison should consider equivalent:

• Resource tier
• Progression stage
• Profession specialization
• Time period
• Available bonuses

Temporary events may improve Worker production, but must not permanently reverse
the intended hierarchy.

Hero Gathering Efficiency
>
Single Worker Gathering Efficiency

This is a permanent balancing rule.

================================================================================
ACTIVE AND PASSIVE PROGRESSION
================================================================================

Workers continue producing while the player performs other activities.

Examples:

• Combat
• Gathering
• Crafting
• Refining
• Island management
• Offline progression

Workers do not require the Hero to remain on the Player Island.

Once a valid assignment is active, production continues independently.

================================================================================
OFFLINE PRODUCTION
================================================================================

Workers continue production while the player is offline.

Offline Worker production follows the same rules as online Worker production.

The system calculates:

• Time elapsed
• Valid production cycles completed
• Partial cycle progress
• Generated resources
• Mastery gained
• Storage limitations
• Blocking conditions

Offline production must not use special reward multipliers unless explicitly
defined by another authority document.

================================================================================
OFFLINE TIME LIMIT
================================================================================

Worker production may be restricted by a global offline duration limit.

The limit must be defined by the Offline Progression System.

When the offline limit is reached:

• Worker production stops advancing
• Existing progress is preserved
• No additional rewards are generated

Workers resume normally when the player returns.

================================================================================
OFFLINE CALCULATION RULES
================================================================================

Offline production must be calculated from elapsed time.

The system must not simulate every second individually.

The calculation should determine:

• Number of complete cycles
• Remaining partial-cycle progress
• Total output
• Total Mastery gain
• First blocking event, when relevant

Offline calculations must produce results equivalent to online production under
the same conditions.

================================================================================
ASSIGNMENT CHANGES
================================================================================

The player may change a Worker's assignment within the same profession.

Example:

A Miner may switch from Tier 2 Ore to Tier 3 Ore.

A Worker may not switch to another resource family outside its profession.

When changing assignments, the system must define how current progress is
handled.

Default rule:

• Current incomplete production progress is cancelled
• No partial resources are generated
• Completed pending output is preserved
• The new assignment begins from zero progress

The interface must warn the player before cancelling meaningful progress.

================================================================================
PAUSING AND RESUMING
================================================================================

The player may pause a Worker assignment.

While paused:

• No production progress is gained
• No Mastery is gained
• Existing progress is preserved
• Completed pending output remains available

Resuming continues from the preserved production progress.

================================================================================
WORKER REMOVAL FROM BUILDINGS
================================================================================

Removing a Worker from an assigned building makes the Worker inactive or
unassigned.

Default behavior:

• Incomplete production progress is preserved
• Completed output remains pending
• The Worker stops producing
• The Worker remains owned
• The Worker can be assigned again later

Building removal must never delete the Worker.

================================================================================
WORKER DISMISSAL
================================================================================

Worker dismissal is not part of the initial system.

Workers are permanent owned units.

A future dismissal feature may be added only if it includes:

• Explicit player confirmation
• Clear explanation of permanent loss
• Safe handling of current assignments
• Safe handling of pending output
• Appropriate economic consequences

Until such a system is formally approved, Workers cannot be dismissed.

================================================================================
WORKER NAMES
================================================================================

Workers may receive generated or player-selected display names.

Worker names are cosmetic.

Names do not affect:

• Profession
• Mastery
• Productivity
• Assignment validity
• Resource output

Renaming may be supported as a convenience feature.

================================================================================
WORKER UNIQUENESS
================================================================================

Two Workers may share the same profession and Mastery value.

Workers are still separate owned entities.

Each Worker maintains independent:

• Mastery
• Assignment
• Progress
• Building allocation
• Productivity modifiers
• State

Workers must never share production progress.

WORKER MANAGEMENT ACTIONS
================================================================================

The player must be able to:

• Recruit a Worker

• View owned Workers
• Assign a Worker to a building
• Select a valid production assignment
• Pause production
• Resume production
• Change assignment

================================================================================
NOTIFICATIONS
================================================================================

The Worker System may notify the player when:

• A Worker is recruited
• A profession is permanently confirmed
• A new resource tier is unlocked
• Production becomes blocked
• Storage becomes full
• Worker Capacity is exceeded
• A significant Mastery threshold is reached
• Offline production is collected

Notifications should communicate meaningful changes without creating excessive
interruptions.

================================================================================
ERROR HANDLING
================================================================================

The Worker System must never silently fail.

Invalid conditions must produce clear results.

Examples:

Wrong profession

"This assignment requires a Miner."

No Worker Capacity

"No active Worker slot is available."

Invalid building

"This Worker requires a compatible production building."

Storage full

"Production paused because no storage space is available."

Locked resource tier

"This resource tier has not been unlocked for this Worker."

================================================================================
DETERMINISM
================================================================================

Worker production should be deterministic where possible.

Given identical:

• Worker data
• Assignment data
• Modifiers
• Elapsed time
• Storage conditions

The system should generate identical base production results.

Random bonus output may use controlled RNG.

Random calculations must not be repeated after loading a save if the production
cycle was already resolved.

================================================================================
BALANCING RULES
================================================================================

Worker balancing must satisfy the following rules.

• Workers provide meaningful value.
• Workers do not make gathering obsolete.
• Workers do not make combat obsolete.
• Worker output remains predictable.
• Worker specialization matters.
• Recruiting additional Workers creates strategic value.
• Worker Capacity prevents uncontrolled passive scaling.
• Higher Mastery improves efficiency without creating infinite growth.
• Higher-tier resources require meaningful progression.
• Passive production supports crafting without flooding the economy.

================================================================================
ECONOMIC SAFEGUARDS
================================================================================

The Worker System must include economic constraints.

Possible constraints include:

• Recruitment cost
• Worker Capacity
• Building requirements
• Tier unlock requirements
• Production duration
• Storage capacity
• Diminishing productivity scaling
• Island upgrade costs

The system should prevent unlimited passive resource generation without
corresponding investment.

================================================================================
EXPLOIT PREVENTION
================================================================================

The following behaviors must be prevented:

• Reassigning professions
• Duplicating Worker output through save reloads
• Repeating offline reward collection
• Producing without valid Worker Capacity
• Producing without a valid assignment
• Producing while blocked
• Duplicating Workers through building removal
• Resetting production to reroll rewards
• Bypassing resource tier requirements

Worker production must be validated by authoritative game state.

================================================================================
SUMMARY
================================================================================

Workers are permanent passive production units managed through the Player
Island.

Each Worker has exactly one permanent gathering profession.

Workers do not have traditional levels.

Their permanent progression is represented exclusively through profession-
specific Mastery.

Workers perform one compatible assignment at a time and automatically deliver
their output to island storage.

They continue producing while the player is active or offline, provided all
assignment, building, capacity and storage conditions remain valid.

Workers support production and reduce repetitive gathering, but they never
replace active gameplay.

An actively gathering Hero must always outperform a single Worker of comparable
progression.

The Worker System exists to create long-term production strategy, permanent
specialization and meaningful passive progression while preserving combat and
active gathering as the most valuable player activities.
