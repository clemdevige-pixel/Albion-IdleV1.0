# 20A. COMBAT BALANCING PROCESS

---

# Purpose

This document is the authoritative workflow for balancing combat zones, weapons, equipment and off-hand combinations in Albion Idle.

The live CombatRuntime is the source of truth for player-vs-zone difficulty. Synthetic DPS/EHP tools are diagnostic tools used to understand why a runtime result occurs; they do not replace the runtime.

---

# 1. Core balancing rules

1. Balance from authored data, never from renderer/UI exceptions.
2. Use the exact live combat stack for progression validation.
3. Do not compensate for a weapon problem by globally weakening a zone.
4. Do not compensate for a zone problem by inflating every equipment item.
5. Preserve weapon identity: equal DPS is not the goal.
6. For comparable weapons intended to fill the same role, an approximately 5-10% performance delta is acceptable when it serves identity and the complete package remains coherent. Weapons with deliberately different content niches may diverge more strongly when their strengths reverse in other relevant content.
7. A one-handed weapon and its off-hand are separate balance objects. Never attribute off-hand power to the intrinsic weapon score.
8. Potions are a progression tool, not part of an AFK baseline unless the contract explicitly says otherwise.
9. Raw tuning values are data. The progression contract is design law.
10. Long-term weapon balance targets viability across the game, not equivalence in every activity.
11. NORMAL world progression is structurally monotonic: normal zone starts must not regress below the previous normal zone end, and normal intra-zone curves must not decrease.
12. A final boss explicitly authored with `progressionRole: "boss_gate"` is an approved exception to normal monotonicity. It may create a local difficulty spike above the following zone entry.
13. The following zone is compared to the previous NORMAL progression envelope, not to the boss-gate spike.
14. Representative weapons are not required to clear ordinary progression walls with identical ease. End-of-Tier boss gates are stricter: their explicit contract must be satisfied by the full representative weapon set.

# 2. Live benchmark architecture

PRIMARY RUNTIME TOOL
- `apps/client/src/runtime/CombatRuntimeBenchmarkHarness.ts`
- Entry point: `runCombatRuntimeBenchmark(...)`
- Accepts any authored ZoneDefinitionId; it is not world-band specific.
- Runs the exact live CombatRuntime tick by tick.
- Uses live equipment, mastery, ability, damage, death, consumable and zone-placement systems.
- No alternate combat formula may be implemented inside this harness.

PRIMARY WEAPON DIAGNOSTICS
- `apps/client/src/data/weaponIdealBenchmark.ts`
- `apps/client/src/data/weaponPackageBenchmark.ts`

weaponIdealBenchmark provides sustained DPS, opener DPS, AA/ability contribution and defensive metrics.

weaponPackageBenchmark separates:
- WEAPON ONLY: intrinsic offensive identity;
- NEUTRAL PACKAGE: identical armor and no off-hand;
- EXPLICIT LOADOUT: weapon + explicitly selected armor/off-hand.

Future off-hands must be passed explicitly. Do not recreate a rule where every 1H weapon implicitly owns a shield.

ENCHANTMENT ECONOMY BENCHMARKS
- `enchantmentShardTtkBenchmark.ts`
- `enchantmentShardFarmSweep.test.ts`
- `enchantmentShardWorldFarmSweep.test.ts`
- `enchantmentTierBridgeSweep.test.ts`
- `enchantmentShardBalance.test.ts`

BOSS-GATE REGRESSION

# 4. Weapon balancing workflow

For every new weapon or major rebalance:

1. Define intended identity and handling.
2. Define the content niches where the weapon should excel, remain viable or struggle.
3. Run weapon-only diagnostics at representative mastery/enchantment checkpoints.
4. Inspect sustained DPS and opener 5 s / 10 s.
5. Run neutral package comparisons.
6. Run explicit real loadouts; test relevant off-hands separately for 1H weapons.
7. Validate real progression checkpoints through CombatRuntimeBenchmarkHarness.
8. When one weapon alone breaks a global progression contract, diagnose that weapon/package before changing the zone.
9. Tune the smallest authored lever: base damage, cadence, ability ratio, cooldown, conditional mechanic, control, sustain or off-hand stats.
10. Re-run typecheck + weapon diagnostics + affected progression/economy sweeps.

A weapon may be below the offensive center if another part of its explicit build package compensates. The diagnostic must still report that difference rather than hiding it inside one score.

## 4.1 T5 Broadsword correction — validated 2026-08-20

Ironveil boss-gate diagnostics exposed a real T5 overlap:
- Longbow T5.2 could remain above Broadsword T5.3 in the same boss-gate context.
- A global boss multiplier could not enforce `T5.2 + potion = blocked` and `T5.3 + potion = all weapons pass` without breaking Broadsword.

Weapon/package diagnostics showed the Broadsword T5 authored value created an artificial offensive trough.

Validated correction:
- T4 Broadsword base damage: 86
- T5: 120 (was 110)
- T6: 155
- T7: 210
- T8: 275

The T5 correction is data-driven in `weaponContentCatalog.ts`; there is no T5-specific runtime override.

At T5.3 with the explicit shield package, the 120 candidate produced a package score around 99.7 and restored the strict Ironveil boss-gate contract. Larger candidates were unnecessary.

# 5. Zone balancing workflow

For each new zone or world band:

1. Write the progression contract before editing enemy numbers.
2. Define checkpoints covering entry, middle, late zone and final wall.
3. For every checkpoint define expected tier, enchantment, mastery, potion expectation and intended state.
4. Validate structural monotonicity for NORMAL progression.
5. Run the exact live runtime across the representative weapon set.
6. Read clear/fail, remaining HP, encounter reached, duration and potion usage together.
7. Check weapon-only/package diagnostics before changing the zone when only one weapon is failing.
8. Tune the smallest relevant authored zone curve endpoint or content stat.
9. Re-run earlier checkpoints to protect the normal curve shape.
10. Freeze the contract semantically; do not freeze accidental historical tuning values.

When adding later-band content, reuse CombatRuntimeBenchmarkHarness. Do not create band-specific harnesses.

## 5.1 Normal monotonicity rule

Normal world difficulty remains monotonic.

A normal segment or normal zone entry must not become easier than the previous normal progression point simply to solve a local balance issue.

## 5.2 End-of-Tier boss-gate exception

A final boss may intentionally be harder than the next zone entry when it has an authored `bossGate` profile.

Design purpose:
- the boss certifies completion of the current Tier;
- the player should not enter the next production Tier while still materially under-geared for the previous one;
- the next zone can return to the normal progression envelope instead of inheriting an artificially extreme entry difficulty.

Implementation rule:
- author `bossGate` in `packages/data/src/config/combat-progression.ts`;
- do NOT key behavior from segment index, zone name or renderer state;
- boss pressure remains HP / damage / defense multipliers applied only to the final boss encounter.

STRICT END-OF-TIER CONTRACT
For the representative normal weapon set at end-of-tier mastery:
- full `Tn.2 + potion` = 0 universal clears;
- full `Tn.3 + potion` = all representative weapons clear;
- `Tn.3` without potion is not required to be universal unless separately authored.

The wall is produced by combat pressure, not by an artificial equipment-slot lock.

# 6. Potion and AFK semantics

NO POTION / AFK COMFORT
- A stable farm profile should clear without potion dependency.

POTION BRIDGE
- A previous gear level may use potions to enter or advance into the next progression block when the contract allows it.

ROLE-AWARE POTION FALLBACK
- On ordinary progression walls, representative weapons do not need identical clear comfort.

END-OF-TIER BOSS GATE
- Potion support is part of the validated gate contract.
- `Tn.3 + potion` is the universal reference state.
- `Tn.2 + potion` must not provide a universal bypass.

The benchmark uses live health potion behavior and cooldown. Never model a free heal per encounter.

# 7. Validated Blue progression contract — updated 2026-08-20

BIRCH FOREST
Expected: starter weapon only.
Function: discovery, no real friction.

DARK SWAMP
Expected: starter -> full T3.
Function: first equipment-driven progression block.

STONE HIGHLANDS
Expected: full T3 -> first T4 pieces.
Function: T3/T4 transition.

GOLDEN STEPPE
Expected: full T4.0 -> first enchantments.
Function: T4 consolidation.

FROSTPEAK / MOUNTAIN
Expected normal progression: T4.1 -> T4.2 -> T4.3.
Function: final Blue progression block plus explicit end-of-Tier boss certification.

Normal Frostpeak segments may be pushed before full T4.3 according to weapon profile. The final boss is different: it is an authored `boss_gate` and is intended to require optimized T4.3 + potion across the representative set.

This intentionally supersedes the older contract where T4.2 + potion universally cleared the final boss.

After the boss is defeated, Yellow/T5 entry does NOT need to exceed the Frostpeak boss spike. Yellow entry follows the normal bridge envelope.

# 8. Current representative weapon interpretation

Weapon identity remains intentionally asymmetric.

Longbow and Infernal tend toward higher offense; Broadsword trades offense for explicit shield-package defense; Spiked and Dual Dagger carry their own cadence/mechanics profiles.

Do not treat a faster deterministic farm weapon as automatically broken. Investigate persistent global outliers and progression-contract failures.

The T5 Broadsword correction is the model for this process: diagnose the weapon/package when a single profile prevents a valid global gate before distorting the whole zone.

# Source-of-truth rule

Difficulty truth = live CombatRuntime + authored content data.
Weapon/equipment truth = authoritative catalogs.
Weapon diagnostics explain the result; they never override it.
Documentation stores the intended contract; tests protect the contract.
