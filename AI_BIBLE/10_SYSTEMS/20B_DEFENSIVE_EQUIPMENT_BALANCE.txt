# 20B. DEFENSIVE EQUIPMENT BALANCE

Status: AUTHORITATIVE BALANCE CONTRACT
Date: 2026-08-19

## Slot roles
Chest = primary survivability piece.
Head = secondary survivability piece.
Boots = tertiary but meaningful survivability piece.
Validated ordering: CHEST > HEAD > BOOTS.

## Authored Max Health distribution
T3: Head 70 / Chest 125 / Boots 35 / Total 230
T4: Head 90 / Chest 160 / Boots 50 / Total 300
T5: Head 150 / Chest 210 / Boots 60 / Total 420
T6: Head 170 / Chest 310 / Boots 100 / Total 580
T7: Head 235 / Chest 420 / Boots 125 / Total 780
T8: Head 330 / Chest 535 / Boots 155 / Total 1020

Armor and Magic Resistance stay unchanged. The distribution preserves the previously validated full-set HP totals after per-item enchantment scaling and 5-HP rounding.

## Design rules
- Do not add generic Attack Speed to Boots; attack cadence belongs to weapon identity.
- Do not add generic Cooldown Reduction without a weapon-wide rebalance.
- Do not add sustain or out-of-combat regeneration solely to make Boots relevant.
- Full-set survivability is the invariant: redistribution must not silently buff the complete set.
- Boots must materially affect progression while remaining less important than Head and Chest.

## Runtime validation
A temporary T3-T7 runtime sweep confirmed that removing Boots now frequently turns representative progression clears into failures, while removing Head remains more punishing. The temporary diagnostic was removed after validation.
