# ALBION IDLE — AWAKENED WEAPON (.4) SYSTEM

Status: DESIGN BASELINE VALIDATED — values are data-driven and remain balance-tunable.

## 1. PURPOSE

Weapon enchantment .4 is an endgame progression layer built around long-term investment into one weapon instance.

A .4 weapon is not immediately Awakened. After acquisition, the player must first farm eligible PvE Fame to charge Attunement up to an authored Awakening threshold. Once Awakened, the weapon can be customized through traits. Progression has no hard upgrade cap: increasing Strain makes further investment increasingly expensive and acts as the natural soft cap.

All numeric values in this document are authored balance data. System logic must not hardcode tier tables, roll ranges, slot thresholds, cost curves, critical chance, storage multipliers, or CDR curve parameters.

## 1A. .4 ACQUISITION

.4 is NOT a separate crafting recipe. It is the final step of the existing deterministic enchantment path:

.0 -> .1 -> .2 -> .3 -> .4

Only weapons are eligible for .4 Awakening. Conventional armor/off-hand equipment remains capped by its authored enchantment policy.

The .3 -> .4 transaction reuses the existing EnchantmentService and merchant enchantment UI. It consumes, atomically:
- the same weapon instance currently at .3;
- 100 tier-matching enchantment fragments/shards;
- refined craft resources derived from that weapon's existing craft-material definition;
- Silver through the existing enchantment cost pipeline.

Current V1 data baseline for the .4 transition:
- tier fragment/shard cost: 100;
- refined craft-material multiplier: x8 relative to the weapon's authored craft-material quantities;
- base Silver cost before existing tier/category scaling: 25,000.

These quantities are balance data, not structural rules. They may be recalibrated without changing the .3 -> .4 transaction architecture.

A successful .3 -> .4 commit immediately registers a fresh, UNAWAKENED weapon state for the resulting ItemInstanceId. It must not wait for the first Fame gain.

## 2. CORE LOOP

.4 acquisition -> eligible PvE Fame -> stored Attunement -> reach initial Awakening threshold -> spend that Attunement to Awaken Weapon -> spend Attunement + Silver on trait actions -> gain Strain -> future costs and storage rise.

Baseline conversion:
- 1 eligible PvE Fame = 1 Attunement.
- Fame continues to feed its normal systems independently.
- Attunement earned while the weapon storage is full is not banked beyond the cap.
- Any eligible PvE Fame can generate Attunement for the equipped .4 weapon regardless of the content tier.
- Fame Bonus increases progression/mastery Fame only. It NEVER increases Attunement gain; Attunement uses the eligible Fame before the weapon's Fame Bonus is applied.

The weapon tracks separately:
- Awakened / unawakened state;
- currently stored Attunement;
- lifetime Attunement invested since the last full reset;
- Strain;
- selected traits and their accumulated values;
- dynamic Attunement storage cap.

## 3. INITIAL AWAKENING

A freshly acquired .4 weapon must first accumulate a minimum amount of Attunement before it can be Awakened.

Baseline thresholds:

Tier | Awakening threshold / cost
T4.4 | 5,000
T5.4 | 10,000
T6.4 | 15,000
T7.4 | 20,000
T8.4 | 25,000

Reaching the threshold enables the explicit Awaken Weapon action. Confirming Awakening CONSUMES exactly the tier threshold from stored Attunement and records that amount as lifetime Attunement invested.

Awakening itself:
- consumes no Silver;
- adds no Strain;
- consumes the required Attunement;
- unlocks access to trait actions.

Before Awakening:
- Attunement can be earned and stored normally;
- trait rolls, improvements and rerolls are forbidden;
- Strain remains 0.

## 4. ATTUNEMENT STORAGE

Baseline initial caps:

Tier | Initial cap
T4.4 | 15,000
T5.4 | 28,000
T6.4 | 35,000
T7.4 | 38,000
T8.4 | 40,000

The maximum storage increases after every trait modification because every modification adds Strain.

Baseline dynamic storage rule:

StrainScaledCap = InitialCap * (1 + Strain * 0.025)
AttunementCap = max(StrainScaledCap, NextAttunementCost * 1.5)

Therefore each +1 Strain adds storage equal to 2.5% of the tier's initial cap:
- T4: +375 capacity per Strain;
- T5: +700 capacity per Strain;
- T6: +875 capacity per Strain;
- T7: +950 capacity per Strain;
- T8: +1,000 capacity per Strain.

The 2.5% growth and x1.5 next-cost safeguard are data-driven.
The next cost must be the real cost at the weapon's current Strain, not the tier base cost.

## 5. TRAIT SLOTS

A weapon can have a maximum of 3 active traits.

Trait slots unlock from Strain, not lifetime Attunement invested:
- Slot 1: available immediately after Awakening (Strain 0).
- Slot 2: unlocked at Strain 10.
- Slot 3: unlocked at Strain 30.

Unlocking a slot does NOT grant a free trait. The first roll for the newly unlocked slot is a normal paid modification and adds Strain.

Trait slot thresholds are data-driven.

## 6. TRAIT POOL

Exactly 8 trait types are currently validated:

1. Item Power (IP)
2. Damage
3. Ability Power
4. Cooldown Reduction (CDR)
5. Max HP
6. Armor
7. Magic Resistance (MR)
8. Fame Bonus

Attack Speed is explicitly excluded from the awakened trait pool.

Duplicate traits on one weapon are forbidden. When selecting/rerolling a slot, traits already occupying other slots are excluded from the eligible pool.

Fame Bonus applies to progression/mastery Fame earned while the awakened .4 weapon is equipped. It does not modify Attunement gain and therefore cannot accelerate its own Awakening progression.

## 7. BASELINE TRAIT ROLLS

Normal Awake roll ranges:

Trait | Normal roll
IP | +1 to +3 IP
Damage | +0.20% to +0.40%
Ability Power | +0.20% to +0.40%
CDR progression | +0.5 to +1 internal progression
Max HP | +3 to +6 HP
Armor | +0.5 to +1.0 Armor
Magic Resistance | +0.5 to +1.0 MR
Fame Bonus | +0.5% to +1.0% Fame

Percentage rolls may be continuous values. IP is integer-valued.
HP, Armor and MR are flat values, not percentage modifiers.

All ranges are balance data and may be recalibrated without changing system architecture.

## 8. CRITICAL ATTUNEMENT

Only IMPROVEMENTS of an already selected trait may critically Attune.

Baseline Critical Attunement chance: 15% per trait improvement.

Critical Attunement:
- doubles the rolled improvement gain (x2);
- does not add extra Strain;
- does not change the action cost;
- does not change future roll ranges.

Critical Attunement NEVER applies while choosing a trait for an empty slot and NEVER applies while choosing a replacement trait during a reroll. Trait-selection proposals always display and grant their normal base roll.

Fame Bonus follows the same critical rule as other traits: no critical on selection/reroll, but improvements can critically double the rolled Fame Bonus gain.

For CDR, a critical improvement doubles the INTERNAL progression gained before effective CDR is recalculated. It must never directly double the displayed effective CDR percentage.

Critical chance and multiplier are data-driven.

## 9. COOLDOWN REDUCTION

CDR uses an internal uncapped progression value P with diminishing returns.

EffectiveCDRPercent = 50 * P / (P + 50)
FinalCooldown = BaseCooldown * (1 - EffectiveCDRPercent / 100)

Properties:
- P has no hard cap.
- Effective CDR approaches 50% asymptotically and never reaches/exceeds it through this curve.
- UI displays only effective CDR actually applied to abilities.
- A Critical Attunement improvement doubles internal CDR progression gained, not displayed CDR.

## 10. AWAKE / TRAIT IMPROVEMENT FLOW

To improve an existing trait:
1. Player selects an unlocked trait.
2. UI previews Attunement cost, Silver cost, current/new Strain, normal roll range and Critical Attunement chance.
3. Player confirms.
4. Attunement and Silver are consumed.
5. Strain increases by 1.
6. A value is rolled in the trait's authored range.
7. Critical Attunement is resolved (baseline 15%, x2).
8. Final gain is permanently added to the trait.
9. Lifetime Attunement invested increases by the Attunement actually spent.
10. Slot unlocks and storage cap are recomputed.

A critical result never adds additional Strain.

## 11. FIRST TRAIT ROLL

When an empty unlocked slot is populated:
- the action is paid using the same Attunement + Silver economy;
- it adds +1 Strain;
- traits already used by other slots are excluded;
- the player receives 3 random eligible trait proposals where the eligible pool size permits;
- every proposal uses a normal base roll from that trait's authored range;
- Critical Attunement is disabled during this choice;
- the selected trait begins with exactly the displayed base-roll value;
- lifetime Attunement invested increases by the Attunement spent.

No slot grants a free modification.

## 12. TRAIT REROLL

Reroll targets ONE existing trait slot. It is not a global reroll of the weapon.

Flow:
1. Player selects the trait slot to reroll.
2. Player pays the current Attunement + Silver action cost.
3. Strain increases by 1 immediately.
4. Lifetime Attunement invested increases by the Attunement spent.
5. The traits occupying the other slots are excluded from the pool.
6. The currently targeted trait may appear again.
7. Player receives 3 random eligible proposals where pool size permits.
8. Every proposal uses a normal base roll; Critical Attunement is disabled during reroll selection.
9. Player may accept a proposed trait or keep the existing trait.

The cost and Strain remain consumed even if the player keeps the existing trait.

If a different trait is accepted:
- all accumulated progression of the replaced trait is lost;
- the replacement starts from exactly the normal base-roll value displayed in the proposal;
- no Critical Attunement can occur on the replacement selection;
- no value/progression transfers between trait types.

## 13. FULL AWAKENING RESET

The player may fully reset a .4 weapon's Awakening progression.
Baseline reset cost: free.
The lost investment is the economic cost.

Reset effects:
- Awakened -> false;
- all traits removed;
- all accumulated trait values removed;
- Strain -> 0;
- stored Attunement -> 0;
- lifetime Attunement invested -> 0;
- slots 2 and 3 locked again;
- dynamic storage cap reset to the tier's initial cap;
- no Attunement refunded;
- no Silver refunded;
- the item remains a .4 weapon and is not destroyed/downgraded.

After reset, the weapon must again reach and PAY its tier Awakening threshold before traits can be rolled.

## 14. STRAIN AND COSTS

Every trait modification attempt increases Strain by exactly +1:
- first trait roll;
- first roll on newly unlocked slot;
- improvement of an existing trait;
- reroll attempt.

Initial Awakening itself does NOT add Strain.
A failed/unwanted reroll still adds Strain.
Critical Attunement does not add extra Strain.

There is no designed hard Strain cap. Progressively rising costs are the natural progression cap.

Baseline tier costs at Strain 0:

Tier | Base Attunement | Base Silver
T4.4 | 1,000 | 12,000
T5.4 | 2,000 | 24,000
T6.4 | 3,000 | 36,000
T7.4 | 4,000 | 48,000
T8.4 | 5,000 | 60,000

Base Silver is intentionally tier-linear at 12,000 Silver per 1,000 base Attunement. This keeps tier progression explicit and avoids the previous near-flat T7/T8 Silver baseline.

Both post-Awakening costs now use compounded per-Strain growth so every successive trait action is strictly more expensive than the previous one.

Baseline Attunement growth:

AttunementGrowthPerStrain = 1.03
AttunementMultiplier = AttunementGrowthPerStrain ^ Strain
AttunementCost = BaseAttunement(tier) * AttunementMultiplier

Each +1 Strain therefore increases the next Attunement cost by approximately 3% relative to the previous action.

Approximate Attunement pacing:
Strain 0 -> x1.00
Strain 10 -> x1.344
Strain 20 -> x1.806
Strain 30 -> x2.427
Strain 50 -> x4.384
Strain 75 -> x9.179
Strain 100 -> x19.219

T8 Attunement references:
Strain 0 -> 5,000 Attunement
Strain 10 -> 6,720 Attunement
Strain 30 -> 12,136 Attunement
Strain 50 -> 21,920 Attunement
Strain 75 -> 45,895 Attunement
Strain 100 -> 96,093 Attunement

Baseline Silver growth:

SilverGrowthPerStrain = 1.026
SilverMultiplier = SilverGrowthPerStrain ^ Strain
SilverCost = BaseSilver(tier) * SilverMultiplier

Each +1 Strain therefore increases the next Silver cost by approximately 2.6% relative to the previous action.

Approximate Silver pacing:
Strain 0 -> x1.00
Strain 10 -> x1.293
Strain 30 -> x2.160
Strain 50 -> x3.609
Strain 75 -> x6.856
Strain 100 -> x13.024

T8 Silver references:
Strain 0 -> 60,000 Silver
Strain 10 -> 77,558 Silver
Strain 30 -> 129,590 Silver
Strain 50 -> 216,531 Silver
Strain 75 -> 411,342 Silver
Strain 100 -> 781,425 Silver

The intent is deliberate: every Awake becomes a little more expensive than the previous one, producing a smooth and readable long-term progression rather than threshold-like jumps. Attunement grows slightly faster than Silver so it remains the primary long-term soft-cap mechanism while Silver stays a meaningful economic sink.

Both growth factors are authored balance data and may be recalibrated without changing the progression architecture.

## 15. DESIGN GUARANTEES

The implementation must preserve these invariants:
- .4 is acquired through the existing .3 -> .4 enchantment transaction, never a parallel craft path.
- only authored .4-eligible weapons may enter Awakening.
- a successful .4 transaction registers an UNAWAKENED state for the committed ItemInstanceId.
- trait actions are impossible until the initial tier Awakening threshold is reached and the weapon is explicitly Awakened.
- initial Awakening consumes its full Attunement threshold, consumes no Silver and adds no Strain.
- .4 progression is instance-specific.
- No duplicate traits on one weapon.
- No free trait modification.
- Every trait modification attempt adds Strain except the initial Awakening and full reset.
- every +1 Strain increases both the next Attunement and Silver action costs through their authored compounded growth factors.
- every +1 Strain increases the baseline Attunement storage capacity by 2.5% of the tier initial cap, while next-cost x1.5 remains a minimum-cap safeguard.
- Critical Attunement can occur only on improvements of an already selected trait, never during initial trait choice or reroll selection.
- Critical Attunement rewards luck and never punishes it with extra Strain.
- Reroll cannot transfer accumulated value to another trait.
- Full reset returns the weapon to the unawakened .4 state.
- Stored Attunement and lifetime invested Attunement are distinct values.
- Slot unlocks are controlled by Strain thresholds 0 / 10 / 30.
- eligible PvE Fame can generate Attunement regardless of the equipped .4 weapon tier or content tier.
- Fame Bonus modifies progression/mastery Fame only; eligible Fame before the weapon Fame Bonus remains the source for Attunement.
- CDR UI must display effective reduction, never its hidden progression variable.
- Balance values are authored data, not branching gameplay logic.

## 16. BALANCE STATUS

These numbers are the validated V1 design baseline, not final live balance.

They were established before the planned global T4-T8 combat recalibration following the resistance mitigation formula change. Fame/h and Silver/h may move during that recalibration.

When economy/combat baselines change, recalibrate the .4 DATA only. Do not redesign or fork the system logic unless testing reveals a structural problem.

Current resistance model relevant to Armor/MR awakened traits:

Mitigation = Resistance / (Resistance + 100)

## 17. IMPLEMENTATION DIRECTION

Architecture must remain deterministic and data-driven.

Recommended separation of concerns:
- immutable/authored Awakening balance configuration;
- existing EnchantmentService owns the .3 -> .4 transaction;
- per-item-instance .4 state is registered immediately after acquisition but starts unawakened;
- pure cost/cap/CDR/slot-threshold calculations;
- deterministic RNG integration for trait proposals, rolls and critical resolution;
- explicit domain actions for initial Awakening, trait improvement, reroll and reset;
- persistence of every instance-specific field;
- UI consumes resolved state and previews from the domain layer; UI must not reproduce formulas;
- Fame Bonus is applied in the reward/progression pipeline after eligible Fame is captured for Attunement.

Do not create parallel combat-stat formulas for awakened weapons. Trait outputs must feed the existing stat/equipment/combat pipelines.
