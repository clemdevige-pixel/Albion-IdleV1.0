# 29_MASTERY_SYSTEM

## Purpose

The Mastery System is the primary long-term progression system of the game.

It replaces traditional character levels by allowing players to permanently improve each equipment mastery independently through combat experience.

Masteries reward long-term specialization while preserving complete freedom to switch builds at any time.

The system is entirely data-driven and designed to support future expansion without requiring code modifications.

---

# Design Goals

- Replace character levels.
- Reward long-term specialization.
- Preserve permanent progression.
- Encourage experimenting with multiple weapons.
- Support infinite endgame progression.
- Never waste earned experience.
- Remain entirely data-driven.
- Be easily expandable to armor and future equipment types.

---

# Core Principles

- There is no Character Level.
- Every Mastery progresses independently.
- Every Mastery has its own experience pool.
- Masteries never lose progress.
- Masteries are account permanent.
- Equipment progression remains the primary source of player power.
- Masteries provide long-term progression.

---

# Mastery Categories

## Version 1

Only weapons possess Masteries.

Examples:

- Broadsword Mastery
- Claymore Mastery
- Bow Mastery
- Fire Staff Mastery

---

## Future Expansion

The architecture must support additional Mastery categories without modifying the system.

Examples:

- Helmet Mastery
- Armor Mastery
- Boots Mastery
- Off-Hand Mastery
- Mount Mastery
- Tool Mastery

The Mastery System is therefore based on generic Mastery Definitions rather than weapon-specific logic.

---

# Mastery Discovery

All Masteries are visible inside the Mastery Interface from the beginning of the game.

Undiscovered Masteries appear as locked.

A Mastery becomes permanently unlocked the first time the player obtains the corresponding equipment through any acquisition method.

Examples:

- Loot
- Crafting
- Purchase
- Quest Reward
- Event Reward

Once unlocked:

- it can never become locked again.
- its progression is permanently saved.
- it remains visible forever.

---

# Mastery Progression

Each Mastery has its own:

- Level
- Experience
- Reward Table

Progression earned on one Mastery never increases the Level or Experience of another Mastery.

A weapon specialization may however contribute Item Power to other weapon specializations of the same family through the cross-specialization IP rule defined below.

Example:

Broadsword Mastery Level 82

does not increase

Claymore Mastery Level 15

but Broadsword Mastery contributes cross-specialization IP while a different Sword specialization is equipped.

---

# Weapon Mastery Item Power

Weapon Mastery Item Power is calculated from three independent contributions.

## Family Mastery

Each Family Mastery level grants:

+0.5 IP

to every weapon specialization inside that family.

Example:

Sword Family Mastery Level 50

=

+25 IP to every Sword weapon.

---

## Equipped Specialization Mastery

Each level of the currently equipped weapon specialization grants:

+1 IP

to that equipped specialization.

Example:

Carving Sword Mastery Level 40

=

+40 IP while Carving Sword is equipped.

---

## Cross-Specialization Mastery

Each level earned on a weapon specialization also grants:

+0.2 IP

to every OTHER weapon specialization belonging to the same weapon family.

The currently equipped specialization does not also receive its own +0.2 IP contribution.

Cross-specialization IP never crosses weapon-family boundaries.

Example:

Equipped weapon:

Carving Sword

Masteries:

Sword Family 50

Carving Sword 40

Broadsword 30

Clarent Blade 20

Galatine Pair 10

Claymore 5

Result:

Family contribution

50 × 0.5 = 25 IP

Equipped specialization contribution

40 × 1 = 40 IP

Other Sword specialization contribution

(30 + 20 + 10 + 5) × 0.2 = 13 IP

Total Mastery IP

78 IP

Formula:

Mastery IP = Family Level × 0.5

+ Equipped Specialization Level × 1.0

+ Sum of Other Specialization Levels in Same Family × 0.2

This rule intentionally rewards players for developing the entire weapon branch while preserving the equipped specialization as the strongest individual progression source.

The rule is data-driven from weapon-family membership. Adding a new specialization to an existing family automatically makes it participate in cross-specialization IP.

# Experience

Only the currently equipped weapon receives Weapon Experience.

Experience sources are defined inside the Experience System.

Experience is added to the corresponding Mastery.

Level-ups are processed immediately.

Multiple level-ups may occur simultaneously.

---

# Mastery Rewards

Rewards are milestone-based.

Masteries never grant automatic stat increases every level outside the Weapon Mastery Item Power formula defined above.

Each reward is explicitly defined inside a Reward Table.

Possible rewards include:

- Flat Attack
- Flat Defense
- Flat Health
- Flat Energy
- Ability Unlock
- Ability Upgrade
- Passive Unlock
- Passive Upgrade
- Equipment Tier Unlock

Future reward types can be added without code modifications.

# Independent Weapon Progression

Each weapon possesses its own Mastery.

Example:

Broadsword Mastery

Level 100

Claymore Mastery

Level 41

Bow Mastery

Level 8

Changing weapons immediately switches to the corresponding equipped Specialization Mastery.

Mastery Levels and Experience are never shared between weapons.

Only the cross-specialization IP contribution is shared between different specializations of the same family.

---

# Permanent Progression

Masteries never lose progress.

Selling, destroying or salvaging equipment never affects Mastery progression.

If the player later acquires the equipment again, progression resumes immediately.

---

# Maximum Level

Upon reaching maximum level:

- further level progression stops.
- the Mastery remains usable.
- normal gameplay continues.

Experience earned after reaching the maximum level is converted into Overflow Experience.

---

# Overflow Experience

When a Mastery reaches maximum level, earned experience is no longer lost.

Instead, part of the earned experience is converted into Overflow Experience.

Example:

10,000 Mastery XP earned

↓

Overflow Conversion Rate

↓

2,000 Overflow XP

The conversion rate is fully data-driven.

---

# Global Overflow Pool

Overflow Experience is stored inside a single account-wide pool.

Every maxed Mastery contributes to this shared reserve.

Example:

Claymore Lv100

↓

+ Overflow XP

Bow Lv100

↓

+ Overflow XP

Fire Staff Lv100

↓

+ Overflow XP

↓

Global Overflow Pool

This pool is permanent.

---

# Overflow Usage

Overflow Experience may be spent to accelerate other Masteries.

Requirements:

- Target Mastery must already be unlocked.
- Silver payment is required.
- Overflow Transfer Rate applies.
- Overflow cannot raise a Mastery beyond the configured Overflow Cap.

Overflow transfers are processed instantly in Version 1.

The system architecture must support future progressive training modes.

---

# Overflow Restrictions

Overflow Experience cannot be invested into:

- locked Masteries
- undiscovered equipment
- Masteries above the configured Overflow Cap

This ensures players must still actively play an equipment type before completely mastering it.

---

# Overflow Economy

Overflow transfers consume:

- Overflow Experience
- Silver

Transfer costs increase progressively based on the target Mastery progression.

The transfer efficiency is intentionally below 100%.

All values are defined through data tables.

No balance values are hardcoded.

---

# Data Driven Design

No weapon-family-specific IP logic is hardcoded.

Weapon-family membership is resolved from authored weapon content.

Mastery contribution rates are centralized balance constants consumed by runtime and UI.

---

# Data Tables

## MasteryTable

Defines every available Mastery.

Fields include:

- Mastery ID
- Equipment ID
- Category
- Max Level
- Experience Curve
- Reward Table ID

---

## MasteryRewardTable

Defines milestone rewards.

Fields include:

- Mastery Level
- Reward Type
- Reward Value

---

## MasteryExperienceTable

Defines experience required per level.

Fields include:

- Level
- Required Experience

---

## EquipmentTierUnlockTable

Defines equipment tier unlock requirements.

Fields include:

- Equipment Tier
- Required Mastery Level

---

## OverflowSettingsTable

Defines Overflow generation.

Fields include:

- Overflow Conversion Rate
- Overflow Enabled
- Overflow Maximum Storage (optional)

---

## OverflowTransferTable

Defines Overflow redistribution.

Fields include:

- Transfer Rate
- Overflow Cap
- Silver Cost Formula
- Cost Scaling Curve

---

## OverflowTransferCostTable

Defines Silver costs.

Fields include:

- Target Mastery Level
- Silver Cost
- Additional Cost Modifiers
