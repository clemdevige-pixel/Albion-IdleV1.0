# 30. PROGRESS SCALING SYSTEM

## Purpose

The Progress Scaling System defines how game difficulty, rewards, and long-term progression evolve after completing the initial world.

The objective is to create an infinitely replayable progression loop without introducing permanent power creep or invalidating previously acquired equipment.

This system is entirely data-driven.

---

# Design Goals

The system must:

- Support infinite progression.
- Preserve equipment value.
- Avoid vertical gear inflation.
- Maintain a healthy long-term economy.
- Keep every biome relevant.
- Allow continuous balancing through Data Tables only.

---

# Core Philosophy

Progression does NOT come from stronger equipment tiers.

Progression comes from:

- Weapon Masteries
- Equipment optimization
- Enchantments
- Economy
- Better resource acquisition
- More efficient farming

New Cycles improve the value of existing content instead of replacing it.

---

---

# Cycle Structure

Each Cycle contains:

- Every unlocked biome
- Every Zone Segment
- Every Encounter
- Every Biome Boss

No content is skipped.

---

# Cycle Unlocking

A Cycle is unlocked after defeating the final boss of the final biome.

Example:

Hell Boss Defeated

↓

Cycle 2 Available

The next Cycle never starts automatically.

The player chooses when to activate it.

---

# Player Choice

After unlocking a new Cycle, the player may continue farming the previous one indefinitely.

Reasons include:

- Farming Silver
- Completing Masteries
- Crafting equipment
- Gathering resources
- Preparing enchantments
- Building economy

The player starts the next Cycle manually.

---

# Infinite Progression

There is no maximum Cycle.

Examples:

Cycle 1

Cycle 5

Cycle 20

Cycle 100

Cycle 500

All progression formulas are designed to support unlimited values.

- Health
- Damage
- Armor
- Magic Resistance
- Attack Speed
- Movement Speed
- Experience Value
- Silver Value

These values never change directly.

Rewards are not tied directly to difficulty.

Instead, rewards use their own scaling system.

This allows independent balancing.

# Resource Philosophy

Cycles never introduce new resources.

Resources are fixed.

Cycles only improve:

- Quantity
- Drop Rates
- Rare Resource Chance
- Resource Quality
- Silver
- Mastery XP

New resources only appear through:

- New Biomes
- New Expansions
- New Gameplay Systems

---

# Economy Stability

Because resources never become obsolete:

Old Biomes remain useful.

Players continue gathering early resources even in high Cycles.

This naturally supports:

- Crafting
- Trading
- Marketplace balance

---

# Equipment Philosophy

Equipment never scales with Cycles.

A Tier 8 weapon always has the same base statistics.

Cycle progression never creates:

Tier 9

Tier 10

Tier 11

or equivalent gear inflation.

---

# Long-Term Vertical Progression

Endgame progression comes from optimization instead of replacing equipment.

Main progression sources include:

- Weapon Masteries
- Item Quality
- Enchantments
- Build Optimization
- Economy

---

# Late Game Enchantments

Very Late Game progression focuses primarily on enchantments.

Example:

↓

T8.2

↓

Enchantments become the main source of vertical power progression after obtaining endgame equipment.

The architecture must support future enchantments on:

- Weapons
- Armor
- Helmets
- Boots
- Off-Hand equipment

Activation is entirely data-driven.

---

# Content Philosophy

Cycles do not create new content.

They increase the value of existing content.

New content comes only from:

- New Biomes
- New Weapons
- New Enemy Families
- New Mechanics
- Expansions

This guarantees long-term maintainability.

---

# Summary

The Progress Scaling System is built around infinite replayability without equipment inflation.

Difficulty increases through independent scaling layers.

Rewards evolve independently from combat difficulty.

Every biome remains economically relevant thanks to exclusive resources.

Equipment never becomes obsolete through new Cycles.

Long-term player power comes from mastery, optimization, and enchantments rather than endlessly stronger gear.

The entire system is fully data-driven, deterministic, modular, and designed for years of future expansion.
