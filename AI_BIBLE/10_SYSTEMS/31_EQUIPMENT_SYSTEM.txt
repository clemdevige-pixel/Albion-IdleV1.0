# 31. EQUIPMENT SYSTEM

## Purpose

The Equipment System defines how players equip gear, how equipment contributes to combat, and how every equipment category interacts with progression, Masteries, abilities, enchantments, and the economy.

The system is entirely data-driven and designed to support future expansion without requiring architectural changes.

---

# Design Goals

The Equipment System must:

- Be simple to understand.
- Allow deep build customization.
- Support hybrid builds.
- Avoid unnecessary restrictions.
- Preserve long-term equipment value.
- Integrate naturally with Masteries.
- Support future equipment categories.

---

# Equipment Philosophy

Equipment is one of the three primary progression pillars.

Player power comes from:

- Equipment
- Weapon Masteries
- Economy

Equipment never becomes obsolete because of new Cycles.

Instead, players optimize existing equipment through:

- Better quality
- Better enchantments
- Better build combinations
- Higher Masteries

---

# Equipment Slots (V1)

The player has six equipment slots.

| Slot | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Head | Helmet |
| Chest | Armor |
| Boots | Boots |
| Weapon | Main weapon |
| Off-Hand | Shield, Torch, Book, Orb, etc. |
| Cape | Passive equipment |

---

# Future Slots

The architecture already supports additional slots.

These are disabled during V1.

Future slots include:

- Mount
- Bag
- Food
- Potion

These can be enabled through configuration without redesigning the Equipment System.

---

# Equipment Categories

Equipment belongs to one of the following categories.

- Weapon
- Off-Hand
- Helmet
- Chest Armor
- Boots
- Cape

Every equipment item belongs to exactly one category.

---

# Equipment Families

Armor pieces belong to one of three families.

- Plate
- Leather
- Cloth

Weapons, Off-Hands, and Capes do not belong to armor families.

---

# Armor Family Identity

Armor families define the defensive profile of equipment.

They do not define abilities.

---

## Plate

Identity:

- Highest Armor
- Highest Magic Resistance
- Highest Survivability
- Lowest Offensive Potential

Primary role:

Tank

---

## Leather

Identity:

- Balanced Defense
- Balanced Damage
- Higher Mobility

Primary role:

Hybrid

---

## Cloth

Identity:

- Lowest Defense
- Highest Offensive Potential

Primary role:

Damage Dealer

---

# Equipment Abilities

Equipment abilities are NOT determined by armor family.

Instead, every equipment item defines its own abilities.

Example:

Plate Helmet A

Ability:

Emergency Barrier

Plate Helmet B

Ability:

Counter Strike

Although both belong to Plate, they provide completely different gameplay.

This greatly increases build diversity.

---

# Hybrid Builds

Players may freely combine armor families.

Examples:

Plate Head

Leather Chest

Cloth Boots

or

Cloth Head

Plate Chest

Leather Boots

All combinations are valid.

No restrictions exist.

---

# Armor Set Bonuses

Although hybrid builds are allowed, specialization is rewarded.

Equipping multiple pieces from the same family grants passive bonuses.

Possible thresholds:

- Two Pieces
- Three Pieces

The exact bonuses are fully data-driven.

# Weapon Independence

Weapons never restrict armor selection.

Every weapon may be combined with every armor family.

Examples:

Sword + Cloth

Sword + Plate

Bow + Leather

Staff + Plate

Crossbow + Cloth

All builds are valid.

# Weapon Types

Weapons belong to one of two categories.

- One-Handed
- Two-Handed

---

# One-Handed Weapons

Occupy only the Weapon slot.

The Off-Hand slot remains available.

Examples:

Sword

Axe

Dagger

Spear

Mace

One-Hand Staff

---

# Two-Handed Weapons

Occupy both:

Weapon

+

Off-Hand

The player cannot equip an Off-Hand while using a Two-Handed weapon.

Examples:

Great Axe

Great Hammer

Bow

Crossbow

Halberd

Great Staff

---

# Build Trade-Off

One-Handed Builds

Advantages

- Off-Hand available
- More customization
- More build combinations

Disadvantages

Weapon power is balanced assuming an Off-Hand.

---

Two-Handed Builds

Advantages

- Strong standalone identity
- Simpler equipment setup

Disadvantages

No Off-Hand customization.

Neither category is intended to be universally stronger.

---

# Off-Hand Equipment

Off-Hand items enhance existing builds.

Examples include:

- Shield
- Torch
- Spell Book
- Orb
- Totem
- Future equipment

Every Off-Hand provides:

- Statistics
- Optional passive effects

No active abilities are provided by Off-Hand items in V1.

---

# Cape Philosophy

Capes are gameplay items rather than stat sticks.

Their primary value comes from a unique passive effect.

Statistics remain secondary.

---

# Cape Components

Each cape contains:

- Base Statistics
- PassiveEffectID

The PassiveEffectID defines its gameplay behavior.

# Equipment Statistics

Equipment may contribute any combination of stats.

Examples:

- Maximum Health
- Armor
- Magic Resistance
- Physical Damage
- Magical Damage
- Attack Speed
- Ability Power
- Movement Speed
- Cooldown Reduction
- Gathering Speed
- Resource Yield

The Equipment System never hardcodes supported statistics.

---

# Equipment Quality

Equipment quality is handled by the Item Quality System.

The Equipment System simply reads the item's quality modifier.

---

# Enchantments

Equipment enchantments are handled by the Enchantment System.

The Equipment System only applies their resulting stat modifiers.

---

# Mastery Requirements

Weapon Mastery determines which weapon tiers can be equipped.

Examples:

Mastery Level 1

Unlocks Tier 3

Mastery Level 20

Unlocks Tier 4

...

Armor Masteries are not included in V1.

The architecture supports them in future versions.

---

# Equipment Persistence

Equipment remains equipped until:

- Replaced
- Removed
- Sold
- Destroyed (future mechanics only)

No automatic equipment swapping exists.

---

# Trading

No equipment is account-bound.

Every equipment item can always be:

- Bought
- Sold
- Traded
- Stored
- Reused

There are no:

- Bind on Pickup
- Bind on Equip
- Soulbound
- Account Bound

This supports a fully player-driven economy.

---

# Durability

Equipment durability is managed by the Durability System.

Durability loss depends on the danger level of the current zone rather than being globally fixed.

Examples:

- Safe Zones: no or minimal durability loss.
- Yellow Zones: low durability loss.
- Red Zones: moderate durability loss.
- Black Zones: high durability loss.

The Equipment System only tracks current durability values and delegates durability calculations to the dedicated Durability System.

# Summary

The Equipment System provides a modular foundation for all player builds.

Players freely combine weapons, armor families, Off-Hand items, and capes to create unique playstyles.

Armor families define defensive identities while equipment items define abilities.

One-Handed and Two-Handed weapons offer different strategic choices without restricting build diversity.

Equipment remains permanently valuable because progression comes from optimization, Masteries, quality, and enchantments rather than ever-increasing gear tiers.

The entire system is fully data-driven, future-proof, and designed to support years of content expansion.
