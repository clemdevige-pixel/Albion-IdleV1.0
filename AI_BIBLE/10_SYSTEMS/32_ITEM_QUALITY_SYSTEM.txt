# 32. ITEM QUALITY SYSTEM

## Purpose

The Item Quality System defines the manufacturing and finishing quality of every equipment item in the game.

Quality is completely independent from:

- Tier
- Enchantments
- Weapon Mastery
- Future Affixes

It represents how well an item was crafted or generated.

---

# Design Goals

The system must:

- Increase long-term item value.
- Reward excellent crafting.
- Support a player-driven economy.
- Keep high-quality items rare.
- Remain completely data-driven.
- Never replace Tier progression.

---

# Core Philosophy

Quality is a permanent property of an item.

It represents craftsmanship rather than progression.

A higher-quality item is a better version of the same item, not a different item.

Example:

T8 Sword

↓

Normal

Good

Outstanding

Excellent

Masterpiece

All five are still the same Tier 8 Sword.

---

# Quality Levels

The game contains five quality levels.

| Quality | Order |
|----------|------:|
| Normal | 1 |
| Good | 2 |
| Outstanding | 3 |
| Excellent | 4 |
| Masterpiece | 5 |

Additional quality levels are not planned.

---

# Independence

Quality is calculated independently from every other progression system.

Final Item Power is composed of multiple independent layers.

Example:

Base Tier

+

Quality Modifier

+

Enchantment Modifier

+

Future Modifiers

Each layer is calculated separately.

---

# Permanent Property

Quality is immutable.

Once an item has been generated, its quality never changes.

Quality cannot be:

- Upgraded
- Downgraded
- Re-rolled
- Transferred
- Recrafted onto an existing item

Example:

T8.4 Good

will always remain

T8.4 Good.

---

# Item Identity

The complete identity of an equipment item is determined by:

- Item Type
- Tier
- Quality
- Enchantment

Example:

T8.4 Masterpiece Sword

is a completely different market item than

T8.4 Good Sword.

---

# What Quality Affects

Quality influences the entire lifecycle of an item.

---

## Combat

Quality increases combat effectiveness.

Possible examples:

- Item Power
- Base Statistics
- Offensive Stats
- Defensive Stats

The exact formulas are defined elsewhere.

---

## Economy

Quality also affects economic value.

Examples include:

- Craft Value
- Market Value
- Vendor Value
- Repair Cost
- Future Recycling Value

Higher quality naturally becomes more valuable.

---

## Progression

Future systems may reference quality.

Examples:

- Advanced Crafting
- Legendary Recipes
- Special Quests
- Future Upgrade Systems

The architecture already supports this.

---

# Quality Generation

Every generated equipment item receives exactly one quality.

Generation occurs only once.

Possible generation sources include:

- Loot
- Crafting
- Event Rewards
- Future Systems

After generation, quality becomes permanent.

---

# Separate Generation Tables

Loot and Crafting do not use identical probability tables.

Each system owns its own balancing.

---

# Loot Quality Philosophy

Loot prioritizes accessibility.

Players frequently obtain usable equipment.

Very high qualities remain rare.

The purpose of loot is to:

- Reward combat
- Create excitement
- Feed the economy

---

# Craft Quality Philosophy

Crafting prioritizes excellence.

Specialized crafters naturally produce higher-quality equipment more consistently.

Crafting is intended to become the primary source of:

- Excellent equipment
- Masterpiece equipment

This gives crafting a unique identity within the economy.

---

# Quality Calculation

The quality calculation follows independent layers.

Base Quality Chances

↓

Loot or Craft Table

↓

Generation Modifiers

↓

Final Quality Roll

The final roll produces exactly one quality level.

# Repair Costs

Repair costs scale with quality.

Higher-quality equipment requires greater investment to maintain.

Actual scaling values are defined in Data Tables.

---

# Relationship with Enchantments

Quality and Enchantments are completely independent.

Example:

T8 Normal

T8 Masterpiece

T8.4 Good

T8.4 Masterpiece

All combinations are valid.

---

# Relationship with Tier

Quality never changes an item's Tier.

Examples:

T5 Masterpiece

is still

Tier 5.

T8 Normal

is still

Tier 8.

Tier progression and Quality progression remain independent.

---

# Relationship with Mastery

Weapon Mastery does not influence an item's existing quality.

However, Craft Mastery may improve the probability of creating higher-quality equipment.

# Future Extensions

The architecture supports:

- New crafting bonuses
- Seasonal quality bonuses
- Special crafting stations
- World modifiers
- Event modifiers
- Future professions
- Guild crafting bonuses
- Prestige crafting systems

No redesign is required.

---

# Summary

The Item Quality System represents craftsmanship rather than progression.

Quality is permanent, immutable, and completely independent from Tier and Enchantments.

Loot and Crafting use separate quality generation tables, allowing crafting to become the primary source of exceptional equipment.

Quality influences combat performance, economic value, repair costs, and future gameplay systems while remaining entirely data-driven and fully extensible.
