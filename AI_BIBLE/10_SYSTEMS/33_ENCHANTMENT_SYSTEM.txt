# 33. ENCHANTMENT SYSTEM

Status: AUTHORITATIVE LIVE BALANCE CONTRACT
Updated: 2026-08-25

## Purpose

The Enchantment System upgrades an existing equipment instance while preserving its base Tier. Normal enchantment uses `.0 -> .1 -> .2 -> .3`. Eligible weapons may then use the distinct `.3 -> .4` Awakening transition.

Enchantments are deterministic, permanent, item-specific, sequential and data-driven.

---

## 1. Validated live curve

Enchantment display IP and enchantment combat power are intentionally DECOUPLED.

| Level | Display IP bonus | Combat stat multiplier | Incremental Tier shard cost |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `.0` | +0 | x1.00 | - |
| `.1` | +25 | x1.12 | 10 |
| `.2` | +50 | x1.26 | 30 |
| `.3` | +75 | x1.42 | 60 |
| `.4` | +100 | x1.42 base | 100, weapon Awakening only |

The `.4` base multiplier currently preserves `.3` combat stats. Awakening traits are the separate long-term power layer; Awakening must never reduce base equipment stats.

Display IP is a progression/UX marker. It is not the formula that determines enchantment combat power.

Mastery remains independent: mastery-derived bonus IP still uses the shared bonus-IP stat conversion and is NOT replaced or reduced by the enchantment curve.

A fully enchanted `.4` item displays at most +100 IP over its base Tier so it does not display above the base IP of the next Tier.

Authoritative authored sources:
- `packages/data/src/config/enchantment-balance.ts` for IP/stat scaling;
- `packages/data/src/config/enchantment-recipe-balance.ts` for shard, Silver, tier/category and material-cost balance.

Gameplay calculation adapters remain under `packages/gameplay/src/equipment/` and must consume these authored values rather than redefine them.

## 2. Tier eligibility and sequence

- Enchantment starts at T4 and supports T4-T8.
- Normal equipment may be authored through `.3`.
- Only explicitly eligible weapons may reach `.4`.
- Off-hands do not Awaken.
- Levels cannot be skipped.
- Enchanting never changes base Tier, quality or item identity.
- Every valid enchantment succeeds; there is no failure/downgrade/destruction RNG.

### 2A. Academy discovery gate

The Enchantment service is discovered through gameplay rather than being exposed from the start.

Canonical loop:

1. Enchantment shards may drop normally before the service is unlocked.
2. The first owned shard makes the T4 Academy Research `Étude des enchantements` available.
3. Before discovery, that Research is not displayed in the Academy Research list.
4. Research cost: 5,000 Silver, no materials.
5. Research duration: 30 minutes.
6. Completion unlocks `enchantment:service`.
7. Before completion, the Merchant does not display the `Enchanter` service.
8. After completion, the Merchant displays `Enchanter` and the existing EnchantmentService becomes accessible normally.

Shard tooltips before Research completion explicitly point the player toward the Academy. Shard drop rates and the existing shard economy are unchanged by this gate.

The Academy unlock controls access to the service only. It does NOT alter enchantment recipes, shard requirements, Silver costs, refined-material costs, power curves or eligibility.

## 3. Tier shards and package costs

One combat-earned shard resource exists per Tier: T4, T5, T6, T7, T8. Shards cannot be converted between Tiers.

Normal full-cost incremental shard curve:
- `.0 -> .1`: 10
- `.1 -> .2`: 30
- `.2 -> .3`: 60

Cumulative `.0 -> .3`: 100 shards for one full-cost item.

Weapon package rule through `.3`:
- 2H weapon = 100%
- 1H weapon = 50%
- off-hand = 50%
- 1H + off-hand = exactly one 2H package

This 50/50 equivalence applies to shards, refined-material quantities and Silver for normal `.1-.3` enchantment.

Full-set benchmark convention = five full-cost equivalents:
- weapon package = 1
- helmet = 1
- chest = 1
- boots = 1
- cape = 1

Therefore full-set shard requirements are derived from the authoritative curve:
- `.0 -> .1`: 50
- `.1 -> .2`: 150
- `.2 -> .3`: 300

Do not duplicate these costs in benchmark-local balance tables.

---

## 4. Refined materials and Silver

Enchanting consumes:
- matching Tier shards;
- refined materials derived from the item's BASE CRAFT recipe;
- Silver.

There is no parallel hand-authored enchantment material recipe. Enchantment material requirements scale from the base craft materials.

Validated refined-material quantity multipliers:
- `.1`: x1
- `.2`: x2
- `.3`: x4
- `.4`: x8

This preserves the global economy rule that base crafting remains the source of material identity.

Silver scales through authoritative Tier/category/enchantment data and remains an economic sink, not the sole progression limiter.

---

## 5. Awakening `.3 -> .4`

`.4` is the Awakening transition of the weapon itself, not another normal package enchantment.

Rules:
- shard cost: 100 matching Tier shards;
- eligible 1H and 2H weapons both pay the full weapon Awakening cost;
- 1H receives no 50% discount on `.4`;
- off-hand is not Awakened;
- refined-material multiplier: x8;
- base `.4` combat-stat multiplier currently remains x1.42;
- post-acquisition Attunement/traits/Strain are defined in `20_AWAKENED_WEAPON_SYSTEM.txt`.

## 6. Shard acquisition and open-world pacing

Shard Tier comes from the content being farmed:
- Blue/T4 -> T4 shards
- Yellow/T5 -> T5 shards
- Orange/T6 -> T6 shards
- Red/T7 -> T7 shards
- Black/T8 -> T8 shards

Current open-world authored economy uses:
- base expected shard/kill: 0.0165
- depth bonus per segment: 0.015
- elite multiplier: x1.20
- boss multiplier: x1.35
- authored per-band depth/zone weights in `packages/data/src/config/economy-balance.ts`.

A universal fixed drop chance per monster is forbidden. Shards/hour must be validated using live TTK and encounter cadence so trivial monsters do not become the universal optimum.

Weapon profiles are allowed to produce different shards/hour. Access and progression pacing are protected; identical throughput is not required.

IMPORTANT ACTIVITY TRADEOFF:
- when the HERO gathers, the hero is not fighting and therefore stops personal shard farming;
- workers may gather in parallel;
- global economy projections must account for this combat-vs-active-gather tradeoff rather than treating every resource stream as fully parallel.

## 7. Tier bridge contract

Previous-tier `.3` gear must retain viable autonomous access to the first shard farm of the next world band:
- T4.3 -> T5 / Yellow
- T5.3 -> T6 / Orange
- T6.3 -> T7 / Red
- T7.3 -> T8 / Black

All four bridges were revalidated after the 2026-08-20 enchantment-power recalibration.

Different weapons may reach different segments and shards/hour. The bridge protects ACCESS, not equality.

---

## 8. Item state and restrictions

Enchantments belong to the exact equipment instance. They cannot be transferred to another item.

Enchanting is forbidden while the combat segment is running or stop-requested. It is allowed only after combat is authoritatively stopped/paused and all other transaction requirements pass.

A transaction validates at minimum:
- instance exists;
- level transition is valid;
- item is eligible;
- enough Tier shards;
- enough refined materials;
- enough Silver;
- combat restriction satisfied.

The result is deterministic and must commit as one coherent transaction.

---

## 9. Presentation

Tier halo/frame represents BASE TIER and never changes from enchanting.

`Tn.x` label color represents enchantment:
- `.0`: white/grey
- `.1`: green
- `.2`: blue
- `.3`: purple
- `.4`: yellow

Recommended world IP is also an UX/progression marker. Because enchantment power is decoupled from IP, recommended IP must be authored from validated world walls/bridges rather than inferred from enchantment stat multipliers.
