# 34. REPAIR SYSTEM

## Purpose

The Repair System restores the durability of damaged equipment.

Its purpose is to:

- Extend the lifespan of valuable equipment.
- Create a permanent Silver sink.
- Encourage players to return to cities or their personal island.
- Introduce maintenance costs based on gameplay activity.
- Preserve the long-term health of the player-driven economy.

The Repair System does not create new equipment.

It only restores existing equipment.

---

# Design Goals

The Repair System must:

- Be simple to understand.
- Require strategic planning before dangerous expeditions.
- Support the durability economy.
- Never invalidate crafting.
- Remove Silver from the economy.
- Be fully data-driven.
- Integrate with every equipment category.

---

# Core Philosophy

Equipment is not permanent by default.

Players are expected to maintain their gear throughout its lifetime.

Repairing equipment is significantly cheaper than replacing it, making maintenance the preferred option.

However, players who ignore durability entirely eventually lose their equipment forever.

---

# Repair Locations

Equipment may only be repaired in approved locations.

V1 supports two repair locations:

- Cities
- Player Island

Repairs cannot be performed:

- Inside combat
- Inside exploration zones
- During encounters
- From the inventory
- Through consumable repair kits

---

# Repair Buildings

Repairs are performed through a dedicated building.

Examples include:

- Repair Station
- Blacksmith
- Forge
- Workshop

The visual identity is left to the Art Direction.

The gameplay remains identical.

---

# Repair Flow

The standard repair process is:

Player arrives at repair location

↓

Interact with Repair Building

↓

Open Repair Interface

↓

Select damaged equipment

↓

Preview repair cost

↓

Confirm repair

↓

Silver is consumed

↓

Durability is fully restored

---

# Full Restoration

Repairs always restore equipment to maximum durability.

Example:

Current Durability:

38 / 100

↓

Repair

↓

100 / 100

Partial repairs are not supported in V1.

---

# Unlimited Repairs

Equipment may be repaired an unlimited number of times.

There is:

- No repair limit.
- No permanent wear.
- No reduction of maximum durability.
- No degradation of repair efficiency.

The item remains identical after every repair.

---

# Permanent Maximum Durability

Maximum durability never decreases.

Example:

New Sword

100 / 100

↓

Many repairs later

100 / 100

↓

Many months later

100 / 100

Only the current durability changes.

---

# Repair Cost

Every repair consumes Silver.

Repair costs may depend on:

- Equipment Tier
- Equipment Category
- Current Durability
- Maximum Durability
- Future balancing modifiers

Exact formulas remain data-driven.

---

# Repair Cost Philosophy

Repairing equipment should always be cheaper than replacing it.

This encourages players to:

- Maintain valuable equipment.
- Preserve high-quality items.
- Invest in long-term progression.

Replacing an item should only become necessary when it has been destroyed.

---

# Repair Validation

Before a repair begins, the system validates:

- The item exists.
- The item is repairable.
- The item is damaged.
- The player owns enough Silver.
- The player is interacting with a valid repair station.
- The item is not locked.
- The player is not in combat.

If validation fails, no Silver is consumed.

---

# Repair Result

After a successful repair:

- Current durability becomes maximum durability.
- Equipment statistics remain unchanged.
- Tier remains unchanged.
- Quality remains unchanged.
- Enchantment remains unchanged.
- Mastery requirements remain unchanged.

Repairs only affect durability.

---

# Item Identity

Repairing equipment never changes the item's identity.

The following properties remain identical:

- ItemInstanceID
- Item Definition
- Tier
- Quality
- Enchantment
- Affixes (future)
- Ownership

Only Current Durability changes.

---

# Durability and Destruction

Equipment reaching zero durability is permanently destroyed.

Destroyed equipment cannot be repaired.

Once destroyed:

- The item is removed.
- The ItemInstanceID is deleted.
- No repair option exists.

The player must obtain a replacement.

---

# Economic Role

Repairs create a continuous Silver sink while delaying equipment destruction.

This creates three possible outcomes:

## Good Maintenance

Repair frequently.

↓

Equipment survives.

↓

Silver leaves the economy.

---

## Risky Maintenance

Delay repairs.

↓

Lower maintenance costs.

↓

Higher risk.

---

## No Maintenance

Ignore durability.

↓

Equipment reaches 0 durability.

↓

Permanent destruction.

↓

New equipment must be crafted or purchased.

---

# Relationship with Crafting

Repairs never replace crafting.

Eventually, neglected equipment disappears from the economy.

This ensures continuous demand for:

- Gathering
- Refining
- Crafting
- Marketplace trading

---

# Relationship with Enchantments

Repairs preserve enchantments.

Example:

T8.4 Sword

↓

Repair

↓

Still T8.4 Sword

Enchantments are never removed or downgraded by repairing.

---

# Relationship with Quality

Repairs preserve Quality.

Example:

Masterpiece Armor

↓

Repair

↓

Masterpiece Armor

Quality never changes.

---

# Relationship with Tier

Repairs never modify Tier.

Example:

T6 Armor

↓

Repair

↓

T6 Armor

---

# Relationship with Stats

Repairs never modify:

- Damage
- Armor
- Health
- Resistances
- Ability Scaling
- Passive Effects

Repairs restore durability only.

---

# Relationship with Market

Equipment can be repaired before being sold.

Players may choose between:

- Selling damaged equipment for less.
- Paying repair costs first.
- Selling fully repaired equipment.

Market pricing is determined by players.

---

# Interface

The Repair Interface should display:

- Selected item
- Current durability
- Maximum durability
- Repair cost
- Player Silver
- Confirmation button

---

# Bulk Repair

The interface should support:

Repair Selected Item

and

Repair All Damaged Equipment

Both operations use the same validation rules.

---

# Confirmation Flow

1. Player selects equipment.
2. System calculates repair cost.
3. Cost is displayed.
4. Player confirms.
5. Server validates.
6. Silver is consumed.
7. Durability is restored.
8. Inventory refreshes.
9. Save transaction completes.

---

# Atomic Transactions

Repairs are atomic.

Either:

- Silver is consumed.
- Durability is restored.

or

Nothing happens.

Partial operations are forbidden.

---

# Server Authority

Repair calculations are server authoritative.

The client may only:

- Display preview.
- Send repair request.

The client cannot:

- Set durability.
- Modify costs.
- Complete repairs.

---

# Save Data

Each repair transaction may store:

- TransactionID
- ItemInstanceID
- PreviousDurability
- NewDurability
- SilverCost
- Timestamp

---

# Validation Rules

Repair fails when:

- Item does not exist.
- Item is destroyed.
- Item is already at full durability.
- Player lacks Silver.
- Player is outside a repair location.
- Item is locked.
- Player is in combat.

---

The architecture supports future additions such as:

- Guild repair stations

- Repair achievements

# Explicitly Excluded from V1

V1 does not include:

- Repair anywhere
- Repair Kits

- Durability restoration consumables
- Maximum durability loss
- Limited repair count
- Repair materials
- Instant field repairs
- Automatic repairs

---

# System Relationships

The Repair System interacts with:

- Inventory System
- Equipment System
- Durability System
- Currency System
- Economy System
- Marketplace System
- Save System
- UI System

---

# Summary

The Repair System allows players to fully restore damaged equipment by paying Silver at dedicated repair stations located in cities or on their personal island.

Equipment may be repaired an unlimited number of times without reducing its maximum durability or altering its Tier, Quality, or Enchantment.

Repairing equipment is always preferable to replacing it, but neglecting durability entirely results in permanent item destruction once durability reaches zero.

The system provides a long-term Silver sink while supporting the player-driven economy and preserving the value of crafted equipment.
