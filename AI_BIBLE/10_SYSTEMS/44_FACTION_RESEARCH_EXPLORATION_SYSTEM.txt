# 1. PURPOSE

The Academy is the permanent functional-unlock system for secondary progression.

V1 owns:

- Research;
- Silver Expeditions;
- one generic Faction Expedition track;
- Dungeon discovery;
- the global Dungeon Relic discovery chain;
- common Faction Rune acquisition;
- Research completion recaps.

The system is data-driven. Shared runtime mechanics must not branch specifically for Keeper / Heretic / Undead / Morgana.

Research unlocks capabilities/content. Faction Mastery remains faction-specific progression/yield and is never a second unlock tree.

---

# 2. ACADEMY

The Academy is a UNIQUE Player Island building.

Construction:

- available at Island Level 2;
- 500 Silver;
- 12 T3 Planks;
- 8 T3 Bars;
- 4 T3 Cloth.

Operational tier mapping:

- Academy level 1 -> T4 Research;
- level 2 -> T5;
- level 3 -> T6;
- level 4 -> T7;
- level 5 -> T8.

Upgrade curve:

| Upgrade | Island level required | Silver | Refined materials |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| T4 -> T5 | 3 | 5,000 | 5 of each T4 refined family |
| T5 -> T6 | 4 | 18,000 | 9 of each T5 refined family |
| T6 -> T7 | 5 | 50,000 | 14 of each T6 refined family |
| T7 -> T8 | 6 | 85,000 | 20 of each T7 refined family |

The four refined families are Planks, Bars, Leather and Cloth.

Research capacity:

- Researches are independent and may run simultaneously;
- there is NO global Research slot;
- the same Research may not be started twice;
- every started Research owns its own timer and consumes its own authored cost atomically.

Research progresses online and through authoritative Background Progression.

Research above the current Academy tier must NOT be displayed.

---

# 3. RESEARCH LIFECYCLE

1. A Research definition becomes visible when its Academy tier is reachable.
2. Authored prerequisites determine locked/available state.
3. Starting consumes the authored cost atomically.
4. Each Research receives an independent timer.
5. Multiple Researches progress from the same authoritative elapsed time.
6. Completion commits authored unlock IDs automatically.
7. Completion displays the generic Research recap.

No manual reward collection is used.

# 5. EXPEDITIONS

Expeditions are passive parallel activities and continue offline through Background Progression.

V1 families:

1. Generic Silver Expedition.
2. Generic Faction Expedition.

Requirements:

- Silver Expedition -> matching Cartography tier unlock;
- Faction Expedition -> matching Archaeology tier unlock;
- no Relic prerequisite for Faction Expeditions.

Slots:

- before any Expedition Research unlock: 0;
- T4/T5: 1;
- Cartography III / T6 unlocks slot 2;
- T6-T8: maximum 2.

Two Expeditions of the same type may not run simultaneously.

Durations:

- 2 h;
- 6 h;
- 12 h.

Reward/hour remains equivalent across durations before whole-item rounding unless explicitly authored otherwise.

## 5.1 Silver Expedition

Silver/hour is authored independently.

Faction Mastery does not modify Silver Expedition output.

## 5.2 Faction Expedition

The Faction Expedition is the primary passive/predictable source of common Faction Runes.

The structural migration currently preserves the temporary live baseline of 1 matching-tier Rune/hour until the dedicated multi-reward pass is implemented.

Validated target direction discussed for the next reward pass:

| Tier | Target Rune/h |
|---|---:|
| T4 | 8 |
| T5 | 14 |
| T6 | 25 |
| T7 | 40 |
| T8 | 60 |

Future secondary rewards may include:

- Silver;
- enchantment shards;
- Dungeon key fragments;
- complete Dungeon keys.

Exact quantities/probabilities for those secondary rewards remain pending and must be authored through reward tables, not ad-hoc runtime cases.

The generic Faction Expedition is not modified by an individual Faction Mastery because it carries no Keeper/Heretic/Undead/Morgana identity.

# 7. RUNE CONSUMPTION

## 7.1 Artifact weapons

Artifact weapon craft requires:

- authored refined base resources;
- exactly 1 matching faction Dungeon artifact;
- matching-tier common Faction Runes.

No predecessor weapon is consumed.

| Tier | Rune craft cost |
|---|---:|
| T4 | 5 |
| T5 | 10 |
| T6 | 20 |
| T7 | 35 |
| T8 | 55 |

## 7.2 Faction capes

All Keeper / Heretic / Undead / Morgana capes consume the same matching-tier common Faction Rune.

| Tier | Cape Rune craft cost |
|---|---:|
| T4 | 3 |
| T5 | 4 |
| T6 | 5 |
| T7 | 6 |
| T8 | 8 |

Enchanting reuses authored craft materials through the generic enchantment system. Rune demand therefore scales beyond initial craft cost.

# 8. GLOBAL DUNGEON RELIC

Dungeon discovery uses ONE global Relic.

Canonical IDs:

- Relic definition: `relic_dungeon`;
- inventory item: `item_relic_dungeon`.

Acquisition:

- Blue band;
- Frostpeak Mountain / `zone_mountain_t4`;
- Segment 10 (runtime index 9);
- final boss encounter.

Acquisition records authoritative faction-kill baselines.

The Relic then requires NEW kills after acquisition:

- 50 Keeper;
- 50 Heretic;
- 50 Undead;
- 50 Morgana.

State before Research analysis:

`UNOBTAINED -> BROKEN -> CHARGED`

Historical kills before acquisition never count.

# 9. DUNGEON DISCOVERY RESEARCH CHAIN

## 9.1 Analyse de la Relique

Tier T4.

Prerequisites:

- Academy T4;
- global Relic CHARGED.

Cost: 0 Silver / no materials.
Duration: 10 minutes.

Completion:

- marks the Relic EXAMINED;
- unlocks `Localisation des Sanctuaires`;
- displays generic Research completion recap.

## 9.2 Localisation des Sanctuaires

Tier T4.

Prerequisites:

- Academy T4;
- Analyse de la Relique completed.

Cost: 10,000 Silver / no materials.
Duration: 1 hour.

Completion permanently unlocks:

- World > Donjons;
- Dungeon key-fragment world drops;
- complete Dungeon-key world drops;
- common Faction Rune world-drop channel on eligible faction monsters.

Canonical Rune capability:

- `faction_rune:world_drop`.

# 10. FACTION RUNE WORLD DROP

Before `Localisation des Sanctuaires`:

- world combat cannot grant common Faction Runes through this channel.

After completion:

- Keeper / Heretic / Undead / Morgana monsters may grant the matching-tier common Faction Rune;
- each successful proc grants exactly 1 Rune;
- Animal/generic monsters are not eligible;
- Blue T3 zones remain at 0% and do not produce T4 Runes.

The world channel is an active complement to Expedition income.

Tester-baseline active-farm target at Faction Mastery 0 on the deepest currently farmable reference:

| Tier | Target Rune/h |
|---|---:|
| T4 | ~6 |
| T5 | ~10 |
| T6 | ~18 |
| T7 | ~30 |
| T8 | ~45 |

## 10.1 Displayed rate anchors

Rates are FINAL per-kill probabilities, not hidden runtime multipliers.

Each zone authors a final S1 and S10 rate; intermediate segments interpolate between those two final values.

Summary tester anchors:

| Tier | Entry rate | Mid reference | Deep rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| T4 | 0.50% | 0.85% | 1.15% |
| T5 | 0.75% | 1.50% | 2.10% |
| T6 | 1.80% | 2.90% | 4.30% |
| T7 | 3.00% | 5.60% | 8.30% |
| T8 | 4.75% | 7.80% | 13.60% |

Full zone-by-zone authoring is canonical in:

`AI_BIBLE/05_BALANCE/2026-08-24_FACTION_RUNE_WORLD_DROP_BASELINE.md`

and runtime data:

`apps/client/src/data/factionRuneWorldDropContentCatalog.ts`.

## 10.2 Bestiary

The Bestiary consumes the same authored Rune probability source as live world combat.

Rune entries appear in the existing monster `Table de loot` alongside other loot.

If a monster occurs at several depths, the Bestiary shows the real minimum-maximum base-rate range for the selected band.

The tooltip labels this value `Taux de base` because Faction Mastery can increase expected Rune yield.

## 10.3 Tester status

These rates are a V1 tester baseline and may be retuned after real player feedback.

Numeric retuning must remain data-only and require no runtime architecture changes.

# 11. DUNGEON VISIBILITY AND DROP GATES

Before `Localisation des Sanctuaires`:

- World > Donjons is hidden;
- Dungeon definitions are not enterable;
- world key fragments do not drop;
- world complete keys do not drop;
- world common Faction Runes do not drop through the Rune channel.

After completion, those channels become available subject to their authored rules.

Dungeon keys remain tier-based, faction-independent and consumed by existing Dungeon entry rules.

---

# 12. FACTION MASTERY

Faction Mastery remains faction-specific yield/progression only.

Current yield rule:

- +0.5% per level;
- max 100;
- +50% at level 100.

It may affect matching-faction authored channels such as:

- faction combat Silver/Fame yield;
- authored faction loot channels;
- common Faction Rune world-drop expected yield when killing that faction.

For Rune world drops, mastery increases expected yield on top of the displayed base rate.

It does NOT gate Research, Expeditions, Relic progression, Dungeon discovery, recipes or world progression.

Generic Silver/Faction Expeditions are unaffected by an individual Faction Mastery.

---

# 13. PERSISTENCE / MIGRATION

Research persistence stores a collection of active Research timers.

Research snapshot V1 with one `activeResearch` remains load-compatible.

Runtime save V5 migrates all legacy faction-specific Rune IDs to the matching common tiered Rune ID:

- Keeper Tn -> Faction Rune Tn;
- Heretic Tn -> Faction Rune Tn;
- Undead Tn -> Faction Rune Tn;
- Morgana Tn -> Faction Rune Tn.

Quantities are preserved.

Legacy faction Relics remain non-convertible to the global Dungeon Relic.

# 14. DATA-DRIVEN RULES

Required:

- Research definitions own tier/cost/duration/prerequisites/unlock IDs;
- ResearchService owns parallel active timers;
- Expedition definitions consume Research unlock IDs;
- one generic Faction Expedition definition exists per tier;
- Faction Rune identity is common by tier;
- artifact weapons and faction capes consume common tiered Runes;
- Rune world drops consume the Research capability;
- Rune world numeric rates live in one authored data table;
- Bestiary reads the same Rune probability source as runtime combat;
- future Expedition secondary rewards use authored reward tables.

Forbidden:

- faction-specific runtime branches for shared mechanics;
- duplicate unlock booleans outside owning systems;
- second Research scheduler;
- UI reconstruction of gameplay authority;
- reintroducing separate faction Rune currencies;
- hidden Rune depth multipliers that make displayed rates differ from runtime rates;
- world Rune drops before the Research capability;
- manual per-monster Rune rates duplicated outside the progression table.
