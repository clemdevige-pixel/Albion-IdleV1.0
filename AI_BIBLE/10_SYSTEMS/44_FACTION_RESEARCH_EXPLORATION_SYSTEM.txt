# 1. PURPOSE

This document records the validated design and implementation contract for a secondary progression layer built around:

- Academy / Research;
- the existing Bestiary as monster knowledge surface;
- deterministic faction Relics;
- faction Expeditions;
- a generic Silver Expedition;
- faction crafting resources (Runes);
- faction Capes;
- Faction Mastery;
- the existing World > Achievements surface.

The system must create meaningful parallel progression while the hero continues normal Albion Idle activities, without adding arbitrary resources that only lengthen existing loops.

The complete T4-T8 loop is authored for Keeper, Heretic, Undead and Morgana through shared data-driven systems. Remaining OPEN work is limited to explicitly listed tuning/presentation/future-content points.

# 2. SYSTEM OWNERSHIP

## 2.1 Research

Research is the authority for permanent functional unlocks introduced by this feature.

It may unlock:

- Expeditions;
- Relic examination capability;
- faction Expedition families;
- faction dungeon families.

Faction Mastery must never become a second unlock tree.

## 2.2 Faction Mastery

Faction Mastery owns faction-wide yield only.

It affects the matching faction's:

- Expedition Rune yield;
- loot/drop yield or drop-rate channel;
- Silver earned from faction content;
- Fame earned from faction content.

It does NOT gate:

- Research;
- Relics;
- Expeditions;
- dungeons;
- recipes;
- world progression.

It does NOT affect the generic Silver Expedition.

## 2.3 Bestiary

Do NOT create a second Codex module.

The existing World Bestiary remains the player-facing monster knowledge surface.

Existing references:

- `apps/client/src/ui/world/components/WorldBestiaryView.tsx`
- `apps/client/src/ui/world/worldModels.ts`

Its responsibility is monster information, including:

- discovered / undiscovered state;
- kill count per monster;
- faction identity;
- combat/loot information already present.

The Bestiary does NOT own or display faction Relic state or Relic charge progression.

Relic state belongs to the Relic item in the player Inventory.

Implementation status:

- monster discovery / kill counters: IMPLEMENTED;
- Relic projection removed from Bestiary by design;
- final UX polish/composition: still open.

## 2.4 Achievements

Do NOT create a second Achievement system.

Reuse World > Achievements as the presentation surface for major milestones.

Existing reference:

- `apps/client/src/ui/world/components/WorldAchievementsView.tsx`

Achievements are presentation/completion objectives, not an unlock authority.

The validated milestone definitions are authoritative in:

- `AI_BIBLE/10_SYSTEMS/44A_FACTION_ACHIEVEMENT_MILESTONES.md`

Implementation status:

- 65 validated milestones authored as data-driven definitions;
- progress resolved from owning systems rather than duplicated Achievement state;
- World > Achievements projection implemented;
- final visual polish remains open.

# 3. ACADEMY / RESEARCH

## 3.1 Placement and unlock

The Academy is a UNIQUE Player Island utility building.

Validated unlock timing:

- available at Island Level 2;
- therefore after the early T3 loop / Dark Swamp progression according to the existing Island contract.

## 3.2 Construction and upgrades

Construction uses only the existing economy.

Validated Academy construction cost:

- 500 Silver;
- 12 T3 Planks;
- 8 T3 Bars;
- 4 T3 Cloth.

Do NOT create a Research Point / Science currency.

The initial constructed Academy is operationally T4.

Validated upgrade curve:

| Upgrade | Island level required | Silver | Refined materials |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| T4 -> T5 | 3 | 5,000 | 5 of each T4 refined family |
| T5 -> T6 | 4 | 18,000 | 9 of each T5 refined family |
| T6 -> T7 | 5 | 50,000 | 14 of each T6 refined family |
| T7 -> T8 | 6 | 85,000 | 20 of each T7 refined family |

The four refined families are Planks, Bars, Leather and Cloth at the matching source tier.

Academy progression remains separate from production-building progression. Its building levels map to Research tiers as follows:

- Academy level 1 / Island level 2 -> T4;
- Academy level 2 / Island level 3 -> T5;
- Academy level 3 / Island level 4 -> T6;
- Academy level 4 / Island level 5 -> T7;
- Academy level 5 / Island level 6 -> T8.

## 3.3 Research slots

Validated V1:

- 1 active Research slot.

No second Research slot by default.

## 3.4 Research lifecycle

1. Player selects an available Research.
2. Costs/prerequisites are validated.
3. Costs are consumed.
4. Timer starts.
5. Research progresses online and offline.
6. Completion grants the permanent authored unlock automatically.

No manual reward collection.

Relic examination is a separate Academy action and does NOT consume the Research slot.

## 3.5 Offline model

Research integrates with the existing Background Progression model:

- authoritative elapsed time;
- no client-clock authority;
- no tick-by-tick offline simulation;
- same authored duration online/offline.

Reference:

- `AI_BIBLE/10_SYSTEMS/39_BACKGROUND_PROGRESSION_SYSTEM.txt`

## 3.6 Academy tier behavior

- Academy T4 exposes T4 Research;
- Academy T5 exposes T5 Research;
- Academy T6 exposes T6 Research;
- Academy T7 exposes T7 Research;
- Academy T8 exposes T8 Research.

Increasing Academy tier does NOT grant a generic Research-speed bonus.

Its tier controls which tier-associated Research entries can appear.

# 4. SOCLE RESEARCH

## 4.1 Cartography I — T4

Validated tuning:

- cost: 5,000 Silver;
- duration: 30 minutes;
- no material cost.

Effects:

- unlocks the Expedition system;
- unlocks the generic Silver Expedition T4;
- enables T4 faction Expedition definitions whose faction prerequisites are satisfied.

## 4.2 Archaeology I — T4

Validated tuning:

- cost: 2,500 Silver;
- duration: 20 minutes;
- no material cost.

Effects:

- enables the Academy to examine a CHARGED faction Relic;
- examination converts the Relic state from CHARGED to EXAMINED;
- EXAMINED Relics satisfy the faction Research prerequisite.

Archaeology is acquired once.

Archaeology does NOT control the boss drop or the 50-kill charging phase: a Relic may be obtained and charged before Archaeology is completed, but it cannot be examined until Archaeology I is complete.

Do NOT create Archaeology II/III/IV/V unless a future mechanic genuinely requires it.

## 4.3 Cartography progression

Cartography is sequential: each tier after I requires the previous Cartography unlock in addition to the matching Academy tier.

| Research | Tier | Silver | Duration | Effect |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Cartography I | T4 | 5,000 | 30 min | T4 Expeditions |
| Cartography II | T5 | 15,000 | 1 h | T5 Expeditions |
| Cartography III | T6 | 40,000 | 2 h | T6 Expeditions + second simultaneous Expedition slot |
| Cartography IV | T7 | 70,000 | 3 h | T7 Expeditions |
| Cartography V | T8 | 110,000 | 4 h | T8 Expeditions |

No material cost is added to these Research entries.

No extra route/event mechanics in V1.

# 5. FACTION RELICS

## 5.1 Definition

A faction Relic is a unique deterministic progression item tied to one faction.

It is NOT:

- equipment;
- currency;
- a passive-stat item;
- an RNG drop grind;
- a replacement for Mastery;
- a fragment checklist or separate quest system.

There is exactly ONE Relic per faction across T4-T8.

Initial factions:

- Keeper;
- Heretic;
- Undead;
- Morgana.

The runtime is entirely data-driven through `RelicDefinition`. No faction-specific runtime branch is allowed.

## 5.2 Validated lifecycle

A Relic follows exactly this state machine:

`UNOBTAINED -> BROKEN -> CHARGED -> EXAMINED`

Validated flow:

1. The authored faction boss drops the BROKEN Relic once, guaranteed.
2. Acquisition starts its charge at `0 / 50` faction kills.
3. Only matching-faction kills performed AFTER acquisition count toward charge.
4. At `50 / 50`, the Relic becomes CHARGED.
5. A CHARGED Relic remains CHARGED until the player explicitly sends it to the Academy.
6. If Archaeology I is complete, the Academy may examine it.
7. Examination changes the Relic to EXAMINED and satisfies the faction Research prerequisite.

No automatic examination is allowed.

The boss kill that grants the Relic establishes the acquisition baseline and does not count as charge progress.

Kills performed before the Relic is acquired must never contribute to its 50-kill charge.

## 5.3 Boss acquisition — authored data

Each faction Relic is guaranteed by its authored S10 faction boss:

| Faction | Source boss |
|---|---|
| Keeper | Ancient Keeper |
| Heretic | Heretic Madmen |
| Undead | Lich |
| Morgana | High Priestess |

The boss-to-Relic relationship is authored data, never inferred from names or implemented with faction-specific conditionals.

The Relic drops only once.

Acquisition is committed only when the unique Relic object can actually be granted to the player Inventory. The domain must not create a hidden acquired state without the corresponding Inventory object.

## 5.4 Inventory ownership and presentation

The Relic is a real unique player Inventory item.

Rules:

- quantity 1;
- non-stackable unique entry;
- category: Special;
- cannot be used as a consumable;
- cannot be transferred to the Bank;
- no equipment slot;
- no passive stats.

The Relic tooltip is the authoritative player-facing state display.

It shows:

- BROKEN: charge progress `X / 50`;
- CHARGED: ready for Academy examination;
- EXAMINED: examination completed.

The Bestiary does not display this state.

No final Relic visual asset is invented until separately validated.

## 5.5 Charge rules

Validated charge requirement:

- 50 kills of the matching faction after Relic acquisition.

The charge uses the existing authoritative faction kill stream; it does not create a duplicate combat simulation or independent kill source.

The stored acquisition baseline makes charge equivalent to:

`current_faction_kills - faction_kills_at_relic_acquisition`

clamped to `[0, 50]`.

Historical kills before acquisition are explicitly excluded.

The charge phase is normal active gameplay; it has no separate offline-combat simulation.

## 5.6 Academy examination

A CHARGED Relic is NOT automatically examined.

Before examination, faction Research remains gated and the Academy indicates that it is waiting for the Relic or that the Relic is ready to examine, according to its state.

When both conditions are true:

- Relic state = CHARGED;
- Archaeology I completed;

the Academy exposes an explicit action:

`Send Relic to Academy`

This action:

- has no Silver cost;
- has no material cost;
- has no timer;
- does not consume the Research slot;
- does not automatically start either contextual faction Research;
- changes the Relic state to EXAMINED.

After examination, the two contextual faction Research entries may become available according to their remaining prerequisites.

## 5.7 Persistence and migration

Relic acquisition baseline and EXAMINED state are persistent.

Current persistence contract is versioned.

Migration rule for saves produced by older Relic implementations:

- previous fragment/reconstructed or auto-examined states are NOT allowed to import historical kills as current charge;
- migrated Relics restart as BROKEN at `0 / 50` using the faction kill count at migration/load as their new acquisition baseline;
- a migrated Relic therefore requires 50 new matching-faction kills before it can become CHARGED;
- examination remains an explicit Academy action under the current contract.

This deliberately favors correctness of the validated new loop over preserving obsolete automatic reconstruction semantics.

# 6. CONTEXTUAL FACTION RESEARCH

An EXAMINED faction Relic satisfies the Relic prerequisite for TWO independent Research entries.

The same validated tuning applies to Keeper, Heretic, Undead and Morgana.

Before examination, the Academy displays the Relic gate rather than pretending the Research itself is available.

## 6.1 Faction Expedition Research

For each faction:

`Étude des <Faction>`

- prerequisite: matching faction Relic EXAMINED;
- Academy tier: T4;
- Silver cost: 5,000;
- duration: 30 minutes;
- reward: permanently unlocks that faction Expedition family.

Cartography determines which tier of that family is available.

There is no tiered Study II/III/IV/V chain.

## 6.2 Faction Dungeon Research

For each faction:

`Localisation des Sanctuaires <Faction>`

- prerequisite: matching faction Relic EXAMINED;
- Academy tier: T4;
- Silver cost: 10,000;
- duration: 1 hour;
- reward: permanently unlocks that faction dungeon family as selectable content.

All four dungeon families are authored through the same generic access contract.

## 6.3 Dungeon keys

Faction Dungeon Research does NOT modify dungeon keys.

Existing key behavior remains authoritative:

- tier-based;
- faction-independent;
- current per-run entry behavior unchanged.

Research controls permanent CONTENT ACCESS only.

Reference:

- `apps/client/src/data/dungeonKeyContentCatalog.ts`

## 6.4 Manuscripts

Manuscripts are removed from the validated V1 scope.

Do not implement them unless a future design gives them a distinct concrete purpose.

# 7. EXPEDITIONS — V1 CONTRACT

## 7.1 Core identity

Expeditions are passive parallel activities.

They can run while the hero continues:

- combat;
- gathering;
- refining;
- crafting;
- other normal gameplay.

Expeditions continue while the player is offline.

## 7.2 Families

Validated V1 contains:

1. Generic Silver Expedition
2. Faction Expeditions

### Generic Silver Expedition

- faction-independent;
- requires Cartography;
- no Relic requirement;
- unaffected by Faction Mastery;
- intended as a comfortable parallel Silver source, especially for Awake/Attunement Silver pressure;
- active combat remains the stronger direct Silver/hour source.

### Faction Expedition

- faction-specific;
- requires the faction Relic to have been EXAMINED;
- requires the faction Expedition Research;
- tier availability controlled by Cartography;
- produces the matching faction Rune;
- affected by matching Faction Mastery yield.

All four faction families are authored for T4-T8: Keeper, Heretic, Undead and Morgana.

## 7.3 Slots

Validated V1:

- T4: 1 slot;
- T5: 1 slot;
- T6: 2 slots;
- T7: 2 slots;
- T8: 2 slots.

Second slot unlock: Cartography III / T6.

## 7.4 Same-type concurrency rule

Validated:

> Two Expeditions of the same type may NOT run simultaneously.

Examples with 2 slots:

Allowed:

- Silver + Keeper;
- Keeper + Morgana;
- Keeper + Heretic.

Forbidden:

- Silver + Silver;
- Keeper + Keeper.

This rule preserves allocation choice and prevents the second slot from simply doubling the strongest selected stream.

## 7.5 Durations

Validated V1 formats:

- 2 hours;
- 6 hours;
- 12 hours.

Reward/hour is equivalent between these durations before whole-Rune rounding.

Duration is a convenience choice, not an optimization trap.

Faction Rune rewards are rounded to the nearest whole Rune after Faction Mastery is applied (`Math.round`, exact `.5` rounds upward).

## 7.6 Launch cost

Validated V1:

- no launch cost.

Do NOT add Provisions, Expedition currency, Silver fees or arbitrary material costs.

## 7.7 Completion

On completion:

- rewards are credited automatically;
- no manual Collect action;
- Expedition completion is resolved from authoritative elapsed time online/offline;
- completed Expedition frees its slot through the runtime completion contract.

## 7.8 Completion recap popup

A completion recap popup is REQUIRED.

The popup is informational only; rewards are already credited.

Faction Expedition recap shows at minimum:

- Expedition name;
- duration;
- base Rune reward;
- Faction Mastery bonus;
- final Rune total.

Silver Expedition recap shows at minimum:

- Expedition name;
- duration;
- final Silver credited.

If multiple Expeditions complete together (including offline resolution):

- show ONE grouped recap;
- do NOT chain multiple blocking popups.

Implementation status: IMPLEMENTED for both online and offline completion batches through the same recap pipeline.

# 9. FACTION RUNES

## 9.1 Role

A faction Rune is the durable faction crafting resource produced by that faction's Expedition.

Rune purpose is intentionally limited to meaningful faction content.

Validated sinks:

1. faction Cape crafting/enchantment;
2. future faction item/weapon crafting.

Runes are player Inventory items, not Production Storage materials.

Do NOT inject Runes into ordinary generic gear recipes just to increase grind.

## 9.2 Future faction item recipe contract

A future major faction item/weapon recipe combines three acquisition channels:

- existing Gather/Refining materials;
- faction Artifact from the faction dungeon;
- faction Rune from the faction Expedition.

Conceptual contract:

`Faction Item Tx = Refined Materials + Faction Artifact Tx + Faction Rune Tx`

Artifacts remain dungeon-sourced; Runes remain Expedition-sourced.

## 9.3 Rune tiering and generation

Runes are tiered by faction/content tier.

Validated base faction Expedition generation:

- 1 Rune/hour at Mastery level 0.

Therefore:

- 2h = 2 Runes;
- 6h = 6 Runes;
- 12h = 12 Runes.

Faction Mastery modifies the resulting yield, then the final quantity is rounded to the nearest whole Rune.

# 10. FACTION CAPES

## 10.1 General role

Faction Capes are craftable faction preparation equipment from T4 onward.

They combine:

- normal defensive stats;
- a faction-dungeon-only passive;
- faction Runes as an Expedition-driven progression sink.

They have NO HP stat in the validated design.

## 10.2 Defensive stats — all four factions

Keeper, Heretic, Undead and Morgana use the same validated defensive stat curve:

| Tier | Armor | Magic Resistance |
|---|---:|---:|
| T4 | 3 | 5 |
| T5 | 4 | 7 |
| T6 | 6 | 10 |
| T7 | 9 | 14 |
| T8 | 13 | 20 |

Faction identity changes the matching-faction dungeon resistance target, not these base defensive stats.

## 10.3 Base recipes — all four factions

No predecessor equipment is consumed.

| Tier | Cloth | Leather | Matching faction Runes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| T4 | 3 | 1 | 3 |
| T5 | 4 | 2 | 4 |
| T6 | 5 | 2 | 5 |
| T7 | 6 | 3 | 6 |
| T8 | 8 | 4 | 8 |

All materials are the matching target tier.

Each Cape uses only its own faction Rune family.

Normal equipment crafting remains predecessor-free according to the current live crafting contract.

## 10.4 Rune costs by tier

Validated base Rune quantities:

- T4: 3;
- T5: 4;
- T6: 5;
- T7: 6;
- T8: 8.

## 10.5 Enchantment integration

Faction Runes are normal base craft materials for the Cape and therefore reuse the EXISTING generic enchantment material multiplier.

No faction-specific enchantment formula.

Current generic material multipliers:

- `.0 -> .1`: x1;
- `.1 -> .2`: x2;
- `.2 -> .3`: x4.

Capes are capped at `.3`.

`.4` remains weapon Awakening only.

Example T4 Rune path:

- craft Cape: 3 Runes;
- `.0 -> .1`: 3 Runes;
- `.1 -> .2`: 6 Runes;
- `.2 -> .3`: 12 Runes;
- total from nothing to T4.3: 24 Runes = 24 hours of base Expedition time at Mastery 0.

This must reuse the single authoritative enchantment recipe scaling pipeline.

Reference:

- `packages/gameplay/src/equipment/enchantment-recipes.ts`

## 10.6 Dungeon-only faction passive

Each faction Cape has a passive that reduces damage received ONLY inside dungeons of its matching faction.

Validated reduction curve:

| Cape Tier | Damage reduction in matching faction dungeon |
|---|---:|
| T4 | 6% |
| T5 | 8% |
| T6 | 11% |
| T7 | 14% |
| T8 | 18% |

Rules:

- no effect in open world;
- no effect in another faction's dungeon;
- enchantment `.1/.2/.3` does NOT increase this passive percentage;
- the Cape tier itself determines the passive value.

## 10.7 Tier compatibility rule

A faction Cape may be used in its own dungeon tier and all HIGHER dungeon tiers, but never in a lower tier.

Examples:

- Keeper Cape T4: active in Keeper dungeons T4-T8;
- Heretic Cape T6: active in Heretic dungeons T6-T8;
- Morgana Cape T8: active only in Morgana dungeon T8.

This prevents high-tier Capes from trivializing old dungeon tiers while preserving long-term usefulness of lower-tier Capes.

Implementation resolves this through generic authored faction/tier data, not faction-specific runtime code.

# 11. FACTION MASTERY

## 11.1 Structure

Each supported faction has its own Mastery:

- Keeper Mastery;
- Heretic Mastery;
- Undead Mastery;
- Morgana Mastery.

Maximum validated level:

- 100.

## 11.2 XP source

Validated core rule:

> 1 raw faction Fame = 1 Faction Mastery XP.

Only raw/base faction Fame is used.

Faction Fame bonuses must NOT feed back into Mastery XP generation.

This prevents the Mastery Fame bonus from accelerating its own progression.

## 11.3 XP curve

Validated cumulative curve:

`XP_required_for_level_L = 1,500 × L²`

Reference cumulative values:

- Level 10: 150,000 XP;
- Level 25: 937,500 XP;
- Level 50: 3,750,000 XP;
- Level 75: 8,437,500 XP;
- Level 100: 15,000,000 XP.

This is intentionally a long-term progression layer calibrated against current Fame/hour scale.

## 11.4 Yield bonus

Validated:

- +0.5% faction yield per Mastery level;
- Level 100 cap = +50%.

Affected matching-faction channels:

- faction Rune Expedition yield;
- faction drops/drop rates;
- faction Silver;
- faction Fame.

For integer combat rewards (Silver and Fame), apply the matching-faction Mastery bonus to the raw/base reward, then round to the nearest whole unit with `Math.round` (exact `.5` rounds upward), matching the Rune rounding rule.

Faction Mastery XP itself still consumes raw/base faction Fame before this yield bonus is applied, so the Fame bonus never accelerates its own Mastery progression.

Generic Silver Expedition is excluded.

## 11.5 Offline progression rule

There is NO general offline Faction Mastery progression in the current design.

Do not simulate combat/Fame/Mastery while offline.

The important distinction is:

- Expeditions continue offline;
- Research continues offline;
- Faction Mastery does not independently progress offline.

An Expedition completed while offline still resolves its Expedition rewards normally, but no separate offline-combat Mastery simulation is introduced.

# 14. DATA-DRIVEN IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

Implementation must be definition-driven.

Do not create faction-specific runtime conditionals such as:

`if faction === "Keeper" ...`

outside generic authored-data resolution.

Expected definition families include:

- `ResearchDefinition`;
- `FactionDefinition`;
- `FactionMasteryDefinition`;
- `RelicDefinition`;
- `ExpeditionDefinition`;
- faction Cape/passive definitions;
- `AchievementDefinition`.

Required single-source-of-truth rules:

- Expedition family/type determines same-type concurrency validation;
- Expedition definitions author duration/reward/tier/faction;
- Relic definitions author faction, source boss, Inventory item and charge kill count;
- Relic charge is derived from the authoritative faction kill stream after the stored acquisition baseline;
- Relic state is owned by `RelicService`, while physical presence is mirrored by the existing Inventory system;
- Bestiary never becomes a second Relic state owner;
- Academy examination is an explicit generic action over the Relic requirement already authored on faction Research;
- faction definitions reference existing monster/zone/dungeon IDs;
- Cape recipes use the existing Crafting system;
- Cape enchantments reuse the existing generic enchantment material scaling;
- dungeon passive uses generic faction + minimum-tier matching;
- no duplicated Keeper-specific runtime logic.

Existing catalogs remain authoritative for existing content until deliberately refactored.
