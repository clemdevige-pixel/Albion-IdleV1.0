# 44_FACTION_RESEARCH_EXPLORATION_SYSTEM

Status: VALIDATED / IMPLEMENTED CONTRACT
Authority: canonical Academy / Research / Expedition / Dungeon discovery contract
Last update: 2026-08-22

---

# 1. PURPOSE

The Academy is the permanent functional-unlock system for secondary progression.

V1 currently owns:

- Research;
- Silver Expeditions;
- faction Expeditions;
- Dungeon discovery;
- the global Dungeon Relic discovery chain;
- Research completion recaps.

The system must remain data-driven. Runtime code must not branch specifically for Keeper / Heretic / Undead / Morgana.

Research unlocks capabilities/content. Faction Mastery improves faction yield only and is never a second unlock tree.

---

# 2. ACADEMY

The Academy is a UNIQUE Player Island building.

Construction:

- available at Island Level 2;
- 500 Silver;
- 12 T3 Planks;
- 8 T3 Bars;
- 4 T3 Cloth.

Operational tier mapping:

- Academy level 1 -> T4 Research;
- level 2 -> T5;
- level 3 -> T6;
- level 4 -> T7;
- level 5 -> T8.

Upgrade curve remains:

| Upgrade | Island level required | Silver | Refined materials |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| T4 -> T5 | 3 | 5,000 | 5 of each T4 refined family |
| T5 -> T6 | 4 | 18,000 | 9 of each T5 refined family |
| T6 -> T7 | 5 | 50,000 | 14 of each T6 refined family |
| T7 -> T8 | 6 | 85,000 | 20 of each T7 refined family |

The four refined families are Planks, Bars, Leather and Cloth.

V1 Research capacity:

- 1 active Research slot.

Research progresses online and through the authoritative Background Progression system.

Research from Academy tiers above the current Academy tier must NOT be displayed to the player.

---

# 3. RESEARCH LIFECYCLE

1. A Research definition becomes visible when its Academy tier is reachable.
2. Its authored prerequisites determine whether it is locked or available.
3. Starting it atomically consumes its authored cost.
4. It occupies the single Research slot for its authored duration.
5. Completion automatically commits its authored unlock IDs.
6. Completion displays a generic Research recap.

No manual reward collection is used.

Every Research completion recap must be data-driven and may show:

- Research completed;
- effect applied;
- newly unlocked content/capabilities.

Unlock-specific popups must not be hand-authored as isolated one-off systems when the generic recap model can represent them.

---

# 4. EXPEDITION RESEARCH TRACKS

Silver Expeditions and faction Expeditions intentionally use two independent Research tracks.

## 4.1 Cartography = Silver Expeditions

Cartography controls only the generic Silver Expedition tier.

| Research | Tier | Silver | Duration | Unlock |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Cartography I | T4 | 5,000 | 30 min | Silver Expedition T4 |
| Cartography II | T5 | 15,000 | 1 h | Silver Expedition T5 |
| Cartography III | T6 | 40,000 | 2 h | Silver Expedition T6 + second Expedition slot |
| Cartography IV | T7 | 70,000 | 3 h | Silver Expedition T7 |
| Cartography V | T8 | 110,000 | 4 h | Silver Expedition T8 |

Cartography II-V require the previous Cartography unlock plus the matching Academy tier.

No material cost.

## 4.2 Archaeology = faction Expeditions

Archaeology controls only faction Expedition tiers.

It uses EXACTLY the same validated cost/duration curve as Cartography.

| Research | Tier | Silver | Duration | Unlock |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Archéologie I | T4 | 5,000 | 30 min | faction Expeditions T4 |
| Archéologie II | T5 | 15,000 | 1 h | faction Expeditions T5 |
| Archéologie III | T6 | 40,000 | 2 h | faction Expeditions T6 |
| Archéologie IV | T7 | 70,000 | 3 h | faction Expeditions T7 |
| Archéologie V | T8 | 110,000 | 4 h | faction Expeditions T8 |

Archéologie II-V require the previous Archaeology unlock plus the matching Academy tier.

No material cost.

Archaeology no longer owns Relic examination.

# 5. EXPEDITIONS

Expeditions are passive parallel activities and continue offline through the Background Progression model.

V1 families:

1. Generic Silver Expedition.
2. Keeper Expedition.
3. Heretic Expedition.
4. Undead Expedition.
5. Morgana Expedition.

Silver Expedition requirement:

- matching Cartography tier unlock.

Faction Expedition requirement:

- matching Archaeology tier unlock.

There is NO Relic prerequisite for faction Expeditions.

Faction Expedition reward:

- matching faction Rune;
- affected by matching Faction Mastery yield.

Generic Silver Expedition:

- unaffected by Faction Mastery.

Slots:

- before an Expedition tier Research: 0 slots;
- T4/T5: 1 slot;
- Cartography III / T6 unlocks slot 2;
- T6-T8: maximum 2 slots.

Same-type concurrency rule remains:

- two Expeditions of the same type may not run simultaneously.

Durations remain:

- 2 h;
- 6 h;
- 12 h.

Reward/hour is equivalent across duration choices before whole-Rune rounding.

Expedition tiers above the current Academy tier must NOT be presented to the player.

# 6. GLOBAL DUNGEON RELIC

Dungeon discovery uses ONE global Relic.

It is not faction-specific equipment and does not unlock faction Expeditions.

Canonical IDs:

- Relic definition: `relic_dungeon`;
- inventory item: `item_relic_dungeon`.

## 6.1 Acquisition

The Relic drops once, guaranteed, from:

- Blue world band;
- Frostpeak Mountain / `zone_mountain_t4`;
- Segment 10 (zero-based runtime segment index 9);
- final boss encounter (Ancient Keeper monster definition).

IMPORTANT:

The Ancient Keeper monster definition is reused in other contexts. Relic acquisition MUST therefore validate the authored world context and segment, not only the monster ID.

No other Ancient Keeper encounter may grant this Relic.

Acquisition is committed only if the unique inventory object can actually be granted.

## 6.2 Charge

Acquisition records the authoritative faction-kill baseline for all four factions.

The Relic then requires NEW kills performed after acquisition:

- 50 Keeper;
- 50 Heretic;
- 50 Undead;
- 50 Morgana.

Total displayed aggregate target: 200 kills.

Each faction objective progresses independently.

Historical kills before Relic acquisition never count.

State flow before Research analysis:

`UNOBTAINED -> BROKEN -> CHARGED`

At 50/50 in all four objectives, the Relic remains CHARGED until its analysis Research completes.

The Relic is a unique Special inventory item and cannot be banked/consumed/equipped.

# 7. DUNGEON DISCOVERY RESEARCH CHAIN

The Relic does not use a manual "send to Academy" button anymore.

Its analysis is a real Research and therefore uses the standard Research slot/lifecycle/recap.

## 7.1 Analyse de la Relique

Tier:

- T4.

Prerequisites:

- Academy T4;
- global Dungeon Relic state = CHARGED.

Cost:

- 0 Silver;
- no materials.

Duration:

- 10 minutes.

Completion:

- marks the global Relic as EXAMINED;
- permanently unlocks the next Research: `Localisation des Sanctuaires`;
- displays the generic Research completion recap.

The 200-kill Relic charge is the gameplay cost of this analysis stage; no extra currency tax is added.

## 7.2 Localisation des Sanctuaires

Tier:

- T4.

Prerequisites:

- Academy T4;
- Analyse de la Relique completed.

Cost:

- 10,000 Silver;
- no materials.

Duration:

- 1 hour.

Completion permanently unlocks the Dungeon system.

Its generic completion recap announces:

- World > Donjons tab unlocked;
- Dungeon key fragment drops unlocked;
- complete Dungeon key drops unlocked.

---

# 8. DUNGEON VISIBILITY AND KEY DROP GATE

Before `Localisation des Sanctuaires` is complete:

- the World > Donjons tab is NOT displayed;
- faction Dungeon definitions are not enterable;
- world combat cannot grant Dungeon key fragments;
- world combat cannot grant complete Dungeon keys.

After completion:

- the World > Donjons tab appears;
- authored Dungeon content becomes accessible subject to its normal tier/key/run rules;
- Dungeon key fragments and complete keys enter the normal world-combat loot channel.

Dungeon keys remain:

- tier-based;
- faction-independent;
- consumed according to the existing Dungeon entry contract.

The Research gate activates the loot channel; it does not replace key-tier progression or Dungeon entry rules.

---

# 9. DUNGEON CONTENT OWNERSHIP

There is one global Research unlock for Dungeon discovery.

Do NOT create separate permanent `dungeon_family:<faction>` Research gates for Keeper / Heretic / Undead / Morgana.

Faction identity remains authored on Dungeon definitions and may still drive:

- encounter composition;
- artifacts;
- faction cape effects;
- Faction Mastery yield/progression;
- Achievements.

The Academy answers "have Dungeons been discovered?", not "has every faction been separately rediscovered?".

---

# 10. FACTION MASTERY

Faction Mastery remains yield/progression only.

It may affect matching-faction:

- Expedition Rune yield;
- faction combat Silver/Fame yield channels;
- authored faction loot channels.

It does NOT gate:

- Research;
- Expeditions;
- Relic acquisition/charge;
- Dungeon discovery;
- recipes;
- world progression.

Generic Silver Expeditions are unaffected by Faction Mastery.

# 11. BESTIARY AND ACHIEVEMENTS

Bestiary remains the monster-knowledge surface.

It owns/displays monster discovery and kill knowledge, not the Dungeon Relic progression UI.

The old four faction-Relic Achievement milestones are obsolete because faction Relics no longer exist in the canonical loop. They are removed rather than silently repointed to the global Dungeon Relic.

Achievements remain feedback only and never own functional unlocks.

# 12. PERSISTENCE / MIGRATION

Research, Expeditions, faction knowledge and Relic state remain persistent providers.

Global Dungeon Relic persistence stores:

- acquisition state;
- per-faction kill baselines;
- EXAMINED state.

Legacy saves with old faction Relic IDs are NOT implicitly converted into the new global Dungeon Relic.

Reason:

- the source and charge contract changed fundamentally;
- silently mapping historical faction Relics would bypass the new Mountain S10 + 50x4 progression contract.

The new global Relic must therefore be discovered through its canonical source unless a future explicit migration decision says otherwise.

After loading Research state, derived Research effects (including completed Relic analysis) must be reconciled from authoritative completed unlocks rather than duplicated save flags.

# 13. DATA-DRIVEN RULES

Required architecture:

- Research definitions own tier/cost/duration/prerequisites/unlock IDs;
- Expedition definitions consume Research unlock IDs;
- Relic definitions own contextual source + charge objectives;
- Dungeon navigation consumes one global Research unlock;
- combat reward code asks whether the Dungeon key loot channel is unlocked;
- Research recap presentation consumes authored Research presentation metadata.

Forbidden:

- Keeper/Heretic/Undead/Morgana runtime branches for shared mechanics;
- duplicate unlock booleans saved outside owning systems;
- UI reconstructing gameplay authority independently from Research/Relic data;
- automatic Relic acquisition based only on monster name/ID when context is required.
