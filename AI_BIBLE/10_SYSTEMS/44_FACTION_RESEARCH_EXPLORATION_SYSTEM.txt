Status: IMPLEMENTATION IN PROGRESS / KEEPER T4 PILOT PLAYABLE
Authority: validated design + implementation contract
Last update: 2026-08-22

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document records the validated design and implementation contract for a secondary progression layer built around:

- Academy / Research;
- the existing Bestiary as Codex surface;
- deterministic faction Relic quests;
- faction Expeditions;
- a generic Silver Expedition;
- faction crafting resources (Runes);
- faction Capes;
- Faction Mastery;
- the existing World > Achievements surface.

The system must create meaningful parallel progression while the hero continues normal Albion Idle activities, without adding arbitrary resources that only lengthen existing loops.

The Keeper T4 pilot foundations are implemented. Later-tier/faction content remains data-authoring work where explicitly marked OPEN.

---

# 2. SYSTEM OWNERSHIP

## 2.1 Research

Research is the authority for permanent functional unlocks introduced by this feature.

It may unlock:

- Expeditions;
- Relic reconstruction capability;
- faction Expedition families;
- faction dungeon families.

Faction Mastery must never become a second unlock tree.

## 2.2 Faction Mastery

Faction Mastery owns faction-wide yield only.

It affects the matching faction's:

- Expedition Rune yield;
- loot/drop yield or drop-rate channel;
- Silver earned from faction content;
- Fame earned from faction content.

It does NOT gate:

- Research;
- Relics;
- Expeditions;
- dungeons;
- recipes;
- world progression.

It does NOT affect the generic Silver Expedition.

## 2.3 Bestiary / Codex

Do NOT create a second Codex module.

The existing World Bestiary is the player-facing faction knowledge surface.

Existing references:

- `apps/client/src/ui/world/components/WorldBestiaryView.tsx`
- `apps/client/src/ui/world/worldModels.ts`

It may surface:

- discovered / undiscovered state;
- kill counts;
- elite/boss victories;
- faction identity;
- combat/loot information already present;
- Relic quest progression;
- Faction Mastery progression;
- related Achievements.

Implementation status:

- faction discovery / kill counters / elite counters: IMPLEMENTED;
- Relic progression/reconstruction projection: IMPLEMENTED;
- final UX polish/composition: still open.

## 2.4 Achievements

Do NOT create a second Achievement system.

Reuse World > Achievements as the presentation surface for major milestones.

Existing reference:

- `apps/client/src/ui/world/components/WorldAchievementsView.tsx`

Achievements are presentation/completion objectives, not an unlock authority.

The validated milestone definitions are authoritative in:

- `AI_BIBLE/10_SYSTEMS/44A_FACTION_ACHIEVEMENT_MILESTONES.md`

Implementation status:

- 65 validated milestones authored as data-driven definitions;
- progress resolved from owning systems rather than duplicated Achievement state;
- World > Achievements projection implemented;
- final visual polish remains open.

# 3. ACADEMY / RESEARCH

## 3.1 Placement and unlock

The Academy is a UNIQUE Player Island utility building.

Validated unlock timing:

- available at Island Level 2;
- therefore after the early T3 loop / Dark Swamp progression according to the existing Island contract.

## 3.2 Construction

Construction uses only the existing economy.

Validated Academy construction cost:

- 500 Silver;
- 12 T3 Planks;
- 8 T3 Bars;
- 4 T3 Cloth.

Do NOT create a Research Point / Science currency.

The initial constructed Academy is operationally T4.

Academy upgrade costs for T5-T8 remain OPEN.

## 3.3 Research slots

Validated V1:

- 1 active Research slot.

No second Research slot by default.

## 3.4 Research lifecycle

1. Player selects an available Research.
2. Costs/prerequisites are validated.
3. Costs are consumed.
4. Timer starts.
5. Research progresses online and offline.
6. Completion grants the permanent authored unlock automatically.

No manual reward collection.

## 3.5 Offline model

Research integrates with the existing Background Progression model:

- authoritative elapsed time;
- no client-clock authority;
- no tick-by-tick offline simulation;
- same authored duration online/offline.

Reference:

- `AI_BIBLE/10_SYSTEMS/39_BACKGROUND_PROGRESSION_SYSTEM.txt`

## 3.6 Academy tier behavior

- Academy T4 exposes T4 Research;
- Academy T5 exposes T5 Research;
- Academy T6 exposes T6 Research;
- Academy T7 exposes T7 Research;
- Academy T8 exposes T8 Research.

Increasing Academy tier does NOT grant a generic Research-speed bonus.

Its tier controls which tier-associated Research entries can appear.

# 4. SOCLE RESEARCH

## 4.1 Cartography I — T4

Validated tuning:

- cost: 5,000 Silver;
- duration: 30 minutes;
- no material cost.

Effects:

- unlocks the Expedition system;
- unlocks the generic Silver Expedition T4;
- enables T4 faction Expedition definitions whose faction prerequisites are satisfied.

## 4.2 Archaeology I — T4

Validated tuning:

- cost: 2,500 Silver;
- duration: 20 minutes;
- no material cost.

Effects:

- enables faction Relic quests;
- enables Relic Fragment tracking;
- enables Relic reconstruction when all objectives are complete.

Archaeology is acquired once.

Do NOT create Archaeology II/III/IV/V unless a future mechanic genuinely requires it.

## 4.3 Cartography progression

- Cartography II / T5: enables T5 Expeditions;
- Cartography III / T6: enables T6 Expeditions + unlocks the second simultaneous Expedition slot;
- Cartography IV / T7: enables T7 Expeditions;
- Cartography V / T8: enables T8 Expeditions.

Exact Silver/material costs and durations for Cartography II-V remain OPEN.

No extra route/event mechanics in V1.

# 5. FACTION RELICS

## 5.1 Definition

A Relic is a permanent faction discovery reconstructed through a deterministic mini-quest.

It is NOT:

- equipment;
- currency;
- a passive-stat item;
- an RNG drop grind;
- a replacement for Mastery.

There is exactly ONE Relic per faction across T4-T8.

Initial factions:

- Keeper;
- Heretic;
- Undead;
- Morgana.

## 5.2 Fragment structure

Validated V1:

- 5 fragments complete one faction Relic;
- fragments come from deterministic objectives;
- once the Relic is reconstructed, fragment generation for that Relic ends permanently.

## 5.3 Universal objective template

All factions use the same runtime structure; only authored data changes.

1. DISCOVERY
   - defeat the two main normal units of the faction.
2. FAMILIARIZATION
   - first faction kill threshold.
3. DEEP STUDY
   - second faction kill threshold.
4. ELITE STUDY
   - authored elite kill threshold.
5. TERRITORY PROGRESSION
   - authored segment milestone in a faction-dominant zone.

No final biome boss is required by default.

The Relic must be obtainable before the end of the relevant chapter so dungeon preparation can begin before chapter completion.

## 5.4 Keeper Relic — validated pilot

Exact Keeper objectives:

1. DISCOVERY
   - defeat 1 Keeper Warrior;
   - defeat 1 Keeper Shaman.
2. FAMILIARIZATION
   - defeat 25 Keepers total.
3. DEEP STUDY
   - defeat 100 Keepers total.
4. ELITE STUDY
   - defeat 3 Keeper Champions.
5. TERRITORY PROGRESSION
   - complete Frostpeak Segment 5.

Completing all five objectives reconstructs the Keeper Relic permanently once Archaeology has unlocked reconstruction.

This is the pilot template to transpose to other factions through data.

## 5.5 Historical progress

Where persistent data allows it, Relic objectives recognize progress completed before Archaeology becomes available.

Current implementation records faction/monster/world progress historically and gates only reconstruction behind Archaeology.

Exact migration/backfill behavior for older save formats remains an implementation design task where historical data did not previously exist.

# 6. CONTEXTUAL FACTION RESEARCH

Reconstructing a faction Relic makes TWO independent Research entries appear simultaneously.

## 6.1 Faction Expedition Research

Keeper pilot:

`Étude des Keeper`

- prerequisite: Keeper Relic reconstructed;
- Silver cost: 5,000;
- duration: 30 minutes;
- reward: permanently unlocks the Keeper Expedition family.

Cartography determines which tier of that family is available.

No `Keeper Study II/III/IV/V`.

## 6.2 Faction Dungeon Research

Keeper pilot:

`Localisation des Sanctuaires Keeper`

- prerequisite: Keeper Relic reconstructed;
- Silver cost: 10,000;
- duration: 1 hour;
- reward: permanently unlocks the Keeper dungeon family as selectable content.

Equivalent independent Research exists for Heretic, Undead and Morgana when their authored content is added.

## 6.3 Dungeon keys

Faction Dungeon Research does NOT modify dungeon keys.

Existing key behavior remains authoritative:

- tier-based;
- faction-independent;
- current per-run entry behavior unchanged.

Research controls permanent CONTENT ACCESS only.

Reference:

- `apps/client/src/data/dungeonKeyContentCatalog.ts`

## 6.4 Manuscripts

Manuscripts are removed from the validated V1 scope.

Do not implement them unless a future design gives them a distinct concrete purpose.

# 7. EXPEDITIONS — V1 CONTRACT

## 7.1 Core identity

Expeditions are passive parallel activities.

They can run while the hero continues:

- combat;
- gathering;
- refining;
- crafting;
- other normal gameplay.

Expeditions continue while the player is offline.

## 7.2 Families

Validated V1 contains:

1. Generic Silver Expedition
2. Faction Expeditions

### Generic Silver Expedition

- faction-independent;
- requires Cartography;
- no Relic requirement;
- unaffected by Faction Mastery;
- intended as a comfortable parallel Silver source, especially for Awake/Attunement Silver pressure;
- active combat remains the stronger direct Silver/hour source.

### Faction Expedition

- faction-specific;
- requires the faction Relic;
- requires the faction Expedition Research;
- tier availability controlled by Cartography;
- produces the matching faction Rune;
- affected by matching Faction Mastery yield.

## 7.3 Slots

Validated V1:

- T4: 1 slot;
- T5: 1 slot;
- T6: 2 slots;
- T7: 2 slots;
- T8: 2 slots.

Second slot unlock: Cartography III / T6.

## 7.4 Same-type concurrency rule

Validated:

> Two Expeditions of the same type may NOT run simultaneously.

Examples with 2 slots:

Allowed:

- Silver + Keeper;
- Keeper + Morgana;
- Keeper + Heretic.

Forbidden:

- Silver + Silver;
- Keeper + Keeper.

This rule preserves allocation choice and prevents the second slot from simply doubling the strongest selected stream.

## 7.5 Durations

Validated V1 formats:

- 2 hours;
- 6 hours;
- 12 hours.

Reward/hour is equivalent between these durations before whole-Rune rounding.

Duration is a convenience choice, not an optimization trap.

Faction Rune rewards are rounded to the nearest whole Rune after Faction Mastery is applied (`Math.round`, exact `.5` rounds upward).

## 7.6 Launch cost

Validated V1:

- no launch cost.

Do NOT add Provisions, Expedition currency, Silver fees or arbitrary material costs.

## 7.7 Completion

On completion:

- rewards are credited automatically;
- no manual Collect action;
- Expedition completion is resolved from authoritative elapsed time online/offline;
- completed Expedition frees its slot through the runtime completion contract.

## 7.8 Completion recap popup

A completion recap popup is REQUIRED.

The popup is informational only; rewards are already credited.

Faction Expedition recap shows at minimum:

- Expedition name;
- duration;
- base Rune reward;
- Faction Mastery bonus;
- final Rune total.

Silver Expedition recap shows at minimum:

- Expedition name;
- duration;
- final Silver credited.

If multiple Expeditions complete together (including offline resolution):

- show ONE grouped recap;
- do NOT chain multiple blocking popups.

Implementation status: IMPLEMENTED for online completion batches; offline resolution must feed the same recap pipeline.

# 9. FACTION RUNES

## 9.1 Role

A faction Rune is the durable faction crafting resource produced by that faction's Expedition.

Rune purpose is intentionally limited to meaningful faction content.

Validated sinks:

1. faction Cape crafting/enchantment;
2. future faction item/weapon crafting.

Runes are player Inventory items, not Production Storage materials.

Do NOT inject Runes into ordinary generic gear recipes just to increase grind.

## 9.2 Future faction item recipe contract

A future major faction item/weapon recipe combines three acquisition channels:

- existing Gather/Refining materials;
- faction Artifact from the faction dungeon;
- faction Rune from the faction Expedition.

Conceptual contract:

`Faction Item Tx = Refined Materials + Faction Artifact Tx + Faction Rune Tx`

Artifacts remain dungeon-sourced; Runes remain Expedition-sourced.

## 9.3 Rune tiering and generation

Runes are tiered by faction/content tier.

Validated base faction Expedition generation:

- 1 Rune/hour at Mastery level 0.

Therefore:

- 2h = 2 Runes;
- 6h = 6 Runes;
- 12h = 12 Runes.

Faction Mastery modifies the resulting yield, then the final quantity is rounded to the nearest whole Rune.

# 10. FACTION CAPES

## 10.1 General role

Faction Capes are craftable faction preparation equipment from T4 onward.

They combine:

- normal defensive stats;
- a faction-dungeon-only passive;
- faction Runes as an Expedition-driven progression sink.

They have NO HP stat in the validated design.

## 10.2 Keeper Cape defensive stats — pilot

| Tier | Armor | Magic Resistance |
|---|---:|---:|
| T4 | 3 | 5 |
| T5 | 4 | 7 |
| T6 | 6 | 10 |
| T7 | 9 | 14 |
| T8 | 13 | 20 |

Other faction Cape stat profiles may vary naturally by faction identity but must reuse the same generic Cape/passive architecture.

## 10.3 Keeper Cape base recipes — pilot

No predecessor equipment is consumed.

| Tier | Cloth | Leather | Keeper Runes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| T4 | 3 | 1 | 3 |
| T5 | 4 | 2 | 4 |
| T6 | 5 | 2 | 5 |
| T7 | 6 | 3 | 6 |
| T8 | 8 | 4 | 8 |

All materials are the matching target tier.

Normal equipment crafting remains predecessor-free according to the current live crafting contract.

## 10.4 Rune costs by tier

Validated base Rune quantities:

- T4: 3;
- T5: 4;
- T6: 5;
- T7: 6;
- T8: 8.

## 10.5 Enchantment integration

Faction Runes are normal base craft materials for the Cape and therefore reuse the EXISTING generic enchantment material multiplier.

No faction-specific enchantment formula.

Current generic material multipliers:

- `.0 -> .1`: x1;
- `.1 -> .2`: x2;
- `.2 -> .3`: x4.

Capes are capped at `.3`.

`.4` remains weapon Awakening only.

Example T4 Rune path:

- craft Cape: 3 Runes;
- `.0 -> .1`: 3 Runes;
- `.1 -> .2`: 6 Runes;
- `.2 -> .3`: 12 Runes;
- total from nothing to T4.3: 24 Runes = 24 hours of base Expedition time at Mastery 0.

This must reuse the single authoritative enchantment recipe scaling pipeline.

Reference:

- `packages/gameplay/src/equipment/enchantment-recipes.ts`

## 10.6 Dungeon-only faction passive

Each faction Cape has a passive that reduces damage received ONLY inside dungeons of its matching faction.

Validated reduction curve:

| Cape Tier | Damage reduction in matching faction dungeon |
|---|---:|
| T4 | 6% |
| T5 | 8% |
| T6 | 11% |
| T7 | 14% |
| T8 | 18% |

Rules:

- no effect in open world;
- no effect in another faction's dungeon;
- enchantment `.1/.2/.3` does NOT increase this passive percentage;
- the Cape tier itself determines the passive value.

## 10.7 Tier compatibility rule

A faction Cape may be used in its own dungeon tier and all HIGHER dungeon tiers, but never in a lower tier.

Examples:

- Keeper Cape T4: active in Keeper dungeons T4-T8;
- Keeper Cape T6: active in Keeper dungeons T6-T8;
- Keeper Cape T8: active only in Keeper dungeon T8.

This prevents high-tier Capes from trivializing old dungeon tiers while preserving long-term usefulness of lower-tier Capes.

Implementation must resolve this through generic authored faction/tier data, not Keeper-specific code.

# 11. FACTION MASTERY

## 11.1 Structure

Each supported faction has its own Mastery:

- Keeper Mastery;
- Heretic Mastery;
- Undead Mastery;
- Morgana Mastery.

Maximum validated level:

- 100.

## 11.2 XP source

Validated core rule:

> 1 raw faction Fame = 1 Faction Mastery XP.

Only raw/base faction Fame is used.

Faction Fame bonuses must NOT feed back into Mastery XP generation.

This prevents the Mastery Fame bonus from accelerating its own progression.

## 11.3 XP curve

Validated cumulative curve:

`XP_required_for_level_L = 1,500 × L²`

Reference cumulative values:

- Level 10: 150,000 XP;
- Level 25: 937,500 XP;
- Level 50: 3,750,000 XP;
- Level 75: 8,437,500 XP;
- Level 100: 15,000,000 XP.

This is intentionally a long-term progression layer calibrated against current Fame/hour scale.

## 11.4 Yield bonus

Validated:

- +0.5% faction yield per Mastery level;
- Level 100 cap = +50%.

Affected matching-faction channels:

- faction Rune Expedition yield;
- faction drops/drop rates;
- faction Silver;
- faction Fame.

Generic Silver Expedition is excluded.

## 11.5 Offline progression rule

There is NO general offline Faction Mastery progression in the current design.

Do not simulate combat/Fame/Mastery while offline.

The important distinction is:

- Expeditions continue offline;
- Research continues offline;
- Faction Mastery does not independently progress offline.

An Expedition completed while offline still resolves its Expedition rewards normally, but no separate offline-combat Mastery simulation is introduced.

# 14. DATA-DRIVEN IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

Implementation must be definition-driven.

Do not create faction-specific runtime conditionals such as:

`if faction === "Keeper" ...`

outside generic authored-data resolution.

Expected definition families include:

- `ResearchDefinition`;
- `FactionDefinition`;
- `FactionMasteryDefinition`;
- `RelicDefinition`;
- `RelicObjectiveDefinition`;
- `ExpeditionDefinition`;
- faction Cape/passive definitions;
- `AchievementDefinition`.

Required single-source-of-truth rules:

- Expedition family/type determines same-type concurrency validation;
- Expedition definitions author duration/reward/tier/faction;
- Relic objectives are generic objective definitions;
- faction definitions reference existing monster/zone/dungeon IDs;
- Cape recipes use the existing Crafting system;
- Cape enchantments reuse the existing generic enchantment material scaling;
- dungeon passive uses generic faction + minimum-tier matching;
- no duplicated Keeper-specific runtime logic.

Existing catalogs remain authoritative for existing content until deliberately refactored.
