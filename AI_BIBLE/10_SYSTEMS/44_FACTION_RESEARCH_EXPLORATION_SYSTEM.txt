# 44_FACTION_RESEARCH_EXPLORATION_SYSTEM

Status: FUTURE DESIGN / NOT IMPLEMENTED
Authority: design reference for future integration only
Last update: 2026-08-21

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document records the validated design direction for a future secondary progression layer built around:

- Research / Academy;
- the existing Bestiary;
- faction Expeditions;
- generic Expeditions;
- Relics;
- Manuscripts;
- Faction Mastery;
- the existing World > Achievements tab.

The purpose is to create meaningful parallel progression while the hero is farming combat or gathering, without adding arbitrary resources that only lengthen existing crafting loops.

This system must remain subordinate to the core Albion Idle loop and must feed concrete hero/world progression.

This document does NOT describe an implemented system yet.

---

# 2. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

## 2.1 Research owns unlocks

Research is the authoritative system for permanent functional unlocks introduced by this feature.

Faction Mastery must not become a second unlock tree.

Examples of appropriate Research rewards:

- unlock Cartography / Expeditions;
- unlock Archaeology / Relic reconstruction;
- unlock a faction-specific advanced research;
- permanently unlock access to a faction dungeon after its knowledge requirements are met.

Research should not primarily consist of generic stat bonuses such as +5% damage.

## 2.2 Faction Mastery owns faction-wide yield

Faction Mastery represents familiarity and operational efficiency with a faction.

Its validated role is to increase GLOBAL YIELD on activities/rewards tied to that faction.

It must not gate:

- Research;
- faction dungeon access;
- Relics;
- Expeditions;
- recipes;
- core world progression.

Validated direction:

Higher Keeper Mastery may improve Keeper-related yields such as:

- faction-resource quantity from Keeper Expeditions;
- Keeper loot/drop yield or drop-rate modifiers;
- Silver earned from Keeper content;
- Fame earned from Keeper content;
- other future Keeper-specific economic yields if explicitly authored.

Faction Mastery is therefore a family-wide efficiency progression, not an unlock authority.

Exact curves, affected reward channels and caps are OPEN and require balance work before implementation.

## 2.3 Existing Bestiary becomes the Codex surface

Do NOT create a second Codex module.

The existing World Bestiary is the player-facing knowledge surface and should be extended when this feature is implemented.

Existing reference:

- `apps/client/src/ui/world/components/WorldBestiaryView.tsx`
- `apps/client/src/ui/world/worldModels.ts`

Current Bestiary already exposes faction, tier/category, damage type, abilities and loot information. Future progression data should extend this existing surface rather than duplicate it.

## 2.4 Achievements reuse the existing World tab

Do NOT create a second achievement system solely for this feature.

The existing World > Achievements view is the presentation surface for completion objectives.

Existing reference:

- `apps/client/src/ui/world/components/WorldAchievementsView.tsx`

Current achievements already track dungeon-tier conquest. Future faction objectives can extend the same data-driven achievement model.

# 3. FEATURE COMPONENTS

# 3.1 ACADEMY / RESEARCH

## Role

The Academy provides timed parallel research while the hero continues normal gameplay.

Research is the main FUNCTIONAL UNLOCK layer for this feature.

## Player interaction

Future player flow:

1. Open Academy / Research.
2. Select an available research.
3. Pay its authored requirements.
4. Research progresses with real/offline time.
5. Completion permanently unlocks its authored result.

## Initial research examples

These names and numbers are placeholders until a full progression pass is authored.

### Cartography

Result:
- unlocks Expeditions.

### Archaeology

Result:
- unlocks Relic fragment tracking and Relic reconstruction.

### Faction Studies

Result:
- enables advanced faction knowledge/research chains.

### Faction Dungeon Research

Result:
- permanently unlocks the corresponding faction dungeon family when the authored Manuscript/knowledge requirements are satisfied.

Important integration rule:

Research unlock does NOT modify, replace or reinterpret the existing dungeon-key system.

The current dungeon key contract remains unchanged and authoritative:

- keys are tier-based;
- keys are faction-independent;
- a matching tier key is consumed/required according to the current dungeon-entry rules.

Reference:

- `apps/client/src/data/dungeonKeyContentCatalog.ts`

The future Research layer only adds a permanent CONTENT ACCESS prerequisite before a faction dungeon can be selected/entered. Once access is unlocked, the existing key behavior continues exactly as today.

# 3.2 BESTIARY / CODEX PROGRESSION

## Role

The Bestiary becomes the knowledge and progression surface for encountered creatures and factions.

It is NOT a separate currency and does not need a new standalone module.

## Future data displayed per creature

Potential progression fields:

- discovered / undiscovered;
- kill count;
- boss defeated state;
- faction;
- existing combat information;
- existing loot information;
- faction-mastery contribution;
- related achievements.

## Function

The Bestiary should show the player's accumulated knowledge and make faction progression understandable.

It may provide authored prerequisites for Research, but it must not create a dense independent unlock tree.

Example:

Keeper creature kills and boss victories are visible in Bestiary and contribute to Keeper Mastery / faction achievements.

# 3.3 RELICS

## Definition

A Relic is a permanent faction discovery.

It is NOT:

- equipment;
- a generic currency;
- a passive-stat item;
- a replacement for Mastery.

## Validated V1 purpose

Relics unlock the corresponding FACTION EXPEDITION.

Example:

`Keeper Relic`
-> permanently unlocks the Keeper Expedition.

## Acquisition direction

Relics are expected to be reconstructed from faction-specific Relic Fragments after Archaeology is unlocked.

Exact fragment sources, quantities and tiers are OPEN.

Possible future sources may include existing combat, bosses, dungeons, achievements or exploration, but no source is validated yet.

## Persistence

A completed Relic is a permanent account discovery and should not be consumed when launching Expeditions.

---

# 3.4 EXPEDITIONS

## Role

Expeditions are parallel activities that run independently while the hero continues combat, gathering or production.

They are divided into two validated families:

1. FACTION EXPEDITIONS;
2. GENERIC EXPEDITIONS.

They must not all share the same reward purpose.

## 3.4.1 Faction Expeditions

Their validated V1 purpose is to provide FACTION CRAFTING RESOURCES used for faction capes.

Core loop:

Relic acquired
-> faction Expedition unlocked
-> Expedition runs in parallel
-> faction crafting resource is returned
-> faction resource is consumed by faction cape crafting

Example:

Keeper Relic
-> Keeper Expedition
-> Keeper faction resource
-> Keeper Cape recipe

Faction Mastery improves faction-wide yield, including Expedition output for the same faction.

Example direction:

Keeper Mastery -> +X% Keeper Expedition faction-resource yield.

Exact formula and cap are OPEN.

## 3.4.2 Generic Silver Expedition

A dedicated Silver-oriented Expedition is validated as a GENERIC expedition, not tied to any faction.

Its purpose is to provide a comfortable parallel source of Silver, particularly useful when the player reaches large long-term Silver sinks such as Awakened weapon progression / Attunement.

Example presentation direction:

`Caravane marchande`
-> runs in parallel for an authored duration
-> returns primarily Silver

This expedition must NOT:

- require a faction Relic;
- belong to Keeper/Heretic/Undead/Morgana;
- receive bonuses from Faction Mastery;
- replace the main active Silver economy by default.

Balance direction:

- active combat may remain the strongest direct Silver/hour source;
- the Silver Expedition should be attractive because it runs in parallel;
- its exact Silver/hour value must be calibrated against existing Island, crafting, Awakening and Attunement Silver sinks.

Exact duration, unlock research, input cost, Silver yield and scaling model are OPEN.

## Why Expedition rewards exist

Faction resources are justified because they feed a distinct equipment/content family rather than artificially increasing ordinary weapon/armor material requirements.

The generic Silver Expedition is justified because Silver already has major existing sinks and becomes increasingly constrained during Awakened weapon progression.

Neither reward family should be inserted into unrelated systems simply to create grind.

## Expedition cost

NOT YET VALIDATED.

Do not introduce a dedicated `Provision` currency by default.

Potential costs may reuse existing Silver and/or existing production materials if a meaningful sink is needed, but this requires balance work.

---

# 3.5 FACTION CRAFTING RESOURCE

## Definition

A faction crafting resource is a material produced primarily by that faction's Expedition.

## Validated first use

Faction cape crafting.

Example structure:

`Keeper Cape Tx`
requires:
- authored normal cape materials;
- Keeper faction resource of the relevant authored tier/grade if applicable.

Exact cape recipes are NOT defined in this document and must be authored in the existing equipment/crafting data source when implemented.

## Guardrail

Do not create faction resources unless they have a concrete sink.

For V1, the accepted sink is faction cape crafting.

Future sinks require separate validation.

---

# 3.6 MANUSCRIPTS

## Definition

A Manuscript is a rare knowledge item used as a requirement for ADVANCED RESEARCH.

It is not a generic research-point currency.

## Validated integration direction

Manuscripts should integrate with the existing faction dungeon system.

Example future flow:

Faction content
-> obtain faction Manuscript
-> meet authored Research prerequisites
-> complete faction dungeon-access Research
-> faction dungeon becomes permanently available
-> existing dungeon-key behavior remains unchanged for each run

This gives Manuscripts a concrete purpose without altering the existing key economy.

## Existing dungeon structure to preserve

Faction dungeons already exist across T4-T8 for:

- Keeper;
- Heretic;
- Undead;
- Morgana.

Reference:

- `apps/client/src/data/dungeonContentCatalog.ts`

Dungeon entry currently uses tier keys rather than faction keys.

Reference:

- `apps/client/src/data/dungeonKeyContentCatalog.ts`

This key system must remain unchanged unless a separate future design decision explicitly supersedes it.

## Open design questions

Still to define:

- Manuscript source(s);
- faction-specific vs generic Manuscripts;
- whether Manuscripts are consumed by Research or act as permanent discoveries;
- quantities required per faction/tier;
- exact relationship between one faction Research and multiple dungeon tiers.

Do not implement these details until explicitly validated.

# 3.7 FACTION MASTERY

## Definition

Each supported faction may have its own mastery progression.

Initial candidates based on current authored dungeons/content:

- Keeper Mastery;
- Heretic Mastery;
- Undead Mastery;
- Morgana Mastery.

Future factions may join through data, not faction-specific runtime branches.

## Sources of mastery XP

Potential sources include:

- faction kills;
- faction boss victories;
- faction dungeon completions;
- faction expedition completions;
- faction achievements.

Exact XP weights are OPEN.

## Validated reward model

GLOBAL FACTION YIELD ONLY.

Faction Mastery increases rewards generated from content tied to the matching faction.

Validated categories of possible affected yield include:

- faction Expedition resource yield;
- faction loot/drop yield or drop rate;
- Silver earned from faction content;
- Fame earned from faction content.

Additional faction-specific yield channels may be added later only if they fit the same efficiency role and are explicitly authored.

Faction Mastery must NOT affect generic Expeditions such as the Silver Expedition.

Faction Mastery must NOT grant functional unlocks or become a second progression gate.

Do not use it to gate Research, dungeon access, Relics, Expeditions, recipes or world progression.

Exact formulas, stacking rules, caps and per-channel coefficients are OPEN.

---

# 3.8 WORLD ACHIEVEMENTS

## Role

The existing World > Achievements tab becomes the completion/progression surface for this feature.

It should expose concrete faction accomplishments without creating another currency or progression tree by default.

## Candidate achievement categories

Examples for Keeper:

- discover Keeper creatures;
- reach authored Keeper kill milestones;
- defeat a Keeper boss;
- reconstruct the Keeper Relic;
- complete Keeper Expeditions;
- obtain the relevant Manuscript;
- unlock/complete a Keeper dungeon;
- reach authored Keeper Mastery milestones.

Generic Expedition milestones may also appear here later if explicitly authored.

These examples describe categories only. Exact thresholds/rewards are OPEN.

Achievements may contribute Faction Mastery XP if later validated, but should not become a second unlock authority competing with Research.

# 4. COMPLETE TARGET LOOP

The intended systemic relationship is:

Faction Combat
-> Bestiary progression
-> Faction Mastery XP

Research
-> unlocks Cartography / Archaeology / faction knowledge functions

Archaeology
-> reconstructs faction Relic

Faction Relic
-> permanently unlocks faction Expedition

Faction Expedition
-> produces faction crafting resource

Faction Mastery
-> improves global yield from matching faction content (Expedition resources, drops, Silver, Fame, etc.)

Faction crafting resource
-> consumed in faction Cape crafting

Faction Manuscript
-> requirement for advanced faction Research

Advanced faction Research
-> permanently unlocks faction Dungeon access

Dungeon access
-> existing tier-key behavior remains unchanged

Generic Silver Expedition
-> runs independently of faction systems
-> provides a comfortable parallel Silver source for existing long-term Silver sinks

All major milestones
-> represented in existing World > Achievements

---

# 5. SYSTEM OWNERSHIP / NO-DUPLICATION RULES

To avoid overlapping progression systems:

- Research = functional unlock authority.
- Bestiary = knowledge/progression presentation.
- Relic = permanent faction Expedition unlock.
- Faction Expedition = parallel source of faction crafting resource.
- Generic Silver Expedition = parallel Silver source, faction-independent.
- Manuscript = advanced Research requirement.
- Faction Mastery = global faction-yield progression only.
- Achievements = accomplishment presentation/objectives.
- Dungeon keys = existing dungeon-entry system, unchanged.
- Crafting = authoritative consumer/recipe system for faction capes.

No system should silently take ownership from another.

# 6. DATA-DRIVEN IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

Future implementation must be definition-driven.

Do not create faction-specific conditionals such as:

`if faction === "Keeper" ...`

outside generic authored-data resolution.

Expected future definition families may include:

- `ResearchDefinition`;
- `FactionDefinition`;
- `FactionMasteryDefinition`;
- `RelicDefinition`;
- `ExpeditionDefinition`;
- `ManuscriptDefinition`;
- `AchievementDefinition`.

`ExpeditionDefinition` must support both faction-bound and faction-independent expedition content without requiring separate runtime systems.

A faction definition should reference its related content IDs rather than duplicate monster/dungeon/crafting data.

Existing catalogs remain authoritative for existing content until deliberately refactored.
