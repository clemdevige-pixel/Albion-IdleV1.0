# 44_FACTION_RESEARCH_EXPLORATION_SYSTEM

Status: VALIDATED / IMPLEMENTED CONTRACT + BALANCE FOLLOW-UP REQUIRED
Authority: canonical Academy / Research / Expedition / Dungeon discovery contract
Last update: 2026-08-23

---

# 1. PURPOSE

The Academy is the permanent functional-unlock system for secondary progression.

V1 owns:

- Research;
- Silver Expeditions;
- one generic Faction Expedition track;
- Dungeon discovery;
- the global Dungeon Relic discovery chain;
- Research completion recaps.

The system must remain data-driven. Runtime code must not branch specifically for Keeper / Heretic / Undead / Morgana for shared mechanics.

Research unlocks capabilities/content. Faction Mastery remains faction-specific progression/yield and is never a second unlock tree.

---

# 2. ACADEMY

The Academy is a UNIQUE Player Island building.

Construction:

- available at Island Level 2;
- 500 Silver;
- 12 T3 Planks;
- 8 T3 Bars;
- 4 T3 Cloth.

Operational tier mapping:

- Academy level 1 -> T4 Research;
- level 2 -> T5;
- level 3 -> T6;
- level 4 -> T7;
- level 5 -> T8.

Upgrade curve:

| Upgrade | Island level required | Silver | Refined materials |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| T4 -> T5 | 3 | 5,000 | 5 of each T4 refined family |
| T5 -> T6 | 4 | 18,000 | 9 of each T5 refined family |
| T6 -> T7 | 5 | 50,000 | 14 of each T6 refined family |
| T7 -> T8 | 6 | 85,000 | 20 of each T7 refined family |

The four refined families are Planks, Bars, Leather and Cloth.

Research capacity:

- Researches are independent and may run simultaneously;
- there is NO global Research slot;
- the same Research may not be started twice;
- every started Research owns its own timer and consumes its own authored cost atomically.

Research progresses online and through the authoritative Background Progression system.

Research from Academy tiers above the current Academy tier must NOT be displayed to the player.

---

# 3. RESEARCH LIFECYCLE

1. A Research definition becomes visible when its Academy tier is reachable.
2. Its authored prerequisites determine whether it is locked or available.
3. Starting it atomically consumes its authored cost.
4. It receives an independent active timer.
5. Multiple independent Researches may progress from the same authoritative elapsed time.
6. Completion automatically commits its authored unlock IDs.
7. Completion displays a generic Research recap.

No manual reward collection is used.

Every Research completion recap is data-driven and may show:

- Research completed;
- effect applied;
- newly unlocked content/capabilities.

Unlock-specific popups must not be hand-authored as isolated one-off systems when the generic recap model can represent them.

---

# 4. EXPEDITION RESEARCH TRACKS

Silver Expeditions and Faction Expeditions use two independent Research tracks.

## 4.1 Cartography = Silver Expeditions

Cartography controls only the generic Silver Expedition tier.

| Research | Tier | Silver | Duration | Unlock |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Cartography I | T4 | 5,000 | 30 min | Silver Expedition T4 |
| Cartography II | T5 | 15,000 | 1 h | Silver Expedition T5 |
| Cartography III | T6 | 40,000 | 2 h | Silver Expedition T6 + second Expedition slot |
| Cartography IV | T7 | 70,000 | 3 h | Silver Expedition T7 |
| Cartography V | T8 | 110,000 | 4 h | Silver Expedition T8 |

Cartography II-V require the previous Cartography unlock plus the matching Academy tier.

No material cost.

## 4.2 Archaeology = generic Faction Expedition

Archaeology controls the generic Faction Expedition tier.

There is no longer one Expedition family per Keeper / Heretic / Undead / Morgana faction.

| Research | Tier | Silver | Duration | Unlock |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Archéologie I | T4 | 5,000 | 30 min | Faction Expedition T4 |
| Archéologie II | T5 | 15,000 | 1 h | Faction Expedition T5 |
| Archéologie III | T6 | 40,000 | 2 h | Faction Expedition T6 |
| Archéologie IV | T7 | 70,000 | 3 h | Faction Expedition T7 |
| Archéologie V | T8 | 110,000 | 4 h | Faction Expedition T8 |

Archéologie II-V require the previous Archaeology unlock plus the matching Academy tier.

No material cost.

Archaeology does not own Dungeon Relic examination.

The Cartography/Archaeology cost and duration curves remain the current authored baseline. Their future content/balance may be revisited without changing the Research runtime architecture.

# 5. EXPEDITIONS

Expeditions are passive parallel activities and continue offline through Background Progression.

V1 Expedition families are now:

1. Generic Silver Expedition.
2. Generic Faction Expedition.

Silver Expedition requirement:

- matching Cartography tier unlock.

Faction Expedition requirement:

- matching Archaeology tier unlock.

There is NO Relic prerequisite for Faction Expeditions.

Slots:

- before any Expedition Research unlock: 0 slots;
- T4/T5: 1 slot;
- Cartography III / T6 unlocks slot 2;
- T6-T8: maximum 2 slots.

Same-type concurrency remains:

- two Expeditions of the same type may not run simultaneously.

Durations remain:

- 2 h;
- 6 h;
- 12 h.

Reward/hour is equivalent across duration choices before whole-item rounding unless a future authored reward explicitly states otherwise.

Expedition tiers above the current Academy tier must NOT be presented to the player.

## 5.1 Silver Expedition

The existing Silver/hour curve remains authored independently from Faction Expedition rewards.

Faction Mastery does not modify Silver Expedition output.

## 5.2 Faction Expedition

The Faction Expedition is the PRIMARY and most reliable source of the common Faction Rune.

Canonical guaranteed baseline currently preserved pending the dedicated reward-balance pass:

- 1 matching-tier Faction Rune per hour.

This baseline is intentionally temporary for balance purposes; it prevents inventing unvalidated values during the structural migration.

Validated reward direction for the Faction Expedition:

- guaranteed/predictable Faction Rune output remains the signature reward;
- future authored secondary rewards may include Silver;
- enchantment shards;
- Dungeon key fragments;
- complete Dungeon keys;
- potentially increased Rune yield at higher tiers.

IMPORTANT:

- exact quantities, probabilities and tier curves for secondary rewards are NOT yet validated;
- they must be implemented through a data-driven reward table after a dedicated balance pass;
- runtime code must not inject ad-hoc reward cases.

Because the Expedition is now generic rather than Keeper/Heretic/Undead/Morgana-specific, no individual Faction Mastery boosts its Rune output.

# 6. COMMON FACTION RUNE

The four old faction-specific Rune families are replaced by ONE shared tiered Faction Rune family.

Canonical runtime IDs:

- `item_resource_rune_faction_t4`;
- `item_resource_rune_faction_t5`;
- `item_resource_rune_faction_t6`;
- `item_resource_rune_faction_t7`;
- `item_resource_rune_faction_t8`.

The tier dimension remains authoritative.

The Rune no longer carries Keeper / Heretic / Undead / Morgana identity.

Faction identity remains carried by:

- Dungeon definitions;
- faction monsters;
- faction artifacts;
- artifact weapons;
- faction capes;
- faction matchup bonuses;
- Faction Mastery;
- Achievements / Bestiary where applicable.

This prevents four inventories of functionally identical crafting currency while preserving faction-specific content through the artifact and Dungeon layers.

# 7. ARTIFACT WEAPON CRAFT

Artifact weapon crafting uses:

- authored refined base resources for the weapon family/tier;
- exactly 1 matching faction Dungeon artifact;
- matching-tier common Faction Runes.

No predecessor weapon is consumed.

Current validated Rune cost curve remains:

| Tier | Common Faction Runes |
|---|---:|
| T4 | 5 |
| T5 | 10 |
| T6 | 20 |
| T7 | 35 |
| T8 | 55 |

The faction-specific artifact is the faction identity gate of the craft. The Rune is a common secondary currency.

# 8. GLOBAL DUNGEON RELIC

Dungeon discovery uses ONE global Relic.

Canonical IDs:

- Relic definition: `relic_dungeon`;
- inventory item: `item_relic_dungeon`.

## 8.1 Acquisition

The Relic drops once, guaranteed, from:

- Blue world band;
- Frostpeak Mountain / `zone_mountain_t4`;
- Segment 10 (zero-based runtime segment index 9);
- final boss encounter (Ancient Keeper monster definition).

Acquisition MUST validate authored world context and segment, not only monster ID.

## 8.2 Charge

Acquisition records the authoritative faction-kill baseline for all four factions.

The Relic then requires NEW kills after acquisition:

- 50 Keeper;
- 50 Heretic;
- 50 Undead;
- 50 Morgana.

Historical kills before Relic acquisition never count.

State before Research analysis:

`UNOBTAINED -> BROKEN -> CHARGED`

At 50/50 in all four objectives, the Relic remains CHARGED until its analysis Research completes.

# 9. DUNGEON DISCOVERY RESEARCH CHAIN

## 9.1 Analyse de la Relique

Tier: T4.

Prerequisites:

- Academy T4;
- global Dungeon Relic = CHARGED.

Cost:

- 0 Silver;
- no materials.

Duration:

- 10 minutes.

Completion:

- marks the global Relic as EXAMINED;
- permanently unlocks `Localisation des Sanctuaires`;
- displays the generic Research completion recap.

## 9.2 Localisation des Sanctuaires

Tier: T4.

Prerequisites:

- Academy T4;
- Analyse de la Relique completed.

Cost:

- 10,000 Silver;
- no materials.

Duration:

- 1 hour.

Completion permanently unlocks:

- World > Donjons;
- Dungeon key-fragment world drops;
- complete Dungeon-key world drops;
- the rare common Faction Rune world-drop channel on faction monsters.

Canonical Rune-world capability ID:

- `faction_rune:world_drop`.

This is intentionally the same progression moment as Dungeon discovery because the Rune becomes part of the Dungeon -> artifact -> craft loop.

# 10. FACTION RUNE WORLD DROP

Before `Localisation des Sanctuaires` completes:

- faction monsters cannot grant common Faction Runes through the world Rune-drop channel.

After completion:

- faction monsters may grant matching-tier common Faction Runes through that channel.

Design contract:

- world Rune drops are RARE and SECONDARY;
- the Faction Expedition must remain the best/main Rune acquisition source in expected Rune/hour;
- world combat supplements normal play and must not replace deliberate Expedition farming.

IMPORTANT BALANCE HOLD:

- exact drop probability;
- quantity per proc;
- tier mapping edge cases;
- interaction with authored faction loot multipliers

are NOT yet validated.

The unlock capability is implemented, but final numeric world-drop authoring must wait for the dedicated balance pass rather than receiving invented placeholder values.

---

# 11. DUNGEON VISIBILITY AND KEY DROP GATE

Before `Localisation des Sanctuaires` is complete:

- World > Donjons is not displayed;
- Dungeon definitions are not enterable;
- world combat cannot grant Dungeon key fragments;
- world combat cannot grant complete Dungeon keys;
- world combat cannot grant common Faction Runes through the rare Rune channel.

After completion, those channels become available subject to their normal authored rules.

Dungeon keys remain:

- tier-based;
- faction-independent;
- consumed according to the existing Dungeon entry contract.

Research activates the channels; it does not replace tier progression or Dungeon entry rules.

---

# 12. FACTION MASTERY

Faction Mastery remains faction-specific yield/progression only.

It may affect matching-faction authored channels such as:

- faction combat Silver/Fame yield;
- authored faction loot channels.

It does NOT gate:

- Research;
- Expeditions;
- Relic acquisition/charge;
- Dungeon discovery;
- recipes;
- world progression.

The generic Silver Expedition is unaffected by Faction Mastery.

The generic Faction Expedition is also unaffected by an individual Faction Mastery because it has no Keeper/Heretic/Undead/Morgana identity.

---

# 13. PERSISTENCE / MIGRATION

Research, Expeditions, faction knowledge and Relic state remain persistent providers.

Research persistence now stores a collection of active Research timers.

Research snapshot v1 with one `activeResearch` remains load-compatible and migrates naturally into the parallel active collection.

Runtime save format v5 migrates all legacy faction-specific Rune IDs to the matching common tiered Rune ID:

- Keeper Tn -> Faction Rune Tn;
- Heretic Tn -> Faction Rune Tn;
- Undead Tn -> Faction Rune Tn;
- Morgana Tn -> Faction Rune Tn.

Existing quantities are preserved. If several legacy Rune stacks of the same tier coexist, they now resolve to the same common item ID and normal inventory stacking rules may consolidate them through subsequent inventory operations.

Legacy faction Relics remain non-convertible to the global Dungeon Relic, as previously validated.

---

# 14. DATA-DRIVEN RULES

Required architecture:

- Research definitions own tier/cost/duration/prerequisites/unlock IDs;
- ResearchService owns a collection of active timers, not one hard-coded slot;
- Expedition definitions consume Research unlock IDs;
- one generic Faction Expedition definition exists per tier;
- Faction Rune identity is common by tier;
- artifact recipes consume one faction artifact plus common tiered Runes;
- Dungeon navigation consumes one global Research unlock;
- the Rune world-drop channel consumes the `faction_rune:world_drop` Research capability;
- future Expedition secondary rewards must use authored reward tables;
- Research recap presentation consumes authored metadata.

Forbidden:

- Keeper/Heretic/Undead/Morgana runtime branches for shared mechanics;
- duplicate unlock booleans saved outside owning systems;
- a second Research scheduler layered over ResearchService;
- UI reconstructing gameplay authority independently from Research/Expedition data;
- separate faction Rune currencies reintroduced for equivalent craft purposes;
- hard-coded Expedition secondary reward cases when a data table can own them;
- enabling world Rune drops before the Research capability;
- tuning world Rune drops so strongly that world farming overtakes Faction Expedition as the main Rune source.

# 15. CANONICAL LOOP SUMMARY

## Research / Expeditions

`Academy tier`
`-> parallel Researches`
`-> Cartography I-V -> Silver Expedition T4-T8`
`-> Archaeology I-V -> generic Faction Expedition T4-T8`
`-> common tiered Faction Runes (primary source)`

## Dungeons / Rune secondary source

`Blue Frostpeak Mountain S10 boss`
`-> global Dungeon Relic`
`-> 50 Keeper + 50 Heretic + 50 Undead + 50 Morgana`
`-> Analyse de la Relique`
`-> Localisation des Sanctuaires`
`-> Donjons + keys/fragments + rare world Faction Rune channel`

## Artifact craft

`Dungeon key`
`-> faction Dungeon`
`-> faction artifact`
`+ refined resources`
`+ common Faction Runes`
`-> artifact weapon`

This is the canonical structural contract as of 2026-08-23.

2. Rune/hour progression by Expedition tier if the 1/h baseline is changed.
3. Rare world Faction Rune drop rate/quantity by tier.
