# 44_FACTION_RESEARCH_EXPLORATION_SYSTEM

Status: FUTURE DESIGN / NOT IMPLEMENTED
Authority: design reference for future integration only
Last update: 2026-08-21

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document records the validated design direction for a future secondary progression layer built around:

- Research / Academy;
- the existing Bestiary as Codex surface;
- Relic quests;
- faction Expeditions;
- a generic Silver Expedition;
- Faction Mastery;
- the existing World > Achievements tab.

The purpose is to create meaningful parallel progression while the hero is farming combat or gathering, without adding arbitrary resources that only lengthen existing crafting loops.

This system must remain subordinate to the core Albion Idle loop and must feed concrete hero/world progression.

This document does NOT describe an implemented system yet.

---

# 2. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

## 2.1 Research owns functional unlocks

Research is the authoritative system for permanent functional unlocks introduced by this feature.

Faction Mastery must not become a second unlock tree.

Research rewards should unlock concrete functions/content, not generic stat bonuses.

Examples:

- unlock Expeditions;
- unlock Relic reconstruction;
- unlock a faction Expedition;
- unlock permanent access to a faction dungeon family.

## 2.2 Faction Mastery owns faction-wide yield

Faction Mastery represents familiarity and efficiency with a faction.

Its validated role is GLOBAL FACTION YIELD ONLY.

Possible affected channels include:

- faction Expedition resource yield;
- faction loot/drop yield or drop-rate modifiers;
- Silver earned from faction content;
- Fame earned from faction content.

Faction Mastery must NOT gate:

- Research;
- dungeon access;
- Relics;
- Expeditions;
- recipes;
- world progression.

Faction Mastery must NOT affect generic Expeditions such as the Silver Expedition.

Exact formulas, coefficients and caps remain OPEN.

## 2.3 Existing Bestiary becomes the Codex surface

Do NOT create a second Codex module.

The existing World Bestiary is the player-facing knowledge surface and should be extended when this feature is implemented.

Existing references:

- `apps/client/src/ui/world/components/WorldBestiaryView.tsx`
- `apps/client/src/ui/world/worldModels.ts`

Future progression fields may include:

- discovered / undiscovered;
- kill count;
- elite/boss victories;
- faction;
- existing combat information;
- existing loot information;
- Relic-quest progression;
- faction-mastery progression;
- related achievements.

## 2.4 Existing World Achievements surface is reused

Do NOT create a second achievement system solely for this feature.

The existing World > Achievements view remains the presentation surface for completion objectives.

Existing reference:

- `apps/client/src/ui/world/components/WorldAchievementsView.tsx`

# 3. ACADEMY / RESEARCH

## 3.1 Placement and unlock

The Academy is a UNIQUE island utility building.

Validated unlock timing:

- becomes available at Island Level 2;
- therefore after the early T3 loop is understood and Dark Swamp progression has been completed according to the current Island progression contract.

The Academy belongs to the Player Island architecture rather than being an abstract standalone menu.

## 3.2 Construction

Construction uses the EXISTING economy only:

- Silver;
- existing authored resources/materials.

Do not create a generic Research Point / Science currency by default.

Exact construction cost remains OPEN for balance.

## 3.3 Research slot count

Validated V1:

- one active Research slot.

Do not add a second slot by default.

A future additional slot requires separate design validation.

## 3.4 Research lifecycle

1. Player opens Academy.
2. Player selects an available Research.
3. Authored costs/prerequisites are validated.
4. Costs are consumed.
5. Research timer starts.
6. Research progresses online and offline.
7. On completion, the authored unlock becomes permanent.

Research completion must not require manual reward collection.

## 3.5 Offline/background progression

Research is a passive activity and must integrate with the existing Background Progression model.

Rules:

- server-authoritative time;
- elapsed-time resolution;
- no client clock authority;
- no tick-by-tick offline simulation;
- same authored duration online/offline.

Reference:

- `AI_BIBLE/10_SYSTEMS/39_BACKGROUND_PROGRESSION_SYSTEM.txt`

## 3.6 Academy tier behavior

The Academy follows world/island tier progression.

Validated rule:

- Academy T4 exposes T4 Research;
- Academy T5 exposes T5 Research;
- Academy T6 exposes T6 Research;
- Academy T7 exposes T7 Research;
- Academy T8 exposes T8 Research.

Increasing Academy tier does NOT grant generic Research-speed bonuses.

Its tier exists to make the Research of the associated tier available.

## 3.7 Research structure

Do NOT force an arbitrary fixed number of Research entries per tier.

Validated model:

- a small set of SOCLE Research;
- contextual Research appears only when its authored prerequisites become true.

Examples of contextual triggers:

- faction Relic reconstructed;
- relevant world/tier reached;
- other explicitly authored content prerequisite.

# 4. SOCLE RESEARCH

## 4.1 Cartography I — T4

Validated effect:

- unlocks the Expedition system;
- unlocks the generic Silver Expedition at T4;
- enables T4 Expedition definitions whose own prerequisites are satisfied.

## 4.2 Archaeology I — T4

Validated effect:

- enables faction Relic-quest progression;
- enables Relic Fragment tracking;
- enables Relic reconstruction once all authored objectives are complete.

Archæology is a capability acquired once.

Do NOT create Archaeology II/III/IV/V only to mirror tier progression unless a future mechanic genuinely requires it.

## 4.3 Cartography progression T5-T8

Cartography continues because Expedition capability genuinely expands with world tier.

Validated current V1 direction:

- Cartography II / T5: enables T5 Expedition definitions;
- Cartography III / T6: enables T6 Expedition definitions and unlocks a second simultaneous Expedition slot;
- Cartography IV / T7: enables T7 Expedition definitions;
- Cartography V / T8: enables T8 Expedition definitions.

Do not invent extra special route mechanics for V1.

Future expedition routes/events/specializations may extend this later.

# 5. FACTION RELICS

## 5.1 Definition

A Relic is a permanent faction discovery reconstructed through a deterministic mini-quest.

It is NOT:

- equipment;
- a generic currency;
- a passive-stat item;
- an RNG farm item;
- a replacement for Mastery.

There is ONE Relic per faction across the whole game, not one Relic per tier.

Example:

- one Keeper Relic for T4-T8;
- one Heretic Relic for T4-T8;
- one Undead Relic for T4-T8;
- one Morgana Relic for T4-T8.

The Relic represents discovery/understanding of the faction, not a tiered material.

## 5.2 Fragment count

Validated V1:

- 5 fragments complete one faction Relic.

Fragments are awarded by deterministic objectives, not random monster drops.

Once the Relic is reconstructed:

- it is permanently acquired;
- its fragment progression is complete;
- no further fragments of that Relic are generated.

## 5.3 Universal Relic-quest template

Every faction uses the SAME runtime quest structure.

Only authored data changes.

Five fragment objectives:

1. DISCOVERY
   - discover/defeat the two main normal units of the faction.

2. FAMILIARIZATION
   - reach a first authored faction kill threshold.

3. DEEP STUDY
   - reach a second authored faction kill threshold.

4. ELITE STUDY
   - defeat an authored number of the faction's elite unit.

5. TERRITORY PROGRESSION
   - reach an authored progression milestone inside a zone where that faction is dominant.

Important:

- no final biome boss is required by default;
- the Relic should be obtainable before the end of the relevant chapter so that faction-content preparation can begin before the chapter ends;
- exact thresholds and zone milestone are authored per faction but resolved by one generic data-driven system.

## 5.4 Historical progress

Where existing persistent data allows it, Relic objectives should recognize progress already completed before Archaeology is unlocked.

The player should not be forced to repeat old content purely because the feature became visible later.

Exact migration/backfill behavior requires technical design at implementation time.

# 6. CONTEXTUAL FACTION RESEARCH

Reconstructing a faction Relic makes TWO independent contextual Research entries available.

This creates two branches from the same faction discovery.

## 6.1 Faction Expedition Research

Example Keeper naming direction:

`Étude des Keeper`

Prerequisite:

- Keeper Relic reconstructed.

Validated reward:

- permanently unlocks the Keeper Expedition family.

Cartography controls which tier of that Expedition family is currently available.

Example:

- Keeper Relic + Keeper Expedition Research + Cartography I -> Keeper T4 Expedition;
- Cartography II -> Keeper T5 Expedition;
- etc.

Do NOT create `Keeper Study I/II/III/IV/V` purely to mirror tiers.

## 6.2 Faction Dungeon Research

Example Keeper naming direction:

`Localiser les Sanctuaires Keeper`

Prerequisite:

- Keeper Relic reconstructed.

Validated reward:

- permanently unlocks the Keeper dungeon family as selectable content.

Equivalent Research exists for each supported faction.

Current target families:

- Keeper;
- Heretic;
- Undead;
- Morgana.

The four dungeon-unlock Research entries are separate Research choices.

## 6.3 Dungeon key system remains unchanged

Faction Dungeon Research does NOT modify, replace or reinterpret dungeon keys.

The current key contract remains authoritative:

- keys are tier-based;
- keys are faction-independent;
- current per-run entry behavior remains unchanged.

Reference:

- `apps/client/src/data/dungeonKeyContentCatalog.ts`

Research only controls permanent CONTENT ACCESS.

Keys continue to control dungeon run entry exactly as they do today.

## 6.4 Manuscripts removed from current scope

Manuscripts are NOT part of the currently validated V1 design.

They were explored conceptually but did not have a sufficiently distinct justified role.

Do not implement Manuscripts unless a future design decision reintroduces them with a concrete purpose.

# 7. EXPEDITIONS — V1 CONTRACT

## 7.1 Core identity

Expeditions are passive parallel activities that run independently while the hero continues:

- combat;
- gathering;
- refining;
- crafting;
- other normal gameplay.

They also progress through the authoritative offline/background model.

## 7.2 Expedition families

Validated V1 contains two families:

### Generic Silver Expedition

Purpose:

- comfortable parallel Silver generation;
- particularly useful against existing large Silver sinks such as Awakened weapon progression / Attunement.

Rules:

- faction-independent;
- requires no faction Relic;
- not affected by Faction Mastery;
- does not replace active combat as the default strongest direct Silver/hour source unless later balance explicitly decides otherwise.

### Faction Expedition

Purpose:

- generate the matching faction crafting resource;
- first validated sink is faction cape crafting.

Rules:

- faction-specific;
- requires the faction Relic and completed faction Expedition Research;
- available tiers are controlled by Cartography/current progression;
- affected by the matching Faction Mastery yield bonus.

## 7.3 Expedition slots

Validated V1:

- T4: 1 simultaneous Expedition slot;
- T5: 1 simultaneous Expedition slot;
- T6: 2 simultaneous Expedition slots;
- T7: 2 simultaneous Expedition slots;
- T8: 2 simultaneous Expedition slots.

The second slot is unlocked by Cartography III / T6.

This intentionally creates an early allocation decision between Silver generation and faction-resource generation.

## 7.4 Expedition durations

Validated V1 formats:

- 2 hours;
- 6 hours;
- 12 hours.

Base reward/hour should be equivalent or near-equivalent between durations.

Duration choice is primarily a convenience/availability choice, not a mandatory optimization puzzle.

Exact reward values remain OPEN for balance.

## 7.5 Launch cost

Validated V1:

- no Expedition launch cost.

Do NOT add:

- Provisions;
- generic Expedition currency;
- arbitrary Silver/material cost.

A cost may be reconsidered only if future economy analysis demonstrates a concrete need.

## 7.6 Completion and reward delivery

When an Expedition completes:

- reward is credited automatically;
- no manual `Collect` action is required;
- the completed Expedition releases its slot according to the runtime completion contract.

The Expedition UI must make completion state clear.

## 7.7 Completion recap popup

A completion recap popup is REQUIRED for readability.

The popup is informational only; rewards are already credited.

For a faction Expedition, recap should expose at minimum:

- Expedition name;
- duration completed;
- base faction-resource reward;
- applied Faction Mastery bonus if applicable;
- final total credited.

For a Silver Expedition, recap should expose at minimum:

- Expedition name;
- duration completed;
- final Silver credited.

Example presentation:

`Expédition terminée — Keeper T4`

- Durée: 6h
- Ressource Keeper de base: 120
- Bonus Maîtrise Keeper: +18
- Total: 138

Offline/multi-completion rule:

- if multiple Expeditions completed while the player was away, show ONE grouped recap;
- do NOT chain multiple blocking popups one after another.

The grouped recap lists each completed Expedition and its credited reward.

# 8. FACTION CRAFTING RESOURCE

## Definition

A faction crafting resource is a material produced primarily by that faction's Expedition.

## Validated first use

Faction cape crafting from T4 onward.

This is important because faction capes participate in the preparation loop before the first faction dungeons.

Example structure:

`Keeper Cape Tx`
requires:

- existing authored cape materials;
- Keeper faction resource at the relevant authored tier/grade if applicable.

Exact resource names, tiering and cape quantities are OPEN and must be authored in existing crafting/equipment data when balanced.

## Guardrail

Do not insert faction resources into ordinary weapon/armor recipes merely to increase grind duration.

For V1, faction cape crafting is the validated sink.

# 9. FACTION MASTERY

## Definition

Each supported faction has its own mastery progression.

Initial supported target:

- Keeper Mastery;
- Heretic Mastery;
- Undead Mastery;
- Morgana Mastery.

Future factions must be added through data rather than runtime faction-specific branches.

## Sources of mastery XP

Potential sources currently accepted for future balancing include:

- faction kills;
- faction elite/boss victories;
- faction dungeon completions;
- faction Expedition completions;
- faction achievements.

Exact XP weights remain OPEN.

## Validated reward role

Faction-wide yield only.

Potential affected channels:

- faction Expedition resource yield;
- faction loot/drop yield or drop rate;
- Silver earned from faction content;
- Fame earned from faction content.

No unlock/gate authority.

# 10. WORLD ACHIEVEMENTS

The existing World > Achievements tab should surface major milestones from this system.

Candidate categories include:

- faction discovery;
- faction kill milestones;
- elite milestones;
- Relic quest fragments;
- Relic completion;
- faction Expedition unlock/completions;
- faction dungeon Research completion;
- faction dungeon completion;
- Faction Mastery milestones;
- generic Expedition milestones if later authored.

Achievements are primarily completion/progression presentation.

Do not make Achievements a competing unlock authority by default.

# 12. SYSTEM OWNERSHIP / NO-DUPLICATION RULES

- Academy/Research = functional unlock authority.
- Cartography = Expedition tier/capability progression.
- Archaeology = Relic reconstruction capability.
- Bestiary = knowledge/progression presentation.
- Relic = permanent faction-discovery prerequisite for contextual faction Research.
- Faction Expedition Research = permanent faction Expedition unlock.
- Faction Dungeon Research = permanent faction dungeon-content unlock.
- Faction Expedition = parallel source of faction crafting resource.
- Generic Silver Expedition = parallel Silver source, faction-independent.
- Faction Mastery = faction-wide yield progression only.
- Achievements = accomplishment presentation/objectives.
- Dungeon keys = existing per-run dungeon-entry system, unchanged.
- Crafting = authoritative consumer/recipe system for faction capes.

No system should silently take ownership from another.

# 13. DATA-DRIVEN IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

Future implementation must be definition-driven.

Do not create faction-specific runtime conditionals such as:

`if faction === "Keeper" ...`

outside generic authored-data resolution.

Expected definition families may include:

- `ResearchDefinition`;
- `FactionDefinition`;
- `FactionMasteryDefinition`;
- `RelicDefinition`;
- `RelicObjectiveDefinition`;
- `ExpeditionDefinition`;
- `AchievementDefinition`.

`ExpeditionDefinition` must support both faction-bound and generic Expeditions without separate runtime systems.

A `RelicDefinition` should author its five objectives through generic objective types rather than custom code.

Faction definitions should reference existing monster/zone/dungeon/crafting IDs rather than duplicate their content data.

Existing catalogs remain authoritative for existing content until deliberately refactored.
