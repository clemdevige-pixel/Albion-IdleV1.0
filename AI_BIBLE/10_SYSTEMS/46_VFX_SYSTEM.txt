# 46. VISUAL EFFECTS SYSTEM

## Purpose

The Visual Effects (VFX) System provides immediate and readable visual feedback for gameplay events while preserving the clarity of the pixel art aesthetic.

Its responsibilities are to:

- Reinforce gameplay actions.
- Improve combat readability.
- Highlight important events.
- Support player feedback.
- Maintain stable performance.
- Respect accessibility settings.

Visual effects never replace gameplay information.

They enhance it.

---

# Design Goals

The Visual Effects System follows these principles:

- Readability first.
- Pixel-art friendly.
- Lightweight rendering.
- Minimal screen clutter.
- Consistent visual language.
- Performance stability.
- Accessibility compatibility.
- Modular architecture.

---

# VFX Philosophy

The game adopts a minimalist VFX philosophy.

Visual effects exist to communicate gameplay information quickly and clearly.

Effects should never dominate the screen or hide characters.

Players spend long periods farming and managing progression.

Visual comfort is therefore prioritized over spectacle.

---

- Motion Blur
- Lens Flares

- Screen Distortion

- Procedural Effects

# Color Palette

Each gameplay category owns a dedicated color.

| Category | Color |
|----------|--------|
| Damage | Red |
| Critical Hit | Yellow |
| Heal | Green |
| Buff | Blue |
| Debuff | Purple |
| Loot | Gold |
| Craft / Refine | White |
| Boss | Dark Red |

These colors remain constant across the game.

Biome, equipment and enchantment never modify VFX colors.

---

# Color Blind Compatibility

Important information is never communicated using color alone.

Effects also differ by:

- Shape
- Animation
- Iconography
- Timing
- Position

This guarantees compatibility with Color Blind Modes defined by the Accessibility System.

---

# Lifetime Philosophy

Visual effects are intentionally very short.

Their purpose is to communicate information instantly before disappearing.

Effects never remain on screen longer than necessary.

---

---

# Active Effect Budget

To maintain stable performance, the engine limits the number of simultaneously active effects.

Example:

Maximum Active VFX:

```
30
```

The exact value is configurable by developers.

---

# Priority System

When the VFX budget is exceeded, effects are processed according to priority.

Highest Priority:

1. Boss Spawn
2. Boss Death
3. Critical Hit
4. Player Ability
5. Heal
6. Buff
7. Debuff
8. Enemy Hit
9. Gathering
10. Loot Pickup

Low-priority effects are discarded first.

Existing effects are never interrupted.

---

# Effect Pooling

Visual effects are pooled.

No effect is instantiated during gameplay.

Lifecycle:

```
Pool

↓

Activate

↓

Play

↓

Finish

↓

Return to Pool
```

Pooling minimizes:

- Memory allocations
- Garbage Collection
- Frame spikes

---

# Combat Effects

Combat effects include:

- Weapon Hit
- Ability Cast
- Critical Strike
- Heal
- Death

Effects appear immediately after combat calculations.

No gameplay delay is introduced.

---

# Gathering Effects

Gathering produces small impact effects.

Examples:

- Rock chips
- Wood splinters
- Fiber particles
- Skinning particles
- Water splash

Only one lightweight effect is generated per successful gathering action.

---

# Crafting Effects

Craft completion generates:

- Small burst
- White sparkle
- Short fade

Crafting queues do not continuously generate effects.

Only completion triggers VFX.

---

# Refining Effects

Refining uses the same philosophy.

Only refinement completion generates a visual effect.

Background refinement remains visually silent.

---

# Loot Effects

Loot Pickup displays a brief golden sparkle.

Large loot batches generate a single combined effect.

The system avoids visual spam.

---

# Boss Effects

Boss encounters receive enhanced visual emphasis.

Boss Spawn:

- Dark Red burst
- Ground pulse
- Short fade

Boss Death:

- Larger burst
- Victory emphasis
- Quick fade

Boss effects remain within the minimalist visual philosophy.

---

# Buff Effects

Buff application displays:

- Blue pulse
- Small upward particles

The effect immediately disappears.

---

# Debuff Effects

Debuff application displays:

- Purple pulse
- Small fading particles

Long-lasting particle systems are intentionally avoided.

---

# Healing Effects

Healing displays:

- Green pulse
- Small upward glow

No persistent healing aura exists.

---

# Floating Combat Text

Floating Combat Text is fully configurable.

Options:

- Full
- Critical Only
- Disabled

Configured through:

Settings → Interface → Combat Text

---

# Full Mode

Displays:

- Damage
- Critical Hits
- Healing
- Damage Over Time
- Experience
- Mastery Experience

---

# Critical Only

Displays only critical hits.

Normal damage remains hidden.

---

# Disabled

No floating combat text is rendered.

Gameplay remains fully functional.

Visual effects continue to operate normally.

---

# Floating Text Appearance

Floating text follows consistent animation rules.

Behavior:

- Instant appearance
- Small upward movement
- Quick fade out
- Lifetime around 0.5 seconds

Text never obstructs important gameplay elements.

---

# Floating Text Colors

Floating Combat Text follows the same color palette.

- Damage → Red
- Critical → Yellow
- Heal → Green
- Buff → Blue
- Debuff → Purple

This creates a unified visual language.

---

# Floating Text Budget

Floating texts use a dedicated rendering budget.

When exceeded:

- Low-priority texts are ignored.
- Critical Hits remain visible.
- Existing texts are not interrupted.

---

# Accessibility Integration

The VFX System respects:

- Reduced Motion
- Flash Reduction
- High Contrast Mode
- Color Blind Modes
- UI Scaling

Accessibility options may simplify or reduce effects without affecting gameplay.

---

# Reduced Motion

Reduced Motion decreases:

- Particle count
- Movement distance
- Effect intensity
- Camera interaction

Gameplay information remains unchanged.

---

# Flash Reduction

Flash Reduction decreases:

- White flashes
- Hit flashes
- Critical flashes

Designed for photosensitive players.

---

# Performance

The VFX System is designed for stable performance.

Optimizations include:

- Object Pooling
- Fixed Lifetime
- Active Budget
- Minimal Particle Count
- Lightweight Sprites
- Batched Rendering

Effects are never generated recursively.

---

# Error Handling

If an effect cannot be displayed:

- Gameplay continues.
- Combat calculations remain unaffected.
- Missing effects are logged in developer builds.

Visual effects are never required for gameplay.

---

---

# Summary

The Visual Effects System delivers lightweight, readable, and performance-friendly visual feedback designed specifically for a pixel art idle RPG.

It emphasizes gameplay clarity through short-lived effects, fixed visual categories, strict performance budgeting, configurable floating combat text, and seamless integration with the Accessibility and UI Systems.

Its modular architecture allows future graphical enhancements while preserving the minimalist visual identity established for the game.
