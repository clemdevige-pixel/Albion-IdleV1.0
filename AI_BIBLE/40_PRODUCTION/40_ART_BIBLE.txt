APPROVED

# Purpose

This document defines the complete artistic direction of the project.

Its purpose is to ensure visual consistency throughout production and serve as the single source of truth for artists, animators, developers, and future AI models generating game assets.

This document does **not** define gameplay systems or mechanics. It only establishes the artistic rules that must be respected.

---

# 1. Artistic Philosophy

The project is an **Idle RPG** heavily inspired by the visual identity of Albion Online while building its own original universe.

Players should instantly recognize the visual strengths that made Albion successful:

- Exceptional readability
- Strong silhouettes
- Clear visual progression
- Instantly recognizable equipment

However, **no asset should ever be copied**.

The goal is to create a modern evolution of this artistic direction.

The entire philosophy can be summarized in one principle:

> **Gameplay First, Spectacle Second.**

Every visual element must improve gameplay readability.

The environment should never overshadow the action.

---

# 2. Inspiration Level

The project follows approximately:

**80% Albion-inspired**
**20% Original Identity**

This ratio only applies to the artistic philosophy.

All sprites, textures, animations, and illustrations are entirely original creations.

---

# 3. Art Style

The game uses HD Pixel Art.

Primary objectives:

- Immediate readability
- Strong silhouettes
- Fluid animations
- Production compatible with AI-assisted asset generation

The visual detail level is intentionally higher than Albion.

Approximately:

**20–30% more detail**

while preserving excellent readability.

---

# 4. Visual Gameplay Philosophy

Every visible element must serve a purpose.

Avoid:

- Visual clutter
- Unnecessary effects
- Screen noise

Prioritize:

- Fast readability
- Instant understanding
- Consistency

---

# 5. Characters

Character proportions remain very close to Albion.

Characteristics:

- Small head
- Stocky body
- Broad shoulders
- Equipment defines the silhouette

Players should instantly recognize a character's build and specialization without zooming in.

## 5.1 Canonical Male Hero Identity — Locked

The existing Broadsword hero is the absolute visual reference for the playable male character across every current and future weapon family.

Canonical in-game assets:

- `apps/client/public/assets/characters/hero-broadsword-idle-sheet-v1.png`
- `apps/client/public/assets/characters/hero-broadsword-walk-sheet-v1.png`
- `apps/client/public/assets/characters/hero-broadsword-attack-sheet-v1.png`
- `apps/client/public/assets/characters/hero-broadsword-death-v2.png`

Every equipped variant must preserve the Broadsword hero's:

- facial structure, facial features, eyes, and skin tone;
- hairstyle, hair silhouette, hair volume, and hair colors;
- head-to-body ratio, neck, body shape, and limb proportions;
- recognizable identity throughout every animation frame.

Only the weapon, appropriate armor or clothing, and movement required by the animation may change. Plate, leather, cloth, weapon specialization, animation pose, or equipment tier must never produce a different face, hairstyle, or body morphology.

This rule applies to Broadsword, Spiked Gauntlets, Longbow, Dual Daggers, Infernal Staff, and every future weapon family. The Infernal Staff currently uses the `hero_fire_staff` visual profile.

The Broadsword sprites already integrated into the game take precedence over presentation boards or newly interpreted concept art whenever the male hero's appearance is in question.

Validated production decision: 2026-08-20.

---

# 6. Equipment

Equipment is the player's primary visual identity.

Every equipment upgrade must be immediately noticeable.

All iconic Albion-inspired weapon families remain:

- Swords
- Daggers
- Axes
- Hammers
- Spears
- Bows
- Staves
- etc.

However:

Every asset is completely redesigned.

No equipment should ever be reproduced.

---

# 7. Weapon Philosophy

Every weapon has:

- A unique silhouette
- Unique animations
- An instantly recognizable identity

Iconic weapon archetypes remain familiar while being entirely original.

---

# 8. Armor

Armor follows the same philosophy.

Players should instantly recognize:

- Progression level
- Armor type
- Specialization

using only the sprite.

---

# 9. Animation

Animations are a major improvement over Albion.

Goals:

- 30–50% smoother animations
- More natural transitions
- More satisfying impacts
- Better sense of weight

---

# 10. Visual Effects (VFX)

Visual effects should reinforce gameplay.

Never obscure it.

Four visual intensity levels are defined.

## Level 1

Basic attacks.

Very subtle.

## Level 2

Abilities.

Clearly visible effects.

## Level 3

Elite enemies and critical hits.

More impactful effects.

## Level 4

Boss abilities and major skills.

Spectacular while remaining fully readable.

---

# 11. Colors

The color palette preserves Albion's visual identity.

It is modernized through:

- Higher saturation
- Stronger contrast
- Better biome differentiation

---

# 12. Biomes

The major biome archetypes remain familiar:

- Forest
- Desert
- Swamp
- Mountains
- Snow
- etc.

However,

Every biome is completely reinterpreted.

The project develops its own visual identity.

---

# 13. Monsters

Three categories exist.

## Albion-Inspired Monsters

Approximately 60%.

They preserve their visual DNA while being fully redesigned.

---

## New Monsters Within Existing Factions

Approximately 25%.

These creatures expand familiar factions.

---

## Completely Original Creatures

Approximately 15%.

Designed to establish the project's own identity.

---

# 14. Bosses

Bosses follow the same philosophy.

Approximate distribution:

- 50% Inspired
- 30% New bosses within existing factions
- 20% Completely original bosses

Every boss must be recognizable by silhouette alone.

---

# 15. Boss Scale

Bosses are intentionally larger than those in Albion.

Target size:

Approximately **2 to 3 times** the player's height.

They should feel imposing without reducing combat readability.

---

# 16. NPC Philosophy

NPC roles remain familiar:

- Blacksmith
- Merchant
- Banker
- Enchanter
- etc.

However,

Every NPC is original.

Each one has:

- A unique name
- A unique design
- A unique personality
- A unique backstory

---

# 17. Mounts

Mounts follow Albion's philosophy.

They are entirely redesigned.

Mounts are only visible during exploration.

They automatically disappear during combat to maximize battlefield readability.

---

# 18. Pets

No pet system.

No combat companions.

No cosmetic pets.

The player character always remains the primary focus of the screen.

---

# 19. Weather

No weather system.

Art production resources are focused on:

- Monsters
- Bosses
- Equipment
- Animations
- Visual effects

---

# 20. Character Portraits

No character portraits.

The player's identity is represented exclusively through:

- Equipment
- Animation
- Visual effects

---

# 21. Cosmetics

No cosmetic system in Version 1.

No:

- Skins
- Costumes
- Transmogrification

Cosmetics may be introduced in future expansions once the game's core content is complete.

---

# 22. Rarity Effects

High-rarity equipment features subtle visual enhancements.

Examples:

- Soft glow
- Small particles
- Faint runes
- Light aura

The equipment's design always takes priority over visual effects.

---

# 23. Environment Design

Environments follow a minimalist philosophy.

The scenery exists to support gameplay.

It should never distract players from combat.

Decorative elements remain intentionally restrained.

---

# 24. Readability

Readability is the project's highest artistic priority.

At every moment, players must immediately identify:

- Their character
- Enemies
- Bosses
- Attacks
- Abilities
- Rewards

without effort.

---

# 25. Core Rule

Before creating any new asset, one question must always be asked:

> Does this asset genuinely improve the player experience?

If the answer is no,

the asset should be simplified or removed.

---

# 26. Artistic Vision

The project does not aim to impress through excessive visual effects.

Instead, it seeks excellence through:

- Consistency
- Readability
- High-quality animations
- Rich equipment progression
- Memorable monsters
- Clear gameplay presentation

Every artistic decision must respect this philosophy.
