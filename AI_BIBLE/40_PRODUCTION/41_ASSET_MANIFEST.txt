- Leather

- Chest Armor
- Boots

Each armor piece exists from Tier 2 to Tier 8.

Visual quality, ornaments and materials improve with each tier while keeping the same visual identity.

All armors follow the artistic direction defined in **40_ART_BIBLE.md**.

# ARMOR TIER EVOLUTION
# ============================================================================

The following visual rules apply to every armor piece.

---

## Tier 2

Description:
Basic handcrafted equipment.
Very simple construction.
Almost no decorative elements.

Materials:
Leather
Linen
Raw Iron

---

## Tier 3

Description:
Improved craftsmanship.
Cleaner proportions.
Better stitching and fittings.

Materials:
Iron
Boiled Leather
Better Cloth

---

## Tier 4

Description:
Professional equipment.
Beginning of the recognizable class fantasy.

Materials:
Steel
Dyed Fabric
Refined Leather

---

## Tier 5

Description:
Military grade equipment.
Additional shoulder pieces, belts and metallic reinforcements.

Materials:
Steel
Bronze
Quality Leather

---

## Tier 6

Description:
Elite adventurer equipment.
Visible engravings and premium craftsmanship.

Materials:
Tempered Steel
Silver Details
Fine Cloth

---

## Tier 7

Description:
Master-crafted equipment.
Rich ornaments while preserving gameplay readability.

Materials:
Enchanted Steel
Gold Accents
Rare Fabrics

---

## Tier 8

Description:
Legendary equipment.
Highest craftsmanship available.
Elegant glowing runes and subtle magical effects.
Never excessive.
Gameplay readability always takes priority.

Materials:
Mythic Alloys
Runic Metals
Ancient Fabrics
