12) WEAPON PIPELINE STEP 9 — GENERIC CRAFT FAMILY BRIDGE/UI
Date/session: 2026-08-10
Status: implementation committed; Vercel/manual validation pending.

Objective:
Remove the remaining closed list of weapon families from the React bridge/crafting model so adding a new family such as hammer, dagger or frost_staff does not require editing GameBridge.ts or craftingModels.ts.

Audit findings:
- GameBridge.CraftingRecipeVM.family was a closed union: offhand | bow | sword | fire_staff | armor | gloves.
- craftingModels.ts duplicated weapon family labels/symbols in a closed FAMILY_PRESENTATION record.
- bridgeSync already transports recipe.family generically through CraftingRecipeVM["family"], so widening the bridge type removes the compile-time family lock without changing runtime behavior.
- weapon family gameplay identity already lives in WEAPON_FAMILIES after Step 8.

Files modified:
- apps/client/src/game/GameBridge.ts
- apps/client/src/data/equipmentPresentation.ts
- apps/client/src/ui/production/crafting/craftingModels.ts
- apps/client/src/ui/production/crafting/craftingModels.test.ts
- ALBION_IDLE_DEV_CONTEXT.txt

Changes:
- CraftingRecipeVM.family is now string instead of a closed union.
- craftingModels no longer owns a hardcoded list of weapon families.
- equipmentPresentation now owns explicit craft-family presentation keyed by WeaponFamilyId.
- Record<WeaponFamilyId, ...> forces every declared gameplay family to have presentation content at compile time.
- offhand/armor presentation remains local to the non-weapon crafting UI because those are not weapon families.
- unknown family IDs still render with a neutral fallback label/symbol instead of breaking the UI.

Tests:
- existing armor hierarchy remains unchanged.
- current sword/bow family labels resolve through weapon presentation.
- a synthetic hammer family can pass through CraftingRecipeVM/buildCraftingModel without adding a bridge/UI union member.

No intended gameplay or balance change.
