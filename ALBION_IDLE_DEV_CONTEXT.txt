11) WEAPON PIPELINE STEP 8 — FAMILY AUTHORITY
Date/session: 2026-08-10
Status: implementation committed; Vercel/manual validation pending.

Objective:
Make weapon family identity a first-class catalog entity instead of repeating family mastery/name/craft family in every specialization.

Audit findings:
- Longbow and Badon independently repeated the same bow family metadata.
- every specialization repeated familyMasteryId + familyName even though those are family-level data.
- WeaponCraftRule also repeated a family field that can be resolved from the weapon specialization itself.
- this duplication would scale badly when multiple specializations are added under the same family.
- a separate remaining blocker was identified: CraftingRecipeVM and craftingModels still hardcode the current craft-family union/presentation. That will be handled next; it is more relevant to adding entirely new weapon families than the cosmetic starter-mastery compatibility aliases.

Files modified:
- apps/client/src/data/weaponContentCatalog.ts
- apps/client/src/data/refiningRecipes.ts
- apps/client/src/data/weaponContentCatalog.test.ts
- ALBION_IDLE_DEV_CONTEXT.txt

Changes:
- added WEAPON_FAMILIES as the single definition of each current weapon family.
- family definition owns family mastery ID + display name once.
- specializations now reference familyId only.
- WeaponCraftRule no longer repeats a family field.
- added resolveWeaponFamilyId(itemId).
- added getWeaponFamilyDisplayName(familyId).
- resolveWeaponMastery now derives family mastery through WEAPON_FAMILIES.
- family mastery definitions/names are generated once from WEAPON_FAMILIES; specialization mastery definitions remain generated from specialization content.
- standard crafting recipe generation resolves its family from resolveWeaponFamilyId instead of reading duplicated craft metadata.
- no stats, recipe quantities, abilities, mastery IDs, attack speeds or item IDs intentionally changed.

Tests:
- every current weapon resolves the expected family ID in addition to tier/profile/mastery/ability.
- current family definitions and family display names are locked.
- unknown item IDs do not infer a family from naming conventions.

Audit decision on GameContext starter aliases:
- they are not a parallel mastery source anymore; they derive from weaponContentCatalog.
- removal remains desirable cleanup, but it is not a blocker for adding weapons.
- priority moved to eliminating the actual new-family blocker in CraftingRecipeVM/craftingModels before doing cosmetic bootstrap cleanup.
