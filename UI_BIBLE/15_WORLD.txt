# UI_BIBLE/15_WORLD.md

# Albion Idle — World Panel

Version: 1.0
Status: VALIDATED

---

# Purpose

The World Panel provides access to zone progression.

It allows the player to understand:

- Available zones
- Current progression
- Zone difficulty
- Tier
- Segment progression
- Boss progression
- Access restrictions

The World Panel uses a vertical zone structure optimized for the Right Panel.

---

# Core Principle

The world is represented as a progression through zones.

```text
Blue
  ↓
Yellow
  ↓
Orange
  ↓
Red
  ↓
Black
```

Each zone contains its own progression.

The interface must communicate both:

- Global world progression
- Progression inside each zone

---

Exact segment counts and progression values are defined by gameplay data.

---

# Zone List

Zones are displayed vertically.

Each Zone Card displays relevant information such as:

- Zone name
- Zone color
- Tier
- Progress
- Current segment
- Lock state
- Current activity

The zone color must reinforce identification without becoming the only way to distinguish zones.

---

# Zone Order

Primary progression follows:

```text
Blue
Yellow
Orange
Red
Black
```

The UI must reflect the actual progression rules defined by gameplay.

---

# Current Zone

The active zone must be immediately identifiable.

Example:

```text
BLUE ZONE

CURRENT
```

The active state should remain visually clear without excessive animation.

---

# Zone Selection

Selecting a Zone Card opens its details inside the same Right Panel.

The Game World remains visible.

Example:

```text
WORLD
   ↓
BLUE ZONE
   ↓
Zone Details
```

---

# Zone Details

A selected zone may display:

- Tier
- Difficulty
- Current segment
- Total segments
- Progress
- Boss
- Access requirements
- Equipment restrictions
- Relevant rewards

Only information supported by gameplay data should be displayed.

---

# Segment Progression

Each zone contains progression through segments.

Example:

```text
BLUE ZONE

SEGMENT 18 / 20

█████████████████░░

18
 ↓
19
 ↓
20
 ↓
BOSS
```

The player must immediately understand how far they are from the next meaningful milestone.

---

# Segment Timeline

When useful, the shared Segment Timeline may represent zone progression.

Example:

```text
●──●──●──●──●──○──○──★
                  BOSS
```

The timeline must remain compact enough for the Right Panel.

Detailed combat progression remains visible in the Game World HUD.

---

# Boss

Boss encounters are displayed as meaningful zone milestones.

Example:

```text
ZONE BOSS

Avalonian Bedivere

Segment 20

LOCKED
Reach Segment 20
```

When available:

```text
ZONE BOSS

READY
```

Boss information should be visually distinct from normal segment progression.

---

# Locked Zones

Locked zones remain visible.

The player should understand what lies ahead.

Example:

```text
ORANGE ZONE
T6

LOCKED

Requirement:
Complete Yellow Zone
```

The UI must always explain why access is unavailable.

---

# Zone Restrictions

Zones may impose gameplay restrictions.

When applicable, these restrictions must be displayed before entering the zone.

Examples:

- Equipment Tier cap
- Item Power rules
- Access requirements
- Other zone-specific constraints

Example:

```text
BLUE ZONE

EQUIPMENT CAP
T4

Equipment above the permitted
tier does not provide an advantage.
```

The exact restriction behavior is defined by gameplay systems.

---

# Difficulty

Zone difficulty must be understandable immediately.

Color provides the primary visual identity:

```text
Blue
Yellow
Orange
Red
Black
```

Text labels and icons must support color so that meaning does not depend exclusively on color perception.

---

# Zone Rewards

Zone details may provide a concise overview of relevant rewards.

Examples:

- Resource tiers
- Equipment tiers
- Enemy drops
- Boss rewards
- Special materials

The World Panel should not reproduce complete loot tables.

Detailed loot information belongs in contextual Tooltips or dedicated systems when necessary.

---

# Enter Zone

When zone selection is available, the primary action is clear.

Example:

```text
[ ENTER ZONE ]
```

Before entering, the player must understand any important restriction or consequence.

---

# Active Zone

The currently active zone cannot create ambiguity with the selected zone.

Example:

```text
CURRENT ZONE
Blue

VIEWING
Orange
```

Viewing another zone does not automatically move the Hero there.

---

# Progress Preservation

Previously completed progression remains visible.

Example:

```text
BLUE ZONE

20 / 20
COMPLETE
```

Completed zones remain accessible according to gameplay rules.

---

# Dashboard Integration

The Dashboard may display:

- Current zone
- Current segment
- Progress toward next milestone
- Upcoming boss

Detailed World information remains inside the World Panel.

---

# Header Integration

The Header displays compact current-location information.

The World Panel provides the detailed progression view.

Information should not be unnecessarily duplicated.

---

# Notifications

Relevant World notifications may include:

- New zone unlocked
- Boss available
- Major progression milestone reached

Routine segment progression should not generate excessive notifications.

---

# Scrolling

The World Panel uses vertical scrolling.

Horizontal scrolling is prohibited.

The zone list must remain easy to scan.

---

# Visual Hierarchy

```text
Zone List
   ↓
Current Zone
   ↓
Zone Progress
   ↓
Segments
   ↓
Boss
   ↓
Restrictions / Rewards
```

---

# Scalability

The World Panel must support future zones and world systems without requiring a new global interface.

Additional zones may be added to the vertical structure.

More complex future systems may use contextual sub-sections while remaining inside the Right Panel.

---

# Rules

MUST

- Represent zones vertically.
- Show current zone progression.
- Show segment progression.
- Clearly identify locked zones.
- Explain access requirements.
- Display relevant zone restrictions.
- Keep the Game World visible.
- Use gameplay data as the source of truth.

MUST NOT

- Require a full-screen world map.
- Hide locked zones without reason.
- Depend exclusively on color.
- Automatically change zones when merely inspecting one.
- Duplicate complete loot tables.
- Invent progression or restriction rules in the UI.

SHOULD

- Make global progression understandable immediately.
- Highlight upcoming bosses.
- Show meaningful future progression.
- Keep completed zones readable.
- Remain scalable as new zones are introduced.
